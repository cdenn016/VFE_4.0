# Per-agent grouping with conditional physical compression

It also constructs a conditional compressive parent preserving the original-time port field, every admitted finite joint response-record history, and the actual nonflat collective geometry on a declared family. It does not establish endogenous selection of the sufficient interface. The discarded physical phases are invisible to the fixed interface before grouping.

## Mandatory transfer

The sender law identity \(pK[p]=pW\) is exact, including its derivative, but the resulting per-agent force differs from the reciprocal force. This result supplies no compression by itself.

## What remains unproved

The fixed kernels and protocol make \(\theta_i\) invisible before any grouping event. The dynamics changes which agents group; it does not create this sufficient interface. Consequently the combined conditional theorem cannot be promoted to endogenous compressive formation under the frozen contract. A next construction must obtain a genuinely collective or interaction-selected noninjective interface while retaining the same demanding dynamics, joint-history, record and geometry clauses.

An additional join of already canonical port products is associative but discards no new phase. It therefore does not establish a second compressive handoff or the required counterfactual dependence. Neither this failure nor the phase-probe obstruction is a nonexistence proof for the general existential target.
