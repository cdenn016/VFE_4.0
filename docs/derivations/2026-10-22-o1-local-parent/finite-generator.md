# Conditional finite preparation generator

## 1. Additional condition and why it matters

A bounded jet at one point cannot encode the arbitrary C2 sections admitted in the published packet. There is, however, an exact global contextual realization on any specified finite preparation-generator family. This is a sufficient condition, not a claim that the published entire C2 class already has such a generator. Its actual laws, dynamics and boundary protocol remain the original ones. The reconstruction family restricts initialization only; it does not project the evolved field back onto a finite basis.

Assume the prepared initial section is

\[
x_0(c)=f(c;p),\qquad p\in P\subset\mathbb R^m, \tag{F1}
\]

where f is a known C2 map into the positive O1 natural-parameter chart, with sufficient smoothness in p, and P carries a physical common-rotation action with f(c,g p)=rho(g)f(c,p). Work on its cone domain with S0(p)=sum_i B_i(f(c0;p)) nonzero. Require that the canonical generator coefficients p_star retain all of p except this physical rotation. Redundant parameterizations may instead be quotiented by their exact evaluation equivalence. The constitutive data and fixed protocol are known. The full ordered history has unlimited finite length.

One concrete nonempty choice inside the published window sets A_1(c,0)=a e_chi for c=(a,chi), A_2(c,0)=d e1, with fixed positive marked amplitudes; l_i(0)=epsilon v_i for v near (4,1/2,-1), C_i(0)=0, n_i(0) fixed small, and B_i(c,0)=delta b_i with three noncollinear cone vectors b_i independent of c. These are finite preparation coefficients, with the rotational union included. Fixed or finitely generated C and other coordinates can be added within the original uniform smallness margins. This family is relatively open in its own parameter manifold, not C2-open among all functions c -> x(c). The published grouping and finite nonflat window apply to it. Its B sector still transmits private orientation and has the same relative probes; no primitive hidden factor is introduced.

## 2. Exact chronological evaluation, without ansatz projection

For each retained word h, form its chronological composition E_h(c) of the *original* native flows and evidence-coefficient updates. Each diagnostic contributes its normalized sampling kernel and an identity law-state update. Each evidence transaction appends the original likelihood; it does not reset recognition laws. Its future field is (L1) with the accumulated coefficients. Let

\[
x_t^h(c;p)=E_{t,h}(c) f(c;p). \tag{F2}
\]

Here E is notation for the unique solution map on a finite branch, not a proposed closed-form approximation. The sufficient state is the canonical preparation seed p_star, h, action and census labels and times, and the original current time t. Cached local law values and two-jets may be retained for evolution; they are constrained by (F2), not new free parameters. Between events dot p_star=0, dot h=0, dot t=1, and dot x=V(x,c,h). These equations preserve (F2) by uniqueness. At an event h appends its actual outcome and future likelihood coefficients change accordingly. There is no fitted force on parent laws and no rescaling of time.

Canonicalize the preparation seed once by U(S0(p)). The global original angle is not stored. Equivariance means that evaluating (F2) from p_star gives rho(U(S0(p)))x_t^h(c;p). Alternatively, compute the current reference from the same finite seed at c0 and reanchor by its current U. This latter evaluation is exactly the published representative. Both sample rotations are constant in the evaluation coordinate c for fixed h,t, and both preserve the actual Fisher costs. The first representation needs no live reference integration for a cached local future; the second matches the published moving-canonicalization coordinate convention.

Evaluation at a previously uninitialized c is possible by replaying the known generator and complete native history there. This can cost time proportional to the history and native ODE solution work. Finite representation size does not imply constant-time evaluation, a fixed-length record, or an elementary solution formula. The theorem is about exact mathematical laws, not finite-precision replay guarantees.

## 3. Normalized operational and comparison laws

The next response kernel at c is exactly the original kernel evaluated at (F2), with the same physical/lab outcomes and the same shared-vertex relative sampling. The parent joint response/next-state update uses that same sampled outcome. A causal product of these normalized kernels defines Pi_c(dh). If an action includes external randomization, use its original conditional policy kernel. Its dependence on h is not replaced by independent marginal actions. Thus every admitted finite adaptive joint history is reproduced, even though the lengths and record strengths are unbounded over the protocol class.

The actual parent comparison law is

\[
\bar P_{c,t}(dh,dy)=\Pi_c(dh)\bar Q_{c,t}^{h,p_\star}(dy), \tag{F3}
\]

where bar Q is the normalized product of current recognition, presentation and original Gibbs-label laws constructed from (F2). This is not a delta law on a copied tensor. For each fixed h,t, its map from the fine sample is one Haar rotation of fresh psi samples, independent of c and commuting with the marked action that fixes stored lab labels. Therefore (L6) holds for (F3), including history scores and the conditioning normalizer. Its actual minimizing connection and positive-rank nonflat tensor agree with the published ones on the same common window.

The laws depend on c through f and the native flow evaluation. Their contextual two-jets satisfy the closed equations (L4). Since the actual laws themselves have that dependence, the jet is a way to compute their Fisher geometry. It does not create an artificial derivative on a context-independent canonical density. Curvature is that of the same marked connection, not of the separate physical reference phase.

## 4. Compression and scope

If p' = g p for one common physical angle, all canonical coefficients and history laws agree. An absolute child psi diagnostic distinguishes the fine values because each nonzero B has nonzero first moment, while it was excluded from the boundary protocol before and after handoff. The reference seed contains only canonical b_i and no inverse rotation, so it cannot recover g. Crossing relative probes still prevent doing the same erasure independently on a proper block. The prescribed null, fixed census and persistent evidence are inherited rather than redesigned.

This supplies a complete conditional realization on the stated finite-generator class, with actual normalized laws and explicit closure, memory and initialization conditions. It is a finite preparation-and-history representation, not a universal finite moment closure of all C2 sections. Without a finite generator, one must keep a compatible contextual field, permit a nonfinite seed, or restrict which contexts a single initialized local state promises to answer. The negative theorems apply only under their declared regular finite-encoding or strictly differential-local hypotheses; they do not contradict this construction.
