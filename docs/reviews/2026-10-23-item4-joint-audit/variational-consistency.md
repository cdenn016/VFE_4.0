## Algebra checked by direct reconstruction

For one row, set beta_j^* = pi_j exp(-D_j/T)/Z. Substitution of its log into T KL(beta || beta*) gives the original expected-cost-plus-entropy row objective plus T log Z. Differentiation of the finite partition sum gives d(-T log Z) = sum_j beta_j^* dD_j. Thus F:269-274 uses the whole optimized row energy, and no derivative of an optimized row is missing. This is the finite entropy/log-partition conjugacy of Wainwright and Jordan (2008), Section 3.6.1, Theorem 3.4, equations (3.44)-(3.46); I checked the primary text at [the published paper, pages 67-68](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~blei/fogm/2018F/materials/WainwrightJordan2008.pdf). The manuscript's exact row-gap identity at M/chapters/03_dynamics.tex:76-96 is the same specialization. No numerical residual is claimed here.

For the continuum metric block, its coordinate matrix is [[h + C^T k C, C^T k], [k C, k]]. Pairing the velocity with an arbitrary fiber variation gives k(r_t + C u_t) = -V_r. Substitution into the shape pairing gives h u_t = -V_u + C^T V_r + lambda h tau_h. These are C:209-218. Consequently, the potential may depend on the fiber coordinate without being projected to a false quotient-only force. Pairing these two equations and B b_t = -V_b with the respective velocities gives the stated sum of negative kinetic norms, including the V_r contribution. This check uses the displayed block equations themselves and does not assess the separate PDE existence proof.

For finite contexts, differentiation of sum_v m_v E_v + lambda C_* and application of the inverse weighted metric gives the local force with coefficient one and the contextual force with coefficient lambda/m_v. The same m_v cancels from the row force. This accounts for both the coefficient in F:215 and the outer mass factors in F:241-245; replacing the contextual force by lambda B_v at nonunit masses would be a different model.

## Integration scope

The contextual energy makes the auxiliary connection enter the law velocity. This addresses the open dynamical role stated at M/chapters/02_geometry.tex:264-265. Existing statements at M/chapters/02_geometry.tex:7 and M/future_TODO.md:9 that all flows are pointwise and every connection is only diagnostic need to be qualified to the older constructions once the new chapter or section is inserted. That is a source update required by integration, not a defect of the notes.

The construction establishes coupling and shape regularization. It does not supply separate diffusion for source rows, independently evolving links, or arbitrary frame coordinates. This is stated in F:394-402 and C:184, 234, 267, 314, 469-479, and 599-603. M/future_TODO.md:76 currently asks for smoothness of beliefs, models, rows, links, and frames to be produced dynamically. Integrating these notes must preserve the remaining part of that item rather than mark that entire sentence complete. Pure common frame variation is removed by auxiliary minimization, so a claim of independent frame smoothing would contradict the selected energy.

The finite note uses a supplied contextual graph, a regular finite-distance minimizing branch, and fixed comparisons. The continuum note uses a supplied flat torus, fixed comparisons in its chosen trivialization, a free proper action, one adapted chart, and a finite-dimensional law family. Its continuum connection is the exact minimizer of a tangent quadratic. The finite near-identity expansion recovers that normal equation (F:283-316; C:125-181), but neither note proves a graph-refinement limit. Item 6 at M/future_TODO.md:82-84 therefore remains open. Displacement transport derived from peer links is also absent; the contextual transports are separate from the directed communication channels.

The broader channel dependence K[x,p] should be introduced explicitly when importing the statements, or specialized to the manuscript's K^q[q], K^s[s] notation. A channel rule depending on rows would require additional row derivatives and is excluded here. Likewise, changing comparisons to state-dependent maps would change the pullback metric and first variation; neither note licenses that substitution.
