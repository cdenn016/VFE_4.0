### X1-GAUGE-01: The bundle, its connection, and the per-agent gauge group do no dynamical work in chapters 3 to 12
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** high
**Location:** `chapters/01_foundations.tex:8-17,31,35-42` (def:agent-section, eq:reference-laws); `chapters/02_interactions.tex:5,15-24,62-89` (def:int-generator, prop:int-endpoint, eq:int-reference-equivariance); `chapters/03_dynamics.tex:35-49` (def:dyn-potential); `chapters/02_geometry.tex:149-198` (geo:collective); `chapters/06_fusion.tex:8,127-159` (fus:links, fus:distance); `chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:421-513`; `chapters/12_open_formation.tex:347-410`; `frontmatter.tex:3`; `AUTHORING.md:7`.
**Evidence:** Three separate facts. (i) Per-agent covariance is automatic. In def:int-generator the generator is a function of the reference laws (U_i^a)^{-1}_# nu_i^a, which are unchanged by the passive change by construction (ch2:24). Any measurable function of reference laws therefore gives a link obeying eq:int-endpoint: the frames U_i act as a dressing field (Fournel, Francois, Lazzarini and Masson, Int. J. Geom. Methods Mod. Phys. 11 (2014) 1450016, Main Lemma), and by criterion C1 of Francois (Philos. Sci. 86 (2019) 472-496) a symmetry that admits a local dressing field is artificial. The frames are needed to form the reference laws, so by ch1:17 ("access mechanisms read by its update rule") they are agent state. The reference link e^{L_ij} is itself passively invariant. `check_ch2_links.py` shows that the trace of a single open reference edge is unchanged by a common reference change (2.886883 before and after), while the displayed open edge trace moves from 0.628 to -3.832 under a passive g. Every reference-coordinate entry is thus an invariant, not only loop traces; the prior review (L2-4) found the same unitary-gauge structure in the earlier theory. The only substantive constraint is eq:int-reference-equivariance, a global condition with one h_a per sector at each context. prop:int-endpoint states it with "if", and no later chapter imposes it. Without it a link depends on the choice of reference point in the fiber P_c, so it is not a function on the bundle, and for a nontrivial P no global choice exists across contexts. (ii) The connection never enters an equation of motion. def:dyn-potential lives at one context and contains no context derivative, connection, or curvature. Chapters 4 to 9 never mention a context (grep). Chapter 10 says its coordinates are sample coordinates and does not apply ch:geometry (ch10:182). In chapters 11 and 12 every context evolves by the same context-local flow, and A^F is computed from the evolved section afterwards by geo:collective. No result in chapters 3 to 12 feeds A, F, or h back into a vector field, a row, a channel, or a record. (iii) The exponential-link machinery of chapter 2 (moment family, low-rank exponentials, non-inverse loops) enters a dynamical theorem only in chapter 6. There L_ij = chi(mu_i - mu_j) is a coboundary, Theta_ij = V_i V_j^{-1} with V_i = U_i e^{chi mu_i}, and the normalized cost fus:distance equals the transport-free cost |mu_i - mu_j| exactly (`check_fusion_and_determinant.py`: both residuals about 1e-16). The chapter 6 introduction nevertheless lists "gauge transport" among its specified ingredients. As written, 2.0 is a family over C of decoupled finite-agent systems with a diagnostic connection attached afterwards, and its gauge covariance is covariance in Kretschmann's sense. This leaves no coupling on which to build the PIFB2 goals that need a dynamical or observable connection: latent links, base geometry, and the determinant/Lorentz sector.
**Fix:** State in chapters 1 and 2 that per-agent covariance is dressing and imposes no constraint. Promote eq:int-reference-equivariance to a standing requirement, since it is the actual P-gauge covariance. Then either describe the connection as a diagnostic of supplied sections or add a context coupling through which it acts (X1-GAUGE-10).
**Falsifies:** A dynamical equation, row, channel, or record kernel in chapters 3 to 12 that depends on omega, A^F, or its curvature; or a constitutive map in the general definitions whose covariance under per-agent changes is a restriction rather than an identity.

### X1-GAUGE-02: The "nongauge" and "physical" orientation loss of chapter 12 is relative to an angular frame held only by an external querier
**Category:** inflation
**Severity:** high
**Location:** `chapters/12_open_formation.tex:14-20` (open:channel), `:263`, `:336-345` (open:response), `:415` (open:formation, "loses a continuous nongauge public-law distinction"); `frontmatter.tex:7` ("continuous physical orientation loss"); `chapters/06_outlook.tex:4`; `companion/c12_open_formation.tex:103-107,143`; `chapters/01_foundations.tex:101`.
**Evidence:** (a) Every constitutive datum (uniform references and evaluators, the Haar-angle channel, uniform priors) is invariant under independent rotations of each agent's belief angle and model angle, a group (S^1)^{2N}. `check_open_formation_gauge.py` (a) evolves a correlated three-agent model on an exact 24-point angle grid under independent per-agent and per-sector rotations and finds equivariance residuals 4.4e-16 and 2.2e-16. (b) The Haar-angle channel is the S^1 twirl q -> integral of tau_a q da/2pi (residual 6.7e-16). This is the classical form of the map that describes communication between parties sharing no reference frame for the group (Bartlett, Rudolph and Spekkens, Rev. Mod. Phys. 79 (2007) 555, Sec. II; locator from memory). An identity channel between agents whose angular frames are independent and uniformly unknown produces exactly open:channel, so the population dynamics cannot be told apart from a model in which each agent's angle is pure frame data with no connection. (c) Nothing in the population reads an angle. The conditional angular law of every agent evolves autonomously (X1-GAUGE-08), and the bit dynamics sees the angular sector only through rotation-invariant conditional entropies. (d) The orbits are distinguished only by open:response, which no agent performs ("Queries are observational"). Its singleton queries return Theta_i and its block queries return their sum, both read in a fixed common angular frame. Check (f) confirms that the block response law is H_I invariant (3.5e-18) and changes by 4.0e-3 under a single-agent rotation. So the response alone makes H_I, rather than all of (S^1)^I, the discarded group. (e) Chapters 1 and 2 define only passive changes; the active transformation invoked at ch12:263 is never defined, so "nongauge" has no definition to refer back to. In the sense of Greaves and Wallace (Brit. J. Philos. Sci. 65 (2014) 59-89), a subsystem symmetry has direct empirical significance only relative to an environment that holds the relevant reference. Here that environment is a querier outside the population, contrary to the stance of ch1:101 ("introduces no observing agent outside the population").
**Fix:** Define active transformations in chapter 1 or 2, as automorphisms of the reference laws at fixed frames, and define the observable algebra against which "gauge" is judged. Then either restate the theorem as losing "a distinction invisible to every agent and channel of the population and resolved only by the declared response protocol, which reads angles in a fixed external frame", or model that frame as a reference agent inside the population with an angle-reading record channel and recheck exact descent. The second route exposes a tension: any such channel breaks the H_I symmetry on which exact descent rests.
**Falsifies:** A channel, row, evaluator, or record kernel of an agent in the population whose law depends on an absolute angle, or on a relative angle between two agents; or a definition elsewhere in the manuscript of an internal angular reference used by open:response.

### X1-GAUGE-04: The nonzero curvature and rank two of chapters 11 and 12 come from tying belief and model angles to one structure group, which no constitutive datum requires
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** `chapters/12_open_formation.tex:349,371-388` (open:curvature, open:rank); `chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:423-445,471-513` (form:geometry, form:connection, form:curvature); `frontmatter.tex:7`; `README.md:3`; contrast `chapters/02_interactions.tex:15-24` and `chapters/05_inheritance.tex:434` ("Independent sector frames").
**Evidence:** Chapter 12 declares "the common collective group to be S^1, acting on every belief and model angle simultaneously", and chapter 11 uses one SO(2) acting on both X and Y. In both, the evaluator is constant (ch12: G_i = 1; ch11:104, "adds no cross-sector coupling"), so the constitutive data are invariant under independent belief and model rotations (verified for chapter 12 in `check_open_formation_gauge.py` (a)). With the independent group S^1_q x S^1_s (respectively SO(2)_q x SO(2)_s), the collective cost separates into a(dchi + xi_q)^2 + b xi_s^2, minimized by A_q = -dchi and A_s = 0. That connection is kappa independent, so F = 0, and h_chichi = 0, so the tensor has rank one. `check_collective_geometry.py` reproduces the tied formulas: A_chi = -0.72026 and F = -0.2976394 by finite differences against -0.2976394 from open:curvature, and the Wishart rotational inertia 11.108571 against the closed form of form:inertias. **Fix:** State in the abstract and in both theorems that the curvature belongs to the tied belief/model connection under a declared common representation. To make the tie structural, use an evaluator that couples the two angles, for example G_i((w,psi), d(z,theta)) proportional to exp(lambda cos(theta - psi)), which is invariant only under the diagonal rotation, and rederive the formation windows with it.
**Falsifies:** A constitutive datum in chapter 11 or 12 that is not invariant under independent belief and model rotations, or a statement there that the structure group is G_q x G_s with the curvature computed for it.

### X1-GAUGE-06: A relabeling-equivariant global canonical representative cannot exist, a non-equivariant global one does, and the parent sees holonomy only modulo Z_|I|

**Severity:** medium
**Location:** `chapters/12_open_formation.tex:265-285` (open:anchor, open:parent-law), `:295-303` (open:phase-recursion), `:357`; `AUTHORING.md:3`; `README.md:3`.
**Evidence:** The manuscript says "a single global root representative ... [is] not asserted". This can be settled. Proposition: let n = |I| >= 2, and let D be a set of block tuples with u_i(c_*) != 0 that is invariant under (S^1)^I and under permutations and contains a diagonal tuple (q,...,q). Then no continuous R: D -> D exists with R(x) in H_I x, R constant on H_I-orbits, and R(pi x) = pi R(x). Proof: the loop y(t) = (tau_t q, q, ..., q), t in [0, 2pi], has in its orbit d(t) = (tau_{t/n} q, ..., tau_{t/n} q); rotate agent 1 by t/n - t and the others by t/n, which has zero sum. Then R(y(t)) = R(d(t)) is permutation fixed, hence equal to (tau_{t/n + 2 pi k/n} q, ...) for an integer k, because q has trivial stabilizer when u != 0. Continuity makes k constant. Since y(2pi) = y(0) while tau_{2pi/n} q != q, this is a contradiction. The obstruction is the monodromy of the n-fold cover z -> z^n. `check_open_formation_gauge.py` (g) shows the equal-phase representative shifting by exactly 2pi/3 around this loop. Dropping relabeling equivariance removes it. Rotating agents j >= 2 by -theta_j and agent 1 by the sum of the theta_j over j >= 2 gives a global, continuous, H_I-canonical representative on all of D that closes around the same loop (check (g)). There is also a holonomy consequence. The common S^1 meets H_I in Z_n (ch12:357), so on parent orbits the common gauge group acts through S^1/Z_n. The parent connection is the image of the fine one, and its holonomy is the fine holonomy modulo Z_n. Only Wilson loops whose charge is divisible by n descend. If chi in open:curvature ranges over a full circle, the parent loses the charge-one holonomy exp(-2 pi i a/(a+b)).
**Fix:** Add the proposition and the global non-equivariant section. Replace "not asserted" by "impossible with relabeling equivariance and available without it". Put the S^1/Z_n holonomy statement next to "equality of finite stabilizer groups is not asserted".

### X1-GAUGE-08: The open-law transfer can be made exact, because conditional angular laws evolve autonomously and the bits see only an exogenous forcing

### X1-GAUGE-09: Import the loop sector of the earlier theory with a law-dressed fiber metric

A background Frobenius defect moves from 1.88 to 31.84 under a non-orthogonal h. Conjugating the shear toward I leaves Q(W_t, M_t) at 1.589806, whereas Q with M held fixed falls to 1.6e-4.
**Fix:** Sketch: add to def:dyn-potential a term summing c_ij Q_ij(reference laws) over pairs, using the inverse Fisher matrix of a declared location family for laws without second moments. Its derivative enters through eq:int-total-variation and lem:sgeom-frechet. Recommendation on c08b: import the class-function blindness result, the 2-cycle default loop family, and the loop defect with the law-dressed metric. Do not import the normalized generator prior and evidence decomposition unless 2.0 adopts a generative population model, because its potential is a direct law potential (ch3:49). To keep the latent character, make the generators omega_ij extra state variables with the direct potential (1/2)||omega_ij - L_ij(reference laws)||^2 + sum over loops of c_p Q_p(omega; M). The held-link limit then carries the restriction in L2-1 of the prior review (twists in exp(gl) only). Difficulty: low for the definition and invariance, moderate for the dynamics and the folds of the exponential chart.
**Falsifies:** A passive or reference change under which Q_ij changes, or a non-identity W with Q_ij = 0. Neither can occur, because Q is half the squared Frobenius norm of a similarity transform of I - W.

### X1-GAUGE-10: Make the bundle act: geo:collective is the auxiliary-field equation of a gauged sigma model
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** high
**Location:** `chapters/02_geometry.tex:149-212` (geo:collective, geo:minimal-pullback, geo:aggregate); `appendices/base_connection.tex:71-72` (open:conn-derived); `chapters/01_foundations.tex:31`; `Research/manuscripts/2026-10-21-observer-free-gauge-agent-foundation.md:280-373`.
**Evidence:** For a collective section p: C -> N and a Riemannian metric gamma on C, put E_ctx[p, A] = (1/2) integral over C of gamma^{mu nu} G(D_mu^A p, D_nu^A p) dvol. By geo:passive it is gauge invariant. Its integrand is a sum of the per-direction costs of geo:collective, so minimizing over A at fixed p gives A = A^F pointwise and leaves (1/2) times the integral of tr_gamma h^min. geo:collective is therefore the elimination of an auxiliary gauge field. Since r_X = P_H dp(X), h^min is the pullback, by the projected section C -> N/G, of the orbit-space metric of the Riemannian submersion N -> N/G when the action is free. Up to the sign convention fixed by D^A p = dp + J_p A, F^F is the pullback by p of the curvature of the mechanical connection. This is the structure of the CP^{N-1} model, whose U(1) field has no kinetic term and equals the matter current (D'Adda, Luscher and Di Vecchia, Nucl. Phys. ## Extensions and improvements

The highest-value step is to give the connection a role. Adding lambda E_ctx from X1-GAUGE-10 to the law potential turns 2.0 from a family of context-local systems into a gauged sigma model on the statistical manifold, with the collective connection as the auxiliary field that geo:collective already computes. Two exact statements are available at once: h^min is the pullback of the orbit-space Fisher metric by the projected section, and F^F is the pullback of the mechanical curvature. Combined with geo:retained-connection, they give a clean inheritance criterion: a parent retains the geometry exactly when the retention commutes with the projection to N/G on the contextual jets. The hard part is analytic, the existence and long-time behavior of the harmonic-map heat flow into an open, positively curved target. A finite-dimensional first version on a two-agent von Mises or Wishart family, with the context a 2-torus, is a realistic next theorem. On a closed base the integrated curvature of the mechanical connection gives a topological charge, an invariant that no context-local construction can see.

The loop sector should be reintroduced in the law-dressed form of X1-GAUGE-09. Because the receiver's covariance, or Fisher matrix, transforms with the frame, the defect Q_ij is invariant under the full GL reference group, which the earlier theory could not obtain without a background metric. Combined with the quiver-invariant theorem, this describes what can be observed about directed links: oriented-cycle traces, which separate closed orbits, plus law-dressed defects, which detect unipotent loops. A concrete theorem to aim for: for the moment family of def:int-moment-family, Q_ij = 0 on every 2-cycle if and only if every pair of directed links is reciprocal. This would reproduce at the law level the earlier theory's statement that reciprocal links are the hard-constraint limit of 2-cycles.

Chapter 12 can be made both stronger and more explicit about its gauge content. The exact reduction of X1-GAUGE-08 removes the perturbative open-law step. The quotient-gradient statement of X1-GAUGE-07 replaces the negative remark about the slice metric with a positive identity. The global non-equivariant section of X1-GAUGE-06 removes the local-chart restriction from every invariant claim and isolates the one place where locality is forced, namely relabeling equivariance. The holonomy statement in that finding, that the parent connection is known only modulo Z_|I|, is the natural holonomy-sensitive observable for the construction: a fine-level charge-one Wilson loop around a closed contextual direction descends only to its |I|-th power. To make the orientation loss operational rather than relative to an external querier, add a reference agent R with its own angular law and a record channel that reads the relative angle theta_i - theta_R, as in the reference-frame resource theory of Bartlett, Rudolph and Spekkens. The outcome is informative either way. Exact descent through H_I then fails, because the channel breaks the symmetry, and the size of that failure measures how much operationally accessible orientation information the compression destroys.

The tie between sectors should be derived rather than declared. An evaluator coupling the belief and model angles through theta - psi leaves only the diagonal per-agent rotation as a symmetry, which makes the common S^1 the structure group forced by the constitutive data. The curvature of chapters 11 and 12 then follows from the model's coupling rather than from the choice of group. The same coupling would give a first example in which the q and s sectors carry different representations of one group, for instance charges 1 and 2, so that the representation, and not only the group, changes the curvature. Finally, the foundations would benefit from stating the dressing structure explicitly in chapter 2: frames as a site field, reference generators as unitary-gauge variables, and the common reference change as the P-gauge. That framing explains why every result is automatically covariant, and it identifies the one requirement, eq:int-reference-equivariance, whose failure would make a construction depend on a non-covariant choice.
