# G1: Quantitative proof-check of the open:routing analytic chain behind the chapter-12 headline theorem

I read in full `chapters/12_open_formation.tex` lines 1 to 471 and reconstructed by hand every analytic step of lines 125 to 232 (prop open:routing with open:limit, open:core, open:early-bounds, open:small-gap, the unlabeled late comparison at 199 to 207, the third-pair comparison at 209 to 214, open:distant-bound and the moving-coordinate estimate at 216 to 231), using lines 86 to 123 (open:selector, open:contrast, open:binary-field, open:interior) as context. I read `lenses/L09-OPEN.md` in full (including L09-OPEN-04, L09-OPEN-15 and its checked-table rows for open:distant-bound and open:routing) and the X5-IMPL-07 block of `lenses/X5-IMPL.md` (lines 55 to 61). I did not rerun the event sequence; the four scripts evaluate only the inequalities the proof needs. Verdict: I found no error in the analytic chain, and all five requested items hold with explicit quantities. (a) The late comparison holds for every initial half-gap $b\le\varepsilon_b=1$ at $a=4$ (interval-verified invariant region), so the manuscript's bound $b(t_A)\le2(A/4)^{-1/4}=0.354$ at $A=4096$ meets it with room; (b) open:distant-bound holds with absolute constants (8 for the field difference, 40 for its Jacobian when $D\ge3$) that depend on neither $A$ nor $R$; (c) the Lipschitz constant is $R$-independent, indeed absolute ($\le64.5$), and $D_R$ holds on the radius-one tube; (d) the quantifier order is sound because $\varepsilon_b$ does not depend on $A$; (e) Gronwall plus a tube Lipschitz constant preserves strict windows and transverse crossings, provided the crossing observables are $C^1$-close, which the proof uses without stating. The one quantitative weakness is that the written $h$-step, which compares fields on a fixed-radius tube in $u$, needs $h\lesssim R^{-3/2}$ because a far agent's common-mode field error is $\tfrac23h^2u^3$; at $A=4096$ this forces $T<7\times10^{-22}$ before any Gronwall amplification.

## Findings

### G1-01: The written $h$-step needs $h$ of order $R^{-3/2}$, so the proof's own temperature is below about $10^{-21}$ at $A=4096$
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:137-139 (paragraph after open:limit, "On a compact trajectory tube, a uniform field error $e_h$ ..."), 231 ("take one sufficiently small $h>0$ with $h|u_i|<1$ on the compact tube"); prop open:routing
**Evidence:** The scaled exact field differs from open:limit by a term that grows like $|u|^3$. Symbolically (s3), $h^{-1}(1-h^2u^2)\operatorname{atanh}(hu)=u-\tfrac23h^2u^3-\tfrac2{15}h^4u^5+\dots$, so an isolated far agent has field error $+\tfrac23h^2u^3$ (the exact field decays more slowly). At $u=R=1.7546\times10^7$ and $h=10^{-9}$ the 50-digit value is $3.6014\times10^3$ against the formula's $3.6012\times10^3$ (s2). The uniform field error on the tube is therefore at least $\tfrac23h^2(R+2)^3$. The leading-order center equation $\dot u=-u+\tfrac23h^2u^3$ has the closed form $u(t)^{-2}=(R^{-2}-\tfrac23h^2)e^{2t}+\tfrac23h^2$. Its deviation from $Re^{-t}$ is $\tfrac13h^2R^3e^{-t}(1-e^{-2t})$ to leading order, with maximum $0.1283\,h^2R^3$ at $t=\tfrac12\log3$. At $h=10^{-9}$ (the value used in L09-OPEN's s3 run) the deviation is 693 units of $u$ at $t=0.55$, so that trajectory is far outside the radius-one tube of the written argument. Even staying inside a radius-$\tfrac12$ tube needs $h<2.69\times10^{-11}$, i.e. $T<7.2\times10^{-22}$ (s2, s4). This is before the Gronwall factor $(e^{CL}-1)/C$ that the text applies on top of it, and the admissibility condition $h|u|<1$ quoted in L09-OPEN-04 gives only $h<5.7\times10^{-8}$. The routing itself is unaffected: the far pair lags outward, rows depend only on separations, and inside a far cluster at position $U$ the Gibbs exponent changes only by the relative amount $h^2U^2$ (s3: the $U^2$ coefficient of the $h^2$ term is $(p-q)^2/2$). Existence is untouched, since $h$ is only required to be "sufficiently small". The error lies in the stated estimate route, and in the reading of the numerics: L09-OPEN's $h=10^{-9}$ run supports the mechanism but sits outside the proof's tube.
**Fix:** Run the $h$-comparison in coordinates relative to each cluster's exact center, or in a weighted tube $|\delta_i|\le\rho(1+|u_i|)$. The common-mode error then drops out and $hR\ll1$ suffices. Combined with G1-02 ($A=34$, $R\approx1.7\times10^3$ to $1.8\times10^3$), even the unweighted common-mode tube needs only $h<2.8\times10^{-5}$ (s4). State the resulting $T$ in the proposition, or state explicitly that it is not quantified (L09-OPEN-04).
**Falsifies:** An estimate showing that the exact scaled trajectory of the far pair stays within a fixed-radius $u$-tube of its limit trajectory for $h\gg R^{-3/2}$. The closed form above says it does not, to leading order in $hR$.

### G1-02: The late comparison closes with $\varepsilon_b=1$, and the whole chain already works from $A=34$
**Category:** improvement
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:179-207 (open:small-gap, the unlabeled late comparison, "persist for sufficiently small initial $b$", "Choose one finite $A$")
**Evidence:** Task (a). The text compares the core $(a,b)$ system of open:core, started at $(4,b(t_A))$, with the invariant trajectory $b=0$. I reconstructed the comparison: $B=v/(1+v)$ with $v=e^{-2a^2}$, $p=(1-B)/2$, $\dot a=-a(1+4B)$ and $\dot B=4a^2v(1+4B)/(1+v)^2$. It gives $a_c=\sqrt{\log9/2}=1.048147$, $\dot B(a_c)=0.553701$, $a_*=\sqrt{\log2}=0.832555$ (where $B=1/5$), $S=1.426696\le\log(4/a_*)=1.569551$, and the time from $a=4$ to $a_c$ is $1.281672$ (s1 B). Note $\dot B=8.1\times10^{-13}$ at $a=4$, so positivity of $\dot B$ carries no uniform margin. Only transversality near the crossing is available, which is why the text's perturbative phrasing ("compact margins away from a small crossing interval") is the right shape. Instead of a Gronwall perturbation from $b=0$, the step closes directly by a phase-plane certificate. Let $\Omega=\{a_*\le a\le4,\ 0\le b\le a/4\}$, write $f_a,f_b$ for the right sides of open:core and $k=-f_b/b$. The interval check (s1 D, outward-padded floating-point intervals, $(G_+-G_-)/b$ enclosed in mean-value form, $1600\times40$ boxes) gives:
(I) on $b=a/4$, $k-|f_a|/a\ge0.0838>0$, so $\frac{d}{dt}(b-a/4)<0$ there; with $b=0$ invariant and $f_a\le-a$, the trajectory stays in $\Omega$ until $a=a_*$, which takes at most $\log(4/a_*)$;
(III) on every box meeting $\{B=1/10\}$, $\dot B\ge0.3935$, so the parent threshold is crossed exactly once and transversally. These boxes have $a\ge1.0444$, so the crossing happens within $\log(4/1.0444)=1.3429<S$ after $t_A$, and $L=t_A+S$ leaves a positive window after the crossing;
(IV) $B(a_*,b)\ge0.1999$ for $b\le a_*/4$, and $B(4,b)\le6.7\times10^{-9}$;
(V) on $\Omega$ every first-pair weight $p_\pm=w/Z_\pm$ is at least $0.1177$ and every first-pair contrast $(w-v_0-v_\pm)/Z_\pm$ is at least $0.0789$, margins $0.0177$ and $0.0289$ above $1/10$ and $1/20$, which absorb the third-pair exterior ($<10^{-3}$).
Hence $\varepsilon_b=1$: any $b(t_A)\le1$ keeps all margins and the transverse crossing. A second certificate, $b\le\tfrac12(a/4)^{1/2}$, also passes with larger margins ($p\ge0.342$, contrast $\ge0.064$). The manuscript's bound $2(A/4)^{-1/4}=0.3536$ at $A=4096$ meets $\varepsilon_b=1$. That bound only needs $A\ge4(2/\varepsilon_b)^4=64$. Moreover, open:core gives sharper early constants than the text uses: $-\dot a/a\le1+4e^{-32}+4e^{-8}=1.001342$ and $\dot b\le-(1-68e^{-8})b=-0.977189\,b$ (from $|g_c(a+b)-g_c(a-b)|\le34be^{-8}$ and $c_p\ge0$). With these, $\tau\le\log4/0.977=1.4187$, $a(\tau)\ge0.2416A$, and $b(t_A)\le2(A/4)^{-0.9759}$. The early argument needs $a\ge8$ on $[0,\tau]$ (for $|\dot v_0+\dot v_\pm|<1/100$) and $b(t_A)\le1$, so it holds for $A\ge33.12$ (s4). At $A=34$: $t_A\in[2.137,2.140]$, $b(t_A)\le0.248$, $L\le3.567$.
**Fix:** Replace "Compact margins away from a small crossing interval, and a positive derivative inside it, persist for sufficiently small initial $b$" by the invariant-region lemma above. Its four inequalities are finite and checkable, and the record should cite them with their margins. Replace "Take $A\ge4096$" by $A\ge34$ with the sharpened early constants, or keep 4096 and say the late step needs only $A\ge64$.
**Falsifies:** A point of $\Omega$ where (I), (III), (IV) or (V) fails. The interval enclosure finds none at 4-ulp padding per operation (a certified-rounding library was not used). A residual in the core reduction would also falsify it; s1 A finds $1.55\times10^{-15}$ over 400 random $(a,b)$ against the four-agent limit field.

### G1-03: Transversality under the $h$- and $R$-perturbations needs $C^1$ closeness of the crossing observables, which the proof does not state
**Category:** gap
**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:139 ("Thus strict windows and transverse crossings persist"), 222-226 (open:distant-bound, "field difference and its first derivatives"), 231 ("The exact scaled binary field inherits the strict windows")
**Evidence:** Task (e). The persistence step is the following lemma, which I reconstructed. Let $X$ solve $\dot X=\Phi(X)$ on $[0,L]$, and let $K_\rho$ be the tube of closed sup-norm balls of radius $\rho$ about $X(t)$, so each slice is convex. On $K_\rho$ assume: $|\Psi-\Phi|\le e$; $\Phi$ is $C$-Lipschitz on slices; $|\Phi|,|\Psi|\le M$; $O\in C^1$ with $|\nabla O|_1\le G$ and $\nabla O$ $L_1$-Lipschitz; and $|\tilde O-O|\le\eta_0$, $|\nabla\tilde O-\nabla O|_1\le\eta_1$. If $\dot Y=\Psi(Y)$, $|Y(0)-X(0)|\le\delta_0$ and $\Delta=\delta_0e^{CL}+e(e^{CL}-1)/C<\rho$, then $Y$ never leaves $K_\rho$. Moreover $|\tilde O(Y)-O(X)|\le\eta_0+G\Delta$. Writing $\frac{d}{dt}\tilde O(Y)=\nabla\tilde O(Y)\cdot\Psi(Y)$ and splitting gives $|\frac{d}{dt}\tilde O(Y)-\frac{d}{dt}O(X)|\le\eta_1M+L_1\Delta M+G(e+C\Delta)$. Suppose the reference has $|O(X)-\theta|\ge m$ off a crossing interval $I$, opposite signs at the ends of $I$, and $\frac{d}{dt}O(X)\ge s$ on $I$. If both error bounds are below $m$ and $s$ respectively, then $\tilde O(Y)-\theta$ has exactly one zero, it is transverse and lies in $I$, and all strict windows persist. So the trajectory step needs only $C^0$ field closeness, but the observables must be $C^1$-close as functions, and they do change with each perturbation. The block rows of the exact $h$-system are built from $\beta_{ij}=\exp[-d(hu_i,hu_j)/h^2]/\sum$, not from $w_{ij}$. The six-agent rows have changed normalizers. Neither closeness is stated. Both hold: (i) $h^{-2}d(hu,hv)$ is even and analytic in $h$ on $h\max(|u|,|v|)<1$, with expansion $(u-v)^2/2+h^2(u-v)^2(u^2+2uv+3v^2)/12+O(h^4)$ (s3), so rows and field are analytic in $(h^2,u)$ and converge in every $C^k$ on compact subsets of the tube; (ii) for the core-parent row, $|B^{(6)}_{P_2P_1}-B^{(4)}_{P_2P_1}|\le2e^{-D^2/2}$ and $|\nabla(B^{(6)}-B^{(4)})|_1\le(56e^{-1/2}+8D+16De^{-D^2/2})e^{-D^2/2}$ (derived from $w_{ij}-\tilde w_{ij}=-\tilde w_{ij}\varepsilon_i/Z_i$; s2 observed ratios to these bounds at most 0.038 and 0.014). The "first derivatives of the field difference" in open:distant-bound are not what transversality needs; the row derivatives are.
**Fix:** State the lemma once (it also serves prop open:open-laws), and add the two closeness facts (i) and (ii) with their constants.
**Falsifies:** A transverse reference crossing that becomes non-transverse under a perturbation with $C^0$-small field error and $C^1$-small observable error. The lemma excludes this.

### G1-04: The moving-coordinate sentence attributes $R$-independence to the wrong estimate
**Category:** exposition
**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:216-229 ("Subtract $Re^{-t}$ ...", "Along moving trajectories the extra derivative is also controlled, since $Re^{-t}|r|\le(|r|+A+6)|r|$")
**Evidence:** Task (c). Write $Y=X-Re^{-t}e_P$ ($e_P$ the indicator of the third pair). The full system is $\dot Y=G(Y,t)=F(Y+Re^{-t}e_P)+Re^{-t}e_P$, so $D_YG=DF$, and the Lipschitz constant equals that of open:limit. Translation invariance of the interaction alone makes it $R$-independent; in fact it is absolute. With $M_i=\sum_jw_{ij}x_{ij}$, one has $\partial_mM_i=w_{im}[1-x_{im}(x_{im}-M_i)]$ for $m\ne i$, $\partial_iM_i=-\sum_{m\ne i}\partial_mM_i$, and $\partial_m(w_{ji}x_{ji})=w_{ji}(1-x_{ji}^2)(\delta_{im}-\delta_{jm})+w_{ji}x_{ji}\sum_kw_{jk}x_{jk}(\delta_{km}-\delta_{jm})$. Together with $w\le n$, $n|x|\le e^{-1/2}$ and $nx^2\le2/e$, these give $\|DF\|_\infty\le1+2(1+10/e+25/e)+2(5+10/e+25/e)=64.5$ for every six-agent configuration (s2: sampled maximum 4.32 over 3000 configurations). The inequality $Re^{-t}\rho\le(\rho+A+6)\rho$ is needed only for the explicit time derivative $\partial_tG=-Re^{-t}(DF\,e_P+e_P)$. By translation invariance this contains only cross-cluster columns (s2: at most 0.07 of $40(1+D^2)e^{-D^2/2}$). The same holds for $\partial_t$ of the rows in moving coordinates. That term enters the crossing derivatives if one computes them in the moving frame, not the Lipschitz constant. The inequality is correct when $r$ is the core-to-pair separation $\rho$. On the radius-one sup-norm tube, $|u_i|\le A+3$ for core agents (the extrema comparison keeps the reference core in $[-(A+2),A+2]$) and $|r_j|\le3$ for the pair's moving coordinates. Hence $Re^{-t}=\rho-r_j+u_i\le\rho+A+6$, and $\rho\ge Re^{-L}-A-6=D_R$ (s2: no violations in 20000 random admissible points). Read with $r$ as the moving coordinate, the displayed inequality would say $Re^{-t}\le A+9$, which is false; the symbol is overloaded in that paragraph.
**Fix:** Name the moving coordinates (for example $y_j=u_j-Re^{-t}$). Say that the comparison system's Lipschitz constant is that of open:limit, which is bounded by an absolute constant. Say that the separation inequality controls $\partial_t$ of the rows and field in the moving frame. Alternatively, do the $R$-step in the original coordinates, where the system is autonomous and no time derivative arises.
**Falsifies:** A configuration in the tube where $D_YG$ depends on $R$. None exists, since $D_YG=DF(X)$ and $DF$ is bounded by 64.5 globally.

### G1-05: Explicit absolute constants for open:distant-bound and the Gronwall step
**Category:** improvement
**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:222-227 (open:distant-bound, "The constant depends on the fixed within-core bound, not on $R$"), 139
**Evidence:** Task (b). Let $D$ be the minimum core-to-pair separation, $\varphi(D)=e^{-D^2/2}$, and for agent $i$ in cluster $K$ (complement $K'$) let $\varepsilon_i=\sum_{k\in K'}n_{ik}\le|K'|\varphi(D)$. Then
$\Delta F_i=\sum_{j\in K}[(w_{ij}-\tilde w_{ij})+(w_{ji}-\tilde w_{ji})]x_{ij}+\sum_{j\in K'}(w_{ij}+w_{ji})x_{ij}$,
with $w_{ij}-\tilde w_{ij}=-\tilde w_{ij}\varepsilon_i/Z_i$. These are the changed row normalizers in both the outgoing and the incoming direction, plus the incoming cross forces. Using $\tilde w|x|\le e^{-1/2}$ and $n|x|\le D\varphi(D)$ for $|x|\ge D\ge1$:
$|\Delta F|_\infty\le8(e^{-1/2}+D)\varphi(D)\le8(1+D^2)\varphi(D)$.
(Core rows give $(12e^{-1/2}+4D)\varphi$, pair rows $(8e^{-1/2}+8D)\varphi$.) Differentiating with the formulas of G1-04, together with $|M_i|\le5e^{-1/2}$, the Jacobian row sums are at most $(120.3+38.8D+8D^2)\varphi(D)$ for core rows and $(79.1+58.2D+16D^2)\varphi(D)$ for pair rows. Hence $\|D\Delta F\|_\infty\le40(1+D^2)\varphi(D)$ for $D\ge3$ (and $\le84(1+D^2)\varphi(D)$ for $D\ge1$). I checked this at 50 digits with central differences over 120 configurations with $A\in\{8,100,4100\}$ and $D\in[3,10.5]$, both generic cores and reflection cores. The largest observed ratios to the two bounds are 0.335 ($C^0$) and 0.395 ($C^1$) (s2). This confirms the text's claim and strengthens it: $C_A$ can be taken to be 40, independent of both $A$ and $R$. $A$ enters only through $Re^{-t}\le\rho+A+6$, in the moving-frame time derivative (G1-04), and through $D_R$. With the global Lipschitz constant 64.5 and $L=8.358$, the $R$-step needs $8(1+D^2)\varphi(D)(e^{CL}-1)/C$ below the margins. That holds at $D\approx33$, giving $R=(D+A+6)e^L\approx1.76\times10^7$, within 0.5 percent of the value L09-OPEN-04 found with $D=12$, because $A e^{L}$ dominates (s2, s4).
**Fix:** Replace "The constant depends on the fixed within-core bound" by the explicit bounds above and drop the moving-coordinate device for the $R$-step. Gronwall with the global constant in original coordinates suffices.
**Falsifies:** A configuration with $D\ge3$ violating either bound. The sampled worst ratios are at most 0.4.

## Extensions and improvements

The most useful next step is a fully explicit witness built from pieces that are now nearly all in place. The early phase holds analytically for $A\ge34$ with the sharpened constants of G1-02, and the late phase is the invariant-region certificate with $\varepsilon_b=1$. The third pair is a one-dimensional monotone comparison, and the $R$-step uses the absolute constants of G1-05. What remains is the $h$-step and the Gronwall amplification. For the $h$-step, compare in coordinates relative to each cluster's exact center. The common-mode term $\tfrac23h^2u^3$ then disappears, and within a cluster at position $U$ the exponent and force corrections are of relative size $h^2U^2$ (s3), so $hR\ll1$ suffices. For the amplification, replace $e^{CL}$ with $C=64.5$ by the logarithmic norm $\mu_\infty(DF)=\max_i(\partial_iF_i+\sum_{m\ne i}\lvert\partial_mF_i\rvert)$ enclosed along the reference tube. The reference is a union of contracting clusters, so this integral is plausibly of order one. At $A=34$, $L\le3.57$ and $R\approx1.8\times10^3$, which would give a stated temperature of order $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-10}$ in place of the present unquantified value. This is the analytic half of L09-OPEN-15. The interval-enclosure technique of s1 extends directly, since every remaining inequality involves at most two variables in the decoupled comparison. Difficulty: moderate.

The certificate of G1-02 also suggests a cleaner statement of the late comparison that may serve the finite-depth extension proposed in L09-OPEN-14. That extension needs, at each level, a region in the reduced (center separation, internal gap) coordinates that is forward invariant and on which the inherited parent row crosses $1/10$ transversally. For two clusters of equal size $m$ with internal gaps $b$, the reduced vector field differs from open:core only through the internal-gap dependence of the normalizers. The same four inequalities, (I) the ratio test $k>|f_a|/a$, (III) transversality on the threshold curve, (IV) the exit margin, and (V) the contrast margins, can be enclosed for each $m$ at the cost of one more interval variable. Whether a single region works for all $m$ is open. The $m$-independence of the collapsed-cluster dynamics in L09-OPEN-14 says it holds at $b=0$, so the question is the size of the uniform neighborhood. Difficulty: moderate.

A small structural improvement to the proof is to state the persistence lemma of G1-03 once, before prop open:routing, and use it three times: for the $R$-step, the $h$-step, and the initial-data and sector perturbation (and again in prop open:open-laws). Each use then reduces to listing a field error, a Lipschitz constant, and the two observable-closeness numbers $\eta_0,\eta_1$, which is what a reader needs to check the windows.
