Verdict: every load-bearing identity in the chapter that I recomputed is correct (normalization and common-precision addition, the complete moment-channel derivative and the Fisher flow, global common-precision evolution, original-time parent equations, the event times, the loss witnesses, the response kernel, and the connection, tensor and curvature formulas, all checked symbolically or numerically with the residuals below), and I found no false statement. The weaknesses are structural: the membership schedule is a closed-form function of the prepared initial belief shapes that is independent of the interaction strength (the entire chapter holds verbatim at kappa = 0, where the agents do not interact), the threshold only sets the clock, and the mean-field architecture forces this for every pairwise law comparison; the selection is state-read in form but determined by the prepared initial data. In addition, the constant evaluator is a load-bearing assumption that is not flagged, the lost coordinate is dynamically inert, and the inherited contextual geometry decays exponentially.

## Findings

### L08-CND-01: The selection is interaction-blind and is fixed by the prepared initial shapes
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** high
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:277-348 (form:selector, form:selection), 192-202 (form:coupling remark), 4-20 (chapter introduction); chapters/05_inheritance.tex:776-785 (inh:recursive-formation); chapters/06_outlook.tex:8; README.md:24 ("interacting four-agent Wishart construction")
**Evidence:** By form:ode, alpha_i' = -2(alpha_i - alpha_0) contains neither kappa, nor P, Q, nor any other agent, nor the model sector. Hence b_i(t) = e^{-2t} b_i(0), and because a join replaces scores by their count-weighted average (a linear operation commuting with the common rescaling), every current block score is b_I(t) = e^{-2t} b_I(0), where b_I(0) is the count-weighted initial centroid. Every distance is D e^{-2t} with D an initial-data centroid distance, so every event time is (1/2) log(D/delta). I reconstructed a one-dimensional no-cascade lemma: if I and J join at distance D, then for every other block K the new distance exceeds D (if K lies beyond J, d(K, I u J) = d(K, J) + n_I D/(n_I + n_J) > D; K cannot lie between the centroids of I and J, since then it would have crossed first). Consequently the event-driven rule is exactly count-weighted centroid (UPGMC) agglomerative clustering of the initial belief shapes, with merge heights increasing and join k at time (1/2) log(D_k/delta). Script `s3_selection.py` (f) compares the event-driven rule with `scipy.cluster.hierarchy.linkage(method="centroid")` on 500 random one-dimensional configurations with N = 3 to 8: 0 mismatches in times and 0 inversions. Three consequences follow. First, the tree does not depend on delta; delta only shifts all event times by -(1/2) log delta. Second, nothing in the interaction enters: in `s3_selection.py` (c) the full coupled ODE (alpha_i and P, RK4 with crossing detection) for kappa in {0, 1, 5} gives events at 0.346574, 0.549306, 1.070033 with the trees (0,1), (2,3), (0,1,2,3) in all three runs, while P_11 at t = 1.5 is 1.035, 1.471 and 2.460. Third, at kappa = 0 the channel target equals the reference, C_q = I_2, there is no interaction, and form:full-flow, form:parent, form:selection, form:responses and form:geometry all still hold (the curvature is nonzero because the derivative of the solution map with respect to its initial value is e^{-2t} > 0; `s4_geometry.py` (c) gives F = -1.716 at kappa = 0). Only form:coupling uses kappa > 0. The cause is structural: every receiver has the same reference, evaluator and channel target, and the channels depend on the laws only through the additive moment M, so the density force is h_i = 2 log q_i + V_t with V_t common to all agents, and therefore d/dt log(q_i/q_j) = -2 log(q_i/q_j) + c_ij(t). For common-precision Wisharts, every f-divergence between two agents (indeed every statistic invariant under the common congruence X -> P^{1/2} X P^{1/2}) depends only on (alpha_i, alpha_j). Thus no pairwise law-comparing selector in this family can see the interaction; the choice of b_I is not the cause. The global dynamics also converges to the identical-agent law W(alpha_0, P*) x W(eta_0, Q*) (for the example constants p* = sqrt(2) exactly), so the hierarchy is the order in which pairs become delta-close during a uniform contraction to consensus; the first parents coexist only on [max(t_L, t_R), t_2), of length (1/2) log(17/6) = 0.52 in the example. Measured against inh:recursive-formation ("select a block ... under those inherited interactions") and against PIFB2 24_section.tex ("Separate the asymmetry in the initial preparation from the learning that amplifies it"), the amplification here is identically zero: all pairwise distances shrink by the same factor, and the hierarchy is the ordering of the prepared asymmetry.
**Fix:** State as a proposition in the chapter that the membership schedule is the centroid dendrogram of the initial belief shapes, with join times (1/2) log(D_k/delta), independent of kappa, of all precisions and model data, and (for the tree) of delta. Say explicitly that the construction meets inh:recursive-formation with a selector that is blind to the interaction, and either add an interaction-sensitivity clause to inh:recursive-formation (see L08-CND-10) or describe chapter 11 as conditional inheritance along a prepared contraction ordering. Revise "interacting" in README.md:24 and "Dynamics selects when those descriptions are used" in 06_outlook.tex:8 so that they do not suggest that the interaction influences membership.
**Falsifies:** An admitted preparation in the class of form:gaps and two values of kappa (or of any precision or model datum) with the same initial shapes that produce different membership events or times. form:ode excludes this.

### L08-CND-02: The constant evaluator is load-bearing but is omitted from every list of delimiting assumptions
**Category:** gap
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:76-78 (definition of G_i), 12-17 (declared architecture), 103-104, 234-235 (parent evaluator); companion/c11_formation.tex:185; AUTHORING.md:33; README.md:24
**Evidence:** The chapter chooses G_i(m, dX) = W_2(alpha_0, I_2)(dX), constant in the model sample, and notes only that it "adds no cross-sector coupling". It is used in two load-bearing places. (a) The parent evaluator must be a kernel from the retained model sum m_I to X_I. The sum-pushforward of the product over i in I of G_i(m_i, .) depends on the individual m_i unless G is constant in m; for a precision-valued dependence Gamma(m), the sum of the W(alpha_0, Gamma(m_i)) is not even Wishart unless the Gamma(m_i) agree (`s1_wishart_basics.py` (c): with unequal precisions the diagonal implied shapes are 2.034 and 2.589, which a Wishart law would make equal). (b) With a nonconstant G, the term integral KL(q_i || G_i(m, .)) s_i(dm) gives h_i an X-coefficient E_{s_i} Gamma(m), which differs across agents because the s_i differ; the precisions then cease to be common and form:precision-difference, form:addition and the whole inheritance argument fail. The declared architecture in the introduction (product recognition, moment channels, constant shapes, fresh sums, threshold), the companion's closing list, README.md:24 and AUTHORING.md:33 do not mention the constant evaluator, although conditional dependence of beliefs on the model is the coupling mechanism of def:dyn-potential (eq:dyn-local-joint).
**Fix:** Add "constant (model-independent) evaluator, so that the belief and model sectors are uncoupled" to the declared architecture in the introduction, the companion's closing paragraph, README and AUTHORING, and note at line 234 that descent of the parent evaluator uses this constancy.
**Falsifies:** A nonconstant normalized evaluator for which the parent evaluator descends through the sum map and common precision is preserved by the flow.

### L08-CND-03: The information-loss witness lies in a coordinate that is dynamically inert even in the fine description
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:338-355 (proof of form:selection, loss paragraphs); companion/c11_formation.tex:104 (section title "a real loss of information"), 128
**Evidence:** The loss witness perturbs (eta_i, eta_j) -> (eta_i + epsilon, eta_j - epsilon). In the fine dynamics eta_i' = -2(eta_i - eta_0), Q depends only on the total B, the belief sector never reads s (constant evaluator, L08-CND-02), and the selector reads only alpha. So the individual eta_i influence nothing except the law s_i itself: the fine trajectory of every other variable factors through B_I. The belief-sector allocation, which is what the selector reads, is not lost given the event record: at a join the child scores are b_I +/- delta/2, so (A_I, n_I, t_join, delta, alpha_0) determine the children up to permutation (the chapter notes this at line 350). The witness therefore establishes non-injectivity of the retention map, which is correct, but it does not exhibit loss of anything that any agent, channel, or the selector ever responded to. The companion's "real loss of information" overstates this.
**Fix:** State that the discarded coordinate is a dynamical spectator in both descriptions. A stronger witness would discard a coordinate that influenced the fine trajectory of some other variable before the join; whether exact closure permits that is the question in L08-CND-10. Replace "a real loss of information" with "continuous loss of an uncoupled model-shape allocation".
**Falsifies:** An admitted fine variable other than s_i whose trajectory depends on the allocation of eta within a block at fixed B_I.

### L08-CND-04: The inherited contextual geometry is an exponentially decaying transient, and its infinite-time limit is degenerate
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:441-514 (form:geometry, form:connection, form:curvature); companion/c11_formation.tex:143-183; abstract ("rank-two contextual geometry with nonzero curvature")
**Evidence:** The statement "rank two and nonzero curvature at every finite time" is correct (`s4_geometry.py` (a), (b)). The tensor itself decays. For block {1,2} at a = 0.3 with the example constants, g_aa = 4.40, 2.69e-3, 5.20e-6, 1.65e-11 and h_chichi = 0.756, 3.57e-4, 6.65e-7, 2.09e-12 at t = 0, 1, 2, 4; the decay rate is 2|f_p(p*)| = 12 - 4 sqrt(2) = 6.34, with p* = sqrt(2) the common fixed point and f_p(sqrt 2) = -6 + 2 sqrt(2). By t = 1, before the root join at t_2 = 1.070, g_aa is about 6.1e-4 of its initial value. All contexts converge to the same isotropic precision, so as t -> infinity the stabilizer becomes all of SO(2) and the minimizing connection becomes undetermined; at finite times A^F = -k_1/(k_1 + k_2) is a ratio of inertias that both tend to zero. Its limit depends on a coincidence of rates: for alpha_0 = eta_0 the curvature tends to about -1.81, whereas for alpha_0 = 1, eta_0 = 2 it decays (F = -1.58, -1.51, -0.83, -0.147, -0.022, -0.0064 at t = 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, with A_chi -> -1, a pure-gauge value). The curvature is also not produced by the coupled flow: at kappa = 0 it is -1.716. It is the imprint of the prepared anisotropic section, which the dynamics erases at rate 6.34.
**Fix:** State the decay rate and the infinite-time degeneration next to form:geometry, and avoid presenting the curvature as a property generated by the interacting dynamics. If persistent geometry is wanted, the references must keep the contextual anisotropy alive; L08-CND-12 gives a verified modification.
**Falsifies:** A positive lower bound on h^min uniform in t for the chapter's section; the computed decay excludes it.

### L08-CND-05: The adaptive response theorem is close to definitional
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:368-419 (form:responses, form:batch); companion/c11_formation.tex:130-141
**Evidence:** The fine interface is defined (lines 370-381) as readouts measurable with respect to the current-partition block sums, policies may read only retained state and carried records, and records never feed back into laws ("Recording an output does not reset a belief"). The informative open statement is the version with record feedback (Bayesian or KL updates driven by records), which the chapter excludes.
**Falsifies:** An admitted protocol under which the conclusion of form:responses requires more than additivity and determinism.

### L08-CND-10: A mean-field no-go for interaction-sensitive selection, and a sharper formation obligation
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** high
**Location:** chapters/05_inheritance.tex:776-800 (inh:recursive-formation); chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:94-107, 128-190
**Evidence:** The proposition sketched in L08-CND-01 generalizes. Hypotheses: all receivers share reference and evaluator; every channel is independent of source, receiver and input; and the channels depend on the laws through additive moments M = sum_i E_{q_i} T. Then on any regular dominated family the unit-Fisher flow of the Gibbs-reduced potential satisfies d/dt log(q_i/q_j) = -2 log(q_i/q_j) + c_ij(t). Proof: the two KL terms each contribute log q_i to h_i, and the derivative through the channel is the same function T for every i. For an exponential family this means that natural-parameter differences decay as e^{-2t}; form:precision-difference and the shape equations are special cases. Any relabeling-equivariant selector that reads only pairwise relative data is therefore a function of the initial data and a common clock. Interaction-sensitive selection requires receiver-dependent targets, i.e. nonconstant Gibbs rows, which is the route of chapter 12. Within additive exact-closure architectures there is a candidate obstruction, which I have not proved: if the block-summed force must depend only on block sums on an open fiber of allocations, then for forces of the form theta_i' = -2 theta_i + sum_j beta_ij(theta) phi(theta_j) the rows beta_ij must be constant along fibers, which removes selectivity inside any block that could form.
**Fix:** Add an interaction-sensitivity clause to inh:recursive-formation, for example: there exist an admitted preparation and an admissible one-parameter deformation of the constitutive interaction, with references, evaluator, threshold and initial data fixed, that changes the selected partition sequence. Chapter 11 fails this clause (L08-CND-01). Then either prove the obstruction above for exponential-family agents with exact sum closure (difficulty: moderate; the computation reduces to fiber-invariance of sum_{i in I} F_i) or show that chapter 12's section-valued parents evade it.
**Falsifies:** A construction with receiver-independent targets and additive moment channels whose membership schedule depends on the interaction strength; the identity above excludes this for pairwise relative selectors.

### L08-CND-11: Approximate inheritance without exactly common precision
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:153-161 (form:precision-difference), 357-366 (openness only within the common-precision family)
**Evidence:** The common-precision family has codimension 3(N - 1) per sector, and an exact sum law fails off it. Off the family, however, the defect is small and decays. Write P_i^{-1} = Pbar^{-1} + epsilon F_i, with the moment-matched Pbar^{-1} = sum_i alpha_i P_i^{-1}/A, so that sum_i alpha_i F_i = 0. The first-order term of the log-Laplace transform -sum_i alpha_i log det(I + P_i^{-1} T) cancels, so the law of the sum deviates from W(A, Pbar) at order epsilon^2 (`s5_fiber_and_approx.py` (iii): log-Laplace defect 4.20e-4, 1.05e-4, 2.64e-5, 6.61e-6 at epsilon = 0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.025, with successive ratios 3.985, 3.992, 3.996). The KL divergence to the Wishart family is then of order epsilon^4. By the mean-field identity, |P_i - P_j|/|alpha_i - alpha_j| is time invariant (`s5_fiber_and_approx.py` (ii): 2.39791576 at t = 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2), so at a join time the precision mismatch equals delta rho with rho = |dP(0)|/|d alpha(0)|. The moment-matched precision obeys the parent equation up to O(epsilon^2) in the vector field, because sum_i alpha_i P_i^{-1} B P_i^{-1} = A Pbar^{-1} B Pbar^{-1} + sum_i alpha_i E_i B E_i with E_i = P_i^{-1} - Pbar^{-1}.
**Fix:** State and prove a quantitative theorem: on an open set of 4-agent Wishart preparations with unequal precisions, the parent defined by moment matching inherits the law, the dynamics and the extensive responses up to a KL defect of order (delta rho)^4 at each join, decaying as e^{-8(t - t_join)} afterward. Difficulty: moderate. The second-order Laplace expansion is elementary; a KL bound for the convolution needs a uniform second-order density expansion or a Fisher-information argument, plus a Gronwall estimate for the parameter mismatch on the remaining horizon. This answers the PIFB2 request to "quantify its error on the formation and handoff horizons" (24_section.tex, predictive parent state) and removes the exact common-precision preparation up to a controlled error.
**Falsifies:** A first-order (order epsilon) deviation of the sum law from W(A, Pbar) under moment matching; the cancellation excludes it.

The same derivation gives C = Lambda + (kappa/N)(M - N alpha_0 Lambda^{-1}) and P' = -2P + Gamma + C (natural-gradient check: residual 3.1e-18, `s6_persistent_geometry.py`). The references are then context-dependent sections that transform with the common action; this is compatible with the gauge-covariant setup but changes line 427 ("identity-precision references are invariant").
**Fix:** Offer this as the geometric variant of form:context and form:geometry, with the limit tensor computed from the fixed-point eigenvalues p*_+/-(a). Difficulty: low; the proofs of form:full-flow and form:parent carry over with I_2 replaced by Gamma.

## Extensions and improvements

The most valuable next step is to make formation depend on the interaction. Within the chapter's architecture this is impossible for pairwise relative selectors: when every receiver sees the same targets and the channels read only additive moments, the identity d/dt log(q_i/q_j) = -2 log(q_i/q_j) + c_ij(t) holds on any regular dominated family. Relative organization is then a rescaled copy of the preparation, and any relabeling-equivariant selector reading relative data is a clock-reparametrized function of initial data. A sharpened obligation would add to inh:recursive-formation the requirement that an admissible deformation of the constitutive interaction, with initial data, references, evaluator and threshold fixed, change the selected partition sequence for some admitted preparation. The concrete mathematical task is then a pair of results. The first is a no-go: for exponential-family agents with additive sufficient statistics and exact sum closure on open allocation fibers, receiver-selective Gibbs rows are excluded inside any block that can form, because closure forces the block sum of beta_ij(theta) phi(theta_j) to be constant along fibers. The second is a positive construction that evades the no-go by relaxing exact closure to a controlled defect, which links this item to the next.

The second step is quantitative closure off the common-precision family. The first-order cancellation in the log-Laplace transform under moment matching, verified at a rate of 3.99 per halving of epsilon, shows that sums of Wisharts with nearly equal precisions are Wishart to second order in the precision spread, hence to fourth order in KL. Because the mean-field dynamics preserves the ratio of precision spread to shape spread, the spread at a join is delta times a preparation constant, and it decays afterward at rate 2 (rate 8 in KL). A theorem of the form "approximate inheritance with KL defect O((delta rho)^4 e^{-8(t - t_join)})" on a full open set of 4-agent Wishart preparations would remove the prepared common precision up to a stated error. It would answer the PIFB2 request to quantify the closure error on formation and handoff horizons, and it would show how much redundancy the contraction creates, as opposed to finds at time zero.

The third step is the general-N statement in L08-CND-09. Replacing form:gaps by the exact region costs nothing, and the one-dimensional no-cascade lemma gives recursive formation of every depth up to N - 1 with closed-form join times. Stating it explicitly clarifies what the chapter proves, a centroid dendrogram of the prepared shapes read on a clock set by delta, and it gives a reference case against which any interaction-driven construction can be compared: an interaction-sensitive selector must produce a dendrogram different from the centroid dendrogram of its own initial data for some preparation.

The fourth step makes the geometry persistent. With an anisotropic, context-dependent belief reference W(alpha_0, Gamma(c)) and an anisotropic model reference, the flow keeps common precision and sum compatibility, while the fixed point P*(c) remains anisotropic and context dependent. The numerical example gives a limiting curvature of -1.689 and a limiting tensor diag(1.168, 0.199). This turns "nonzero curvature at every finite time" into a stationary geometric statement, which the program needs before any identification of the contextual tensor with a physical metric.
