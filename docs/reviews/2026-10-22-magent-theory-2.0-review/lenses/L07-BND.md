Verdict: every identity in the chapter that I recomputed is correct. That includes the full coupled fine Fisher flow with incoming source derivatives and row dynamics (machine-precision agreement at depth two with finite uniform circles and at depth three with fair coins), the pair-law form of the class cost, the response and record descent, the reuse and shared-label failures, the total-variation formula, and the observability determinant. The weakness is structural. The theorem is a symmetry reduction: every fibered reference-preserving transformation of the public circle coordinates is an exact symmetry of the fine energy and metric, the prepared family is the fixed-point set of a lifted tree-automorphism subgroup, and the hierarchy is its orbit quotient. In addition, the identity-channel potential drives all public contrast to zero at rate 3 + 2/(N-1), so the discarded information is gauge data that also decays. Atomlessness is not used by any of the exact statements.

## Findings

### L07-BND-01: The hierarchy theorem is an equivariant symmetry reduction; the discarded phases are gauge for the fine energy
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** high
**Location:** chapters/10_boundary_closure.tex:4, 9-37, 100, 126-134, 178-180; thm:boundary-tree-flow, thm:boundary-tree-inheritance; companion/c10_boundary.tex:4
**Evidence:** Let G be the group of bimeasurable maps Phi(r,theta) = (r, phi_r(theta)) of the belief sample space that preserve A x Haar (and the analogous model-sector maps). Because all peer channels are identities and rho(r,w) reads only base coordinates, simultaneous pullback q_i -> q_i o Phi leaves every term of eq:dyn-potential invariant: KL(q_i o Phi || q_j o Phi) = KL(q_i || q_j); the local term depends on q_i only through its entropy against the reference and its base marginal, both invariant; the density-form Fisher metric (eq:dyn-fisher-metric) is invariant. Hence the fine flow is G-equivariant for every configuration, not only on the family.

The family with phases alpha is exactly the fixed-point set Fix(H_alpha) of the subgroup H_alpha of pairs (Phi, pi), pi a leaf permutation, satisfying u_alpha(pi(i), Phi(x)) = u_alpha(i, x). Invariance under arc-preserving maps forces dependence on theta only through the signs. Invariance under "flip sigma_w and swap the circles of the two subtrees of w" forces, by induction from the lowest internal nodes upward, that q_i depends only on its own path signs. Transitivity then gives a single profile f, and rows fixed by H_alpha are class-constant. The map from sign vectors to labelings, (sigma_v)_v -> (i -> u(i,theta)), is a bijection from {-1,1}^(N-1) onto Aut(T_d), whose order is 2^(N-1), so q_i(x) = F(r, tau(x) i) with tau(x) uniform on Aut(T_d).

Fixed-point sets of an equivariant flow with unique solutions are invariant, and the restricted field is the gradient of the restricted energy for the induced metric (Palais, "The principle of symmetric criticality", Comm. Math. Phys. 69 (1979) 19-30; for flow invariance of fixed-point subspaces, Golubitsky, Stewart and Schaeffer, Singularities and Groups in Bifurcation Theory II, Springer 1988, and Field, Dynamics and Symmetry, Imperial College Press 2007; I did not check section locators). The parent descriptors are orbits under the normalizer elements that move between fixed-point sets (phase rotations and base-dependent tree automorphisms). The fresh uniform-descendant mixture at node v is invariant under the part acting below v, and base-only record likelihoods commute with all of G. Every claim of thm:boundary-tree-inheritance follows from these two facts.

The "public partition field" that is discarded is therefore data that no term of the fine energy, metric, or declared record kernel can read; the TV-continuous, non-Fisher-smooth character of its orbits (L07-BND-09) is the expected geometry of orbits of a group of measure-preserving maps. The chapter text (line 4: an exact hierarchy of public compressions with a coupled evaluator; line 180: general base laws, a coupled evaluator, identity interactions) presents the coupled evaluator and general base laws as features of the compression, but both act only on f_0, g_0 and are orthogonal to the compressed data. The heterogeneous-block computation in L07-BND-14 shows the converse: as soon as the two branches at a node are not conjugate under the symmetry, the fresh response at that node exposes its phase (TV 0.018 at t = 1, `hetero_blocks_check.out`). Compression in this construction exists exactly where symmetry exists.
**Fix:** State the result as an instance of a short equivariance lemma: for a group of reference-preserving fibered sample maps combined with agent permutations that preserve the edge set, priors and evaluator, (i) Fix(H) is flow-invariant with restricted energy and metric, (ii) the flow descends to Fix(H)/K for K in the normalizer, (iii) K-invariant response and record kernels descend. The binary tree is then the case H = H_alpha, K = phase rotations and base-dependent Aut(T_d) below level l. Cite symmetric criticality and fixed-point-subspace invariance. Say explicitly that the discarded phases are gauge for the fine energy and metric, and identify what is not a consequence of symmetry (at present only the explicit class costs D_k and the identification of the fresh response with the subtree mean). The nontrivial targets are listed in L07-BND-12, -14, -16 and in the closing section.
**Falsifies:** A pair of fine states with the same descriptor that are not related by any element of G combined with a tree automorphism, yet have the same future descriptor and responses (a genuine lumping not induced by symmetry) within the stated family.

### L07-BND-02: Public contrast decays exponentially under the identity-channel potential; the compression describes a transient
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/10_boundary_closure.tex:166-171 (eq:boundary-tree-witness and "Strict differences persist on a nonzero regular time interval by continuity"), 180; companion/c10_boundary.tex:74; AUTHORING.md boundary paragraph (contrast with its collective-phase paragraph, which states "their differences decay")
**Evidence:** Linearize the profile flow eq:boundary-tree-flow at a u-independent profile, f = f_0(r)(1 + phi) with E_U phi = 0. With log f = log f_0 + phi, P_k log f = log f_0 + P_k phi and P_k f / f = 1 + P_k phi - phi, the u-dependent part of the force is (3 - 2 sum_k b_k P_k) phi, so phi' = -(3 - 2 sum_k b_k P_k) phi. On a Walsh mode chi_S with largest index m, P_k chi_S = 0 for k < m-1, = -chi_S for k = m-1, and = +chi_S for k >= m, so the rate is lambda_m = 3 + 2 b_{m-1} - 2 sum_{k>=m} b_k >= 1 for every row vector. At the trivial manifold D_k = 0, so rows relax to pi_k = n_k/(N-1), and then b_{m-1} - sum_{k>=m} b_k = 1/(N-1) for every m: all contrast modes decay at 3 + 2/(N-1), the same rate 1 + kappa_N as the complete-graph difference mode of ch. 9 (eq:public-phase-linearization with J = 0). Numerically, `pairlaw_jensen_decay.out` gives eigenvalues 3.6667, 3.2857, 3.1333 for d = 2, 3, 4 (all modes equal), and `depth3_coin_check.out` measures a nonlinear contrast decay rate of 3.2856 against the prediction 3.2857.

Globally, for fixed f_0 the profile energy is minimized only by u-independent f, because the conditional relative entropy (integral of f log(f/f_0)) and every D_k are nonnegative and vanish exactly there. In the depth-two run the child-law TV caused by a pi/2 phase rotation falls from 0.0965 at t = 0 (`reuse_t0_check.out`) to 0.0021 at t = 1 (`depth2_flow_check.out`). So the "public information lost at every depth" is a quantity the fine dynamics itself erases. At consensus the family does not even separate in rate from non-family difference directions, since the same matrix 3I - 2B acts pointwise on leaf vectors.
**Fix:** State the decay rate and the energy argument next to eq:boundary-tree-witness, mirroring the treatment in ch. 9 and in AUTHORING's collective-phase paragraph. Replace "Strict differences persist on a nonzero regular time interval" by the sharper pair of statements: differences decay like exp(-(3 + 2/(N-1))t) near the trivial manifold, and, if wanted, do not vanish at finite time. The latter needs its own short argument, because the set of states with equal branch means is not invariant.
**Falsifies:** A nontrivial (u-dependent) stable equilibrium of the profile flow, or a positive-measure set of initial profiles whose contrast does not tend to zero.

### L07-BND-03: The record interface reads only the common base marginal; any record reading a public coordinate breaks the compression
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/10_boundary_closure.tex:127, 144-153 (eq:boundary-tree-feedback); companion/c10_boundary.tex:62-66
**Evidence:** With L_e(o|r) depending only on r, the record law from any fresh sample at any node is the integral of f_0 L_e dA (the ancestor bits integrate out), so every admitted record history is a functional of the root-marginal trajectory f_0(t) alone, and the conditioning f+ = L f / Z multiplies all profile values by a G-invariant factor. If instead the likelihood reads a public coordinate, for example L(o | r, sigma_0), the update multiplies leaves in the two root branches by different factors. By the computation in L07-BND-14 the fine flow still closes on a two-profile family, but the next fresh root response becomes (1/2)[f^L_1(r, sigma_0) + f^R_1(r, -sigma_0)], which depends on the root phase. So the admissible record class is exactly the class that cannot observe anything beyond f_0. Records must also be broadcast to all N agents, because a record delivered to one subtree produces the same two-profile asymmetry. The theorem is correct as stated, but "arbitrary finite adaptive sequences" of records descend only because these records see nothing that the compression touches.
**Fix:** Add one sentence: the admitted records are those whose likelihoods are invariant under the compression group, which here means base-only and broadcast, and their histories are functionals of f_0(t). If a richer feedback interface is wanted, the next case is records reading ancestor path bits at node v; it requires retaining the ancestor phases and gives a different descriptor.
**Falsifies:** A record kernel reading a circle coordinate, applied to all agents, for which the updated fresh responses remain phase-independent for generic f.

### L07-BND-07: The associative selective-channel construction is exact but source-blind and mean-field
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** low
**Location:** Research/docs/research/2026-10-21-boundary-closure.md:60-126 (not in the manuscript; referenced from README.md)
**Evidence:** All recorded identities check (`selective_channel_check.out`). E = 2 sum KL(q_i||A) + N W(m,e) holds to 1.1e-16; q_j K_ij = nu to 2.8e-17. The gradient of the generic energy, taken by finite differences with the law-dependent channel rebuilt at each evaluation (so source and channel derivatives are included without using the cancellation), matches 2 log f_i + c T to 4.8e-10 after centering. The explicit solution l_i(t) = e^(-2t) l_i(0) + b(t) T - log Z_i matches the fine flow to 7.8e-15 at t = 1. Joint characteristic functions confirm P_(I u J) = P_I * P_J (residual 1.2e-10, limited by key rounding). The pairs (ab, 1) and (a, b) have identical block statistics (2.8e-17) and different child laws.

The weakness is in the design. Since q_j K_ij = nu_(m,e) for every source, every source in a row has the same cost, the rows carry no information about sources, and the interaction reduces to a mean-field potential N W(m). Associativity under arbitrary regrouping is the exchangeability of a function of the empirical mean. Initial log-density heterogeneity decays like e^(-2t), so all agents converge to the same exponential-family law. The channel's epsilon must satisfy epsilon < (1/2) exp(-2 L_lambda R - 2B) with B depending on the initial sup-norm bound L_0, so the "original channel" is defined relative to an admitted class of initial conditions rather than independently of the state.
**Fix:** If the construction is moved into the manuscript, state that it is a mean-field realization with source-blind channels, that the rows are uninformative, and that epsilon is fixed by the admitted initial class.
**Falsifies:** A version with source-dependent costs (nonuniform Gibbs rows) that keeps the convolution join exact.

### L07-BND-15: Global existence on [0, infinity) by an energy bound and a maximum principle
**Category:** improvement
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/10_boundary_closure.tex:100 (last sentence: "unique solutions on their regular existence intervals")
**Evidence:** Sketch. Write l = log f, so l' = -2l - V + sum_k b_k (P_k l + P_k e^l / e^l) + c. ### L07-BND-17: Bridge to the Gaussian implementation: the mechanism has no Gaussian realization with continuous phases
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/10_boundary_closure.tex:156-161, 171-180; implementation C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_4.0 (finite Gaussian model)
**Evidence:** The construction hides a continuous phase because complementary children m(1 + a sigma) and m(1 - a sigma) average to m, which is flat in the circle coordinate. For Gaussian children the analogue fails: with isotropic covariance, (1/2)[N(v, I) + N(-v, I)] has density proportional to phi(y) exp(-|v|^2/2) cosh(y . v), which depends on the direction of v unless v = 0. A finite Gaussian mixture invariant under a continuous rotation group must have every component centered and isotropic in the rotated plane, which forces identical children. In the Gaussian implementation, only discrete child exchanges (Z_2 reflections) can be discarded under fresh mixture responses, that is, nothing beyond agent permutations.
**Fix:** Record this as a scope statement for any implementation of boundary compression: phase-free fresh responses with continuous phases require non-Gaussian (complementary) child laws, or a response kernel restricted to rotation-invariant statistics. Difficulty: low for the displayed computation; I did not locate a primary citation for the general invariance statement about finite Gaussian mixtures.
**Falsifies:** A nontrivial pair of Gaussian child laws whose equal mixture is invariant under a continuous rotation of their mean directions.

## Extensions and improvements

The most useful reframing separates what symmetry gives from what it does not. With identity channels and an evaluator that reads only base coordinates, the fine system is invariant under every fibered reference-preserving map of the public coordinates, so its flow descends to the joint conditional spectrum of all agents' density values for every configuration (L07-BND-12). The boundary hierarchy then becomes the statement that, on the fixed-point set of lifted tree automorphisms, the full-sample fresh responses at intermediate nodes are measurable with respect to conditional spectra given ancestor coordinates. Writing the chapter this way shortens the proofs, makes the wreath-product generalization immediate (L07-BND-13), and isolates the open content, which is compression of dynamically active information; no symmetry quotient provides that.

A nontrivial version needs at least one of three ingredients. The first is a fiber of the retention containing states not related by a symmetry. The two-agent observability proposition rules this out locally for mixture responses in the open binary model, so any positive result must live on a thin invariant set that is not a fixed-point set, or use a non-identity, state-dependent channel. The second is persistence. Under the present potential every public contrast decays at rate 3 + 2/(N-1) near consensus (L07-BND-02), so persistent compression requires a potential with u-dependent stable equilibria; the first concrete check is whether the linearized operator 3 - 2 sum_k b_k P_k, augmented by a candidate term (for example agreement with leaf-specific records in a symmetric record design), can acquire a zero or negative eigenvalue on Walsh modes. The third is heterogeneity. The two-block family (L07-BND-14) gives exact dynamics with genuinely different parent states, and the conjugacy condition that f^L_1(r,s) + f^R_1(r,-s) be independent of s is the precise obstruction to compressing the join phase; asking which potentials drive heterogeneous blocks into the conjugate set is a formation question with a checkable target.

On the analytic side, the a priori bound sketched in L07-BND-15 turns all statements into global-in-time statements, and the convexity of D_k (L07-BND-08) gives a Lyapunov function for the belief sector with frozen inputs; together they would allow a convergence theorem for the profile flow. On the interface side, minimality of the unordered tree (L07-BND-16) is the natural analogue of prop:boundary-observable inside the hierarchy itself and is tractable at d = 1 by the same Jacobian method. For the implementation, the Gaussian obstruction in L07-BND-17 means that any boundary compression in VFE_4.0 must either discard only discrete child exchanges or restrict its response kernels to rotation-invariant statistics.
