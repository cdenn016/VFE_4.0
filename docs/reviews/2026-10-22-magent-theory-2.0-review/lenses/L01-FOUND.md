### L01-FOUND-02: Law-level loop comparisons cannot detect holonomy inside the stabilizer of the compared law; shared-sample records can
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** high
**Location:** chapters/02_interactions.tex:131-145 (eq:int-nongaussian-loop, "communication loop"); supplements/s01_probability.tex:51-53 (two routes); chapters/01_foundations.tex:116
**Evidence:** Proposition (proved here). Let $H$ be a bimeasurable bijection of a standard Borel space $E$ (a loop or holonomy map) and $\nu\in\Prob(E)$. (i) Every functional of the pair of laws $(\nu,H_\#\nu)$, in particular $\KL(\nu\Vert H_\#\nu)$, takes the same value for $H$ and $H'$ whenever $H_\#\nu=H'_\#\nu$; $\KL(\nu\Vert H_\#\nu)=0$ exactly when $H\in\operatorname{Stab}(\nu)$. (ii) The shared-sample law $(\mathrm{id},H)_\#\nu$ of $(Y,HY)$, $Y\sim\nu$, determines $H$ $\nu$-almost everywhere: if $(\mathrm{id},H)_\#\nu=(\mathrm{id},H')_\#\nu$, evaluate both on the Borel set $\{(y,z):z\ne H'y\}$ (the complement of a Borel graph) to get $\nu\{H\ne H'\}=0$. Under a frame change $g$ at the base vertex the pair transforms diagonally, $(Y,HY)\mapsto(gY,(gHg^{-1})gY)$, so invariant records are diagonal-invariant functions of the pair. Instances: the s01 route mark ($\nu$ uniform on a bit, $H$ the flip) gives $\KL(\nu\Vert H_\#\nu)=0$ while the shared-sample XOR record equals the mark with probability one (script: pair law [[0,0.5],[0.5,0]], XOR record 1.0); a rotation holonomy by 0.7 rad gives $\KL=0$ for an isotropic Gaussian and 0.2767 for covariance diag(1,3); the design record's Hopf witness uses $H=-I$ on a rotation-invariant law, invisible to $\KL(\nu\Vert H_\#\nu)$ and visible to $\Vert(H-I)v_s\Vert^2$ on a shared sample. Every interaction cost in chapters 2 and 3 has the law-level form $\KL(\nu_i\Vert\nu_j\mathcal K_{ij})$, so the dynamics as defined cannot respond to holonomy in the stabilizer of the transported law.
**Fix:** State the proposition after eq:int-nongaussian-loop or in the foundations' correlation section, and state that a holonomy-sensitive record must read a coupling of the same sample along the routes, which requires the content law of L01-FOUND-01. Difficulty: the proposition is one page; constructing a dynamics in which such a record conditions the laws and persists is a substantive open step (the Hopf witness is an existence instance with a fixed record).
**Falsifies:** An interaction in the manuscript whose cost reads a coupling rather than a pair of laws, or a law-level functional that separates $H$ from $H'$ with $H_\#\nu=H'_\#\nu$ (impossible by (i)).

### L01-FOUND-05: The measurable structure of the statistical bundle needs a jointly Borel action, and no section regularity is stated
**Category:** gap
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/01_foundations.tex:8-17 (def:agent-section, eq:statistical-bundle)
**Evidence:** The definition gives $\Prob(\mathsf Y)$ its evaluation sigma-algebra but specifies no sigma-algebra on $\mathcal B$ and no regularity or openness for sections and domains $\mathcal U_i$. The phrase "measurable $G$-actions by bimeasurable bijections" admits the reading "each $g$ acts bimeasurably", under which $\mathcal B$ is not well defined as a measurable object. Counterexample: $G=(\R,+)$, $\mathsf Y=\R$, $g\cdot y=y+\varphi(g)$ with $\varphi$ a discontinuous additive (Hamel) function; each $g$ is a translation. A Lebesgue-measurable additive function is linear (Banach, Sierpinski 1920), so $\varphi^{-1}(A)$ is non-Borel for some Borel $A$. On $P=\R\times G$ take sections $\sigma_1(c)=(c,0)$, $\sigma_2(c)=(c,c)$, transition $g_{12}(c)=c$. The section that is constant $\delta_0$ in the $\sigma_2$ trivialization has $\sigma_1$-coordinate $c\mapsto\delta_{\varphi(c)}$, and $c\mapsto\delta_{\varphi(c)}(A)=1_A(\varphi(c))$ is non-Borel, so measurability of sections depends on the trivialization. Under a jointly Borel action $G\times\mathsf Y\to\mathsf Y$ (with $G$ a second-countable Lie group and $\mathcal C$ second countable) the statement is repaired: $(g,\zeta)\mapsto\zeta(g^{-1}A)=\int1_A(gy)\zeta(dy)$ is Borel by a monotone-class argument from rectangles; the gluing maps $(c,\zeta)\mapsto(c,g_{\alpha\beta}(c)_\#\zeta)$ are Borel automorphisms; $\mathcal B$ is standard Borel with an atlas-independent structure ($\Prob(\mathsf Y)$ is standard Borel, Kechris 1995 sec. 17.E, locator not opened). Transition maps then preserve the evaluation sigma-algebra, the mixture structure (pushforward is affine, so fiberwise mixtures are defined), null sets and mutual absolute continuity, KL (prop:kl-coordinate), total variation, and the Fisher metric on $G$-stable regular families. They preserve no weak topology unless the action is continuous; by the Becker-Kechris theorem (Becker and Kechris 1996, Thm. 5.2.1, locator from memory) a compatible Polish topology making the action continuous exists, so a topological bundle is available but no result needs it. Later chapters use only smooth sections on open domains into $E=P\times_G\mathcal N$ (02_geometry.tex:63-66); openness of $\mathcal U_i$ is used there but not stated in def:agent-section.
**Fix:** "Let $G$ act on $\mathsf K,\mathsf M$ by jointly Borel actions." Add the two-sentence lemma above, state that $\mathcal U_i$ is open, that a statistical section is a Borel section of $\mathcal B$ over $\mathcal U_i$, and that smooth sections are defined only into admitted smooth subbundles. State that disintegration commutes with the product action: the conditional of $g_\#\zeta$ is $m'\mapsto g_\#q(\cdot\mid g^{-1}m')$, $g_\#s$-almost everywhere, so eq:agent-disintegration descends to bundle points.
**Falsifies:** A sentence in the manuscript declaring joint measurability of the action and a measurable structure on $\mathcal B$.

### L01-FOUND-06: Configuration-dependent channels are not required to be measurable in the configuration, and the generator definition is narrower than the channels used later

## Extensions and improvements

The most valuable next step is L01-FOUND-01 together with L01-FOUND-04: restate locally the design record's content law $Q$ on represented fields, its finite-design consistency, and covariant record kernels $K_a(do\mid z)$, and add covariance requirements on readouts. With $\mathbb P=P_0\prod_aK_a$ fixed, the identity $\mathcal F(Q;o)=\KL(Q\Vert\Pi_o)-\log Z_o$ and its projected chain rule give a generative object against which the law-level potential of chapter 3 can be compared exactly; the affine-versus-convex obstruction of eq:dyn-affine-obstruction then says the comparison must be made on $Q$, not on law points. A concrete target theorem: for a declared sufficient-message architecture, the marginal sections $\zeta_i^{Q_t}$ of the Fisher flow of $\mathcal F(Q;o)$ obey an autonomous law-level equation, with the projected chain-rule term as the exact closure error.

Holonomy-sensitive records need two facts that this lens supplies. L01-FOUND-02 shows that law-level loop comparisons see holonomy only modulo the stabilizer of the transported law, whereas the graph law $(\mathrm{id},H)_\#\nu$ of a shared sample determines $H$ almost everywhere; L01-FOUND-09 gives a covariant split of directed links into a reciprocal part $\Omega_{ij}$ (a groupoid representation, so reversal is inversion) and a backtrack loop $N_j$. A holonomy record should read conjugacy-class functions of $\Omega$ cycle products acting on a shared sample. The next theorem would show that such a record, fed back through Bayesian or variational assimilation, changes the laws in a way that no law-level cost can, with the Hopf or $\mathbb Z_2$ equator witness as the base case. The $\Omega$ construction is also a candidate input to open:conn-derived: a displacement family $\Omega(c+\delta c,c)$ with identity at zero displacement would give the connection by prop:conn-curvature.

For coarse-graining, the equivariant-kernel bundle morphisms of L01-FOUND-10 supply the missing definition behind the map $\Psi$ of the geometry chapter and the retention of chapter 5, and an agent type makes "a parent is an agent" a checkable statement. This requires the agent-dependent spaces of L01-FOUND-03. L01-FOUND-11 makes the record appendix quantitative: the KL between the Bayesian and any variational record process is the expected sum of one-step predictive divergences, bounded by the posterior divergences, which links the internal record process to evidence comparisons.

Finally, one appendix lemma could close L01-FOUND-05, -06 and -13 together: a jointly Borel action gives a standard Borel bundle; channels that are kernels on $\mathsf S\times E_j$ give Borel costs, rows and reduced potentials through the partition representation of KL; and vector fields built from pointwise Borel maps and integrals against fixed kernels have Borel flows. This would make the frontmatter's statement that "General probability measures are used whenever measurability suffices" true as written.
