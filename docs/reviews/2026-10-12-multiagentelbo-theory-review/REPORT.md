## 1. Summary and recommendation

The manuscript develops a multi-agent variational free-energy theory at one base point: agents as fiber values of associated bundles, independent links (held, or latent Lie-algebra generators under a normalized prior), one collective evidence bound, attention as inference over a latent source label, fiber information geometry, fast/slow learning, exact finite-step coarse-graining of the agent network, and a formation theory culminating in an adiabatic pairing-selection theorem on a four-cycle. The mathematics is in good condition. Across eleven lenses no load-bearing theorem was found false, and every identity, witness, and constant that was recomputed (several hundred, in float64, SymPy, exact rationals, exhaustive enumeration, and small simulations) reproduced, typically to 1e-13 or better. The one statement that fails under its literal reading is a sub-item of a Chapter 8 theorem with a missing hypothesis (comment 7). The pairing-selection proof, the parent propositions, and the ESTABLISHED negatives hold at the level of detail written.

The defects that matter are of two other kinds. First, the framing claims more than the numbered statements deliver: the program aim of Chapter 1 asserts three theses (participatory theory, formation through coupled inference, parents acting back on children) that no model family in the manuscript realizes together, and two lenses independently found that the adaptive multiscale tower, the setting of the formation witnesses, has a free energy identical to the depth-0 free energy, so its parents add no inference. Second, the prior-art positioning is thin in exactly the places where the manuscript's distinctive claims sit (formation, attention as a latent label, Markov-blanket coarse-graining, exact RG). For implementation, the manuscript leaves open several data an implementer must fix before writing code. The author has now settled two of them (independent links in the two directions of a mutual pair, identity links on agent-level self edges; see the conventions below); the discrete update schedule remains open, along with two narrower items that matter only in some regimes: an estimator for the generator-prior normalizer, needed only when anchors or loop coefficients are learned, and a prior-width guideline for the exponential chart.

Recommendation: major revision, driven by framing, prior art, and the multiscale reading, not by mathematical error. The technical core would be minor revision on its own. Comments 1 to 3 are essential; comments 4 to 9 should be fixed before submission; the minor list is optional polish.

### Author conventions recorded October 12, 2026

After reading the first version of this report, the author fixed two conventions that the manuscript leaves open. They govern VFE_4.0 and resolve the modeling question of comment 5; the manuscript still needs to state them.

Links in the two directions of a mutually attending pair are independent: Θ_ij ≠ Θ_ji in general. In the manuscript's typing (`def:geo-graph-links`, `c02:308-322`, where every copy e has a reversal ē with Θ_ē = Θ_e⁻¹ and parallel copies are allowed), this means that row i's source copy j→i and row j's source copy i→j are two distinct copies, each with its own reversal, not one copy and its reversal. Two consequences follow. Every mutually attending pair is a closed walk i→j→i with holonomy Θ_ij Θ_ji, generically different from the identity. The author's choice is that these 2-cycles belong to the declared loop family of the generator prior, so that even two agents interacting only with each other carry a loop term c_p Q_p with Q_p the defect of Θ_ij Θ_ji from the identity. The prior then favors reciprocal links, Θ_ji ≈ Θ_ij⁻¹, softly, with strength set by the loop coefficient; as that coefficient grows the prior concentrates on Θ_ji = Θ_ij⁻¹, so the inverse convention is the hard-constraint limit of this model rather than a separate one. Because the defect is measured in a fixed fiber metric, the 2-cycle based at i (Θ_ij Θ_ji) and the one based at j (Θ_ji Θ_ij) are conjugate but generally have different defects (L2 measured 0.159 between rotations of a triangle), so the base vertex is still to be declared; including both based walks, one per endpoint, keeps the prior symmetric in i and j and is the natural default. Under product generator recognition the two links of a 2-cycle are distinct reversal pairs, so its expected defect reduces to per-edge first and second exponential moments (g11, `eq:glink-expected-defect`). The latent-link lane of MultiAgentELBO (`coupled_learning.py:15-58`, `latent_calibration.py:74-75`) uses the other convention, one reversal pair per unordered pair with Θ_ji = Θ_ij⁻¹, so it implements a different model from the one VFE_4.0 will build; only `locality.py` draws independent maps per direction. VFE_3.0's frame-aligned transports exp(φ_i) exp(−φ_j) satisfy Ω_ji = Ω_ij⁻¹ and are the trivial-holonomy special case of the independent convention.

At the agent level a self edge carries the identity link, Θ_ii = I. This makes D_ii = 0 a consequence rather than the bare hypothesis it is in `prop:obs-attention-elbo`(a). It does not extend to coarse depth: when a block of agents becomes a meta-agent, the internal copies become loop copies of the parent whose links are internal holonomies (`def:full-graph-coarse-links`), so Θ_II ≠ I there, and the loop-copy energy and cost branch of comment 5 are still needed.

## 3. Major comments

**1. The program aim and the formation framing claim more than the numbered statements establish (essential).** Chapter 1 (`c01:24-27`) states that "the program is a participatory multi-agent theory" in which collectives form through coupled inference and parents act back on their children. L10-01 traced each clause to the statements that would realize it and found that no model family realizes the three together: formation runs on a supplied hierarchy or a seeded initial assignment, downward coupling is proved only in a declared hierarchy (an ordinary hierarchical prior), and in the adaptive towers used by the formation witnesses the downward kernel is the Bayes reversal kernel (see comment 2). The headline theorem `conj:phil-contrast-formation` (ESTABLISHED, stated in c14) works through the aggregate-prior learning rule, which is the continuous-time M-step for mixture proportions, a mechanism that `c14:164-173` itself calls non-discriminating; yet `c14:13` says the theorem "sharpens the participatory reading" (L10-02). L6-01 found that the label "idle source learning" on `prop:phil-seeded-contrast-formation` is stronger than the proof, which gives a learning-independent lower bound while learning acts on the seed interval and rows move by up to 0.43 within the horizon; the heading and the summaries at `c14:7-11`, `c14:245-248`, and `c01:45-47` repeat it. L10-07 lists further stale or causal wording ("with learning" at `c01:17`, "seed of symmetry breaking" at `c06:1165`, "conjectured more generally" at `c01:245`, "the conjecture" at `c13:26`). The fix is to restate the program aim as a target, with each thesis pointing to the open problem or declared model that would realize it, and to scope the formation summaries to what the selection theorem proves: law-level selection by a mixture-proportion learning rule from almost every Gaussian initial law, against a symmetric frozen arm.

**2. The adaptive-tower free energy equals the depth-0 free energy, and Chapter 12 does not say so (essential; corroborated by L5 and L11 independently).** When the recognition law is pushed through the same structural channels as the generative law and the posterior, the chain rule for relative entropy gives KL(Q_0 ⊗ C ‖ Π_0 ⊗ C) = KL(Q_0 ‖ Π_0), so F_tower = F_0 (L5 residual 1.3e-15; L11 by derivation). The partition, graph, and parent–child terms of `thm:full-graph-vfe-decomposition` then redistribute KL(Q_0 ‖ Π_0) without adding an objective, and a coarse recognition law parameterized independently of the children has infinite divergence whenever the channel has a deterministic part (L5-1, L11-7). The theorem is correct; what is missing is the consequence, which bears directly on the "parents act back" thesis of comment 1 and on how a multiscale implementation must be built. Parents that carry their own inference need a declared hierarchy, and the only written Gaussian instance with link transports is the one-level anchored star of g10. Fix: state F_tower = F_0 as a corollary in `sec:full-graph-vfe-joint`, point to the g10 star as the declared-hierarchy instance, and record the multilevel declared hierarchy (stars of stars with the dressed links of `def:full-graph-coarse-links`) as an open construction. Related: L5-2 found that the c11 interaction coordinates assume a fixed product over agents, so they do not type the c12 tower state, whose vertex set depends on the random mark R_ℓ and which carries edge variables; `open:full-graph-hierarchy` uses a closure residual that is undefined for that class.

**3. Prior art is missing where the distinctive claims sit (essential).** L10-03 to L10-06 checked the positioning against primary literature. Chapter 13 contains no citations, although cluster formation is known in non-participatory models (Hegselmann–Krause bounded confidence; self-attention clustering and metastability, Geshkovski et al. 2023 and 2024; Palacios et al. 2020), which makes "formation could separate the program from hierarchical models" a weak test in Mayo's sense. Attention as a latent source label and the transformer correspondence are presented without Kim et al. 2017, Deng et al. 2018, Movellan and Gabbur 2020, Ramsauer et al. 2021, or Singh and Buckley 2023, and sheaf neural and sheaf attention networks, which carry per-edge transports, are not discussed; five of these keys are already in `references.bib` and cited nowhere. The c15 contrast with multi-agent active inference is contradicted by Friston et al. 2021 ("Parcels and particles"), which computes coarse states from fine dynamics. "Exact coarse-graining" is Kadanoff's exact-RG normalization condition (see Mehta and Schwab 2014, Eqs. 8 and 9), which is never credited, and the claim at `c05:493` that no partition criterion "is known" does not engage mutual-information RG (Koch-Janusz and Ringel 2018) or the information bottleneck. L9-1 adds a Supplement B instance: the native free model of `def:rg-pressure-native` is exactly a 2D ferromagnetic Ising lattice gas in a field (brute force at L = 2, 3 to 1e-15), so by Lee–Yang and Lebowitz–Penrose (1968) its pressure is real-analytic on all of (0,1)², and the B = 1 C^4 and Fisher statements are special cases of classical results (L9 checked Lebowitz–Penrose through the abstract and a secondary summary only).

**4. Links: one sentence of prose needs a qualifier, and the prior width needs a stated guideline.** Neither point affects a theorem. L2-1: latent links are written Θ = exp(ω), and not every element of GL⁺(K) is an exponential of a real matrix (diag(−1, −2) is not; for K = 2 about 21% of determinant-positive matrices are missed, and for GL(K) the whole determinant-negative component). `thm:link-held-limit` correctly proves that a held link is the zero-width limit of the latent model when the held link is exp of an anchor, but the prose at `c08b:21`, `c08b:1207`, `c01:37-40`, `c01:117`, and `c14:120` drops that qualifier. If every link of interest comes from a generator, nothing is lost; the sentences should say "held links in the image of exp", or the model can add a held base twist A_e to reach the rest. L11-5: exp is also many-to-one (generators differing by 2π times a rotation generator give the same link), so a wide generator prior produces a posterior with several equivalent modes, of which a Gaussian recognition law on ω captures one. Under a prior N(0, s²I) on gl(K) the mass in the ambiguous region is 2.8% at K = 8, s = 1 and 45% at K = 8, s = 1.5, scaling like s√K. A remark that the base covariance should keep entry widths below about π/√K, so that the Gaussian-recognition statements are read on the principal domain, would close this.

**5. The graph conventions the author has fixed are not written in the manuscript.** L11-1 (high as a typing gap; the modeling question is now settled by the author conventions above): `def:obs-attention-label-model` and `c01:168-171` do not say whether, when i attends to j and j attends to i, row j's copy is the reversal of row i's copy (one link, Θ_ji = Θ_ij⁻¹) or a distinct copy with an independent link. The author's convention is the second. The text should declare the copy set as a structural datum before the rows, with independent copies for mutually supported pairs, change "is the reversal closure" to "contains the reversal closure" at `c01:168-171`, and say whether the resulting 2-cycles i→j→i are in the declared loop family. Endpoint notation Θ_ij is defined only when the copy j→i is unique (`c02:313`), and under this convention it is not (the source copy of row i and the reversal of row j's source copy both run j→i), so the text should define Θ_ij as the link of the source copy of row i. Under the independent convention the link space has dimension N(N−1) dim G for complete support, one generator per ordered pair. L11-2 and L4-3: self edges are typed only through the hypothesis D_ii = 0. The author's convention Θ_ii = I at the agent level supplies this at depth 0 and should be stated in `def:obs-attention-label-model`; at coarse depth the loop copies carry internal holonomy and D ≠ 0, which the text should note with a pointer to `def:full-graph-coarse-links`. Independently of the link, `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` is silent on the self edge, where its formula fails (Monte Carlo weights 0.893, 0.106, 0.001 against 0.561, 0.434, 0.006 from the formula): the self component is an exact attention sink with factor det(I + W̃ S^Q Σ S^{Q⊤})^{1/2}, and the proposition needs a self-edge case.

**6. The transformer correspondence table derives its rows in two different models.** L4-1: the query/key, softmax, and temperature rows come from the law-point model (`cor:obs-selective-softmax`), whose weights are the posterior row; the value/residual row comes from the comparison-record model, whose weights are not that row. The comparison-record model already gives a dot product on its own with metric (Σ^pred)⁻¹ (1.1e-16), and the parallel mean-field update has the same transformer-shaped mean with a third, different row (2.6e-15). The table should be derived in one model, and the text should name which of the three rows it means by "attention". L4-2: the multi-head row ("no label-level construction / absent") understates what is derived; the exact H-head receiver update has the multi-head operator form (4.4e-16), with per-head rows that are marginals of a coupled, non-factoring label table. L4-6: the table has no positional row, though RoPE is a flat nonidentity twist (Su et al. 2021, Sec. 3.2.2, Eq. 16) and ALiBi is log π_ij; "a nonidentity twist has no counterpart" holds only for absolute encodings. L11-6 is the implementation side of the same issue: the c12 row free energies use the law-level transported KL (the VFE_3.0 energy), which has no in-bound realization while `open:prob-law-point-prior` is open; the only in-bound route is the sample-level comparison record.

**7. `thm:rg-memory-inference-descent`(iv) fails under its literal reading.** L1-1: the text concludes that the parent sweep decreases `eq:rg-memory-fixed-vfe` from an identity about KL(q ‖ p) alone. If the sweep acts on q while the recognition record conditional ψ′ is held fixed and mismatched, the free energy rises from 0.693 to 3.446; with the marked sweep it decreases, but the strictness condition needs ψ′ = ψ. Nothing else cites (iv). Add the hypothesis ψ′ = ψ, or state (iv) for the marked sweep.

**8. Two information-geometry statements need hypotheses.** L3-01: `c07:358-362` says the configuration metrics are frame independent, and the sentence covers the exact-lift pullback ι_i^* G_{R_B}; nothing in `hyp:hist-exact-vfe-lift` gives R_B a G-action or makes the lift equivariant, and the chapter lists equivariant correlated lifts as open (`open:hist-parity-lift-scope`). L3-02: `prop:ig-fisher-characterizations` cites Bauer, Bruveris, and Michor for uniqueness on smooth positive densities; their theorem is on Prob(M), and on Dens⁺(M) there is a two-coefficient family (counterexample G^FR + (∫α)(∫β)), so "uniqueness up to a constant" is false as worded. No downstream result uses either uniqueness claim.

**9. Numerically vacuous constants are stated without their scale.** L6-02: the seeded lower bound of `prop:phil-seeded-contrast-formation` is about 1e-30 at the best mean, while a Monte Carlo of the frozen-row kinetics gives a formation probability of 3 to 10%. ## 7. Implementation guidance for VFE_4.0

This section collects the lenses' implementation notes. The detailed versions, with formulas and measured residuals, are section 4 of each lens report; L11 section 4 has the full map from theory objects to MultiAgentELBO modules.

What MultiAgentELBO already gets right. The part of the single-point theory it implements matches the manuscript: the latent-link collective free energy with per-receiver comparison records equals `latent_links.relative_free_energy` plus log Z_S to 8.2e-13, and the link-sector formulas (BLR step, anchor quadratic, delta current, law-level energy and gradient, expected loop defects) hold. The `latent_links`, `latent_natural`, `latent_prior`, `latent_coarse`, and `network` kernels are reusable nearly verbatim. Absent from the code are the tower VFE, the structural channel with a random mark, membership kernels, the event laws, the composite potential, the anchor and loop-coefficient M-steps, any Z_S estimator, and any declared hierarchy.

Conventions fixed by the author. Links in the two directions of a mutual pair are independent, so VFE_4.0 stores one link (or generator) per ordered source copy j→i, traverses a copy backwards with the inverse (−ω in the chart), and treats every mutual pair as a 2-cycle with holonomy Θ_ij Θ_ji whose loop defect enters the generator prior. Because MultiAgentELBO's latent-link lane uses Θ_ji = Θ_ij⁻¹, its kernels can be reused for per-copy formulas but not for its pair bookkeeping, and its results are not directly comparable. Agent-level self edges carry Θ_ii = I. Coarse loop copies carry internal holonomy, so VFE_4.0 still needs a loop-copy cost branch with residual covariance (I − M)Σ_i(I − M)^⊤ (the product-matter formula gives 4.009 against the exact 0.703 in L11's test), and the exact receiver update needs its self-edge case.

Decisions the theory still leaves to the implementer, each of which should be an explicit, documented configuration choice. The base vertex of each declared loop, including whether a 2-cycle enters once per endpoint. Which attention row is computed (comment 6). The multiscale reading: an adaptive tower gives diagnostics only, and parents with their own inference need a declared hierarchy, starting from the g10 star (comment 2). The coupling selector and the fast/slow split with its rate ratio, which are declarations and change the selected orbit. The configuration metric, the partition (no selector exists, `open:cg-natural-selector`), spanning trees under nonflat links, the parent belief map, the retained projection, and the interface rank. Base vertices for declared loops, since the loop defect depends on the rotation of the walk (0.159 between rotations in L2's test). The factor-½ convention when c10 cut formulas are reused (L8-1).

Update schedule. The descent guarantees cover sequential (Gauss–Seidel) block updates, damped kernel mixtures, and continuous flows only. Simultaneous exact mean-field updates fail, which L1, L11, and L4 found independently: one three-block Gaussian with pairwise correlation 0.6 rose from 0.46 to 9.0 in ten Jacobi sweeps, and another case went from 0.43 to 1.84 in six. A GPU implementation should use a chromatic schedule over independent sets of the record hypergraph, which is a sequential sweep in disguise, and should accept any other simultaneous step only through a line search on the collective free energy. Damping in mean (moment) parameters inherits the descent inequality and damping in natural parameters does not. The replicator at fixed energies has the closed form β(t) ∝ β_0^{e^{−rt}} β*^{1−e^{−rt}} (L4, 5.4e-14, not in the manuscript), so the exact row update is the multiplicative β ← β^{1−h}(β*)^h with h = 1 − e^{−rΔt}; explicit Euler on the replicator can leave the simplex. Explicit natural-gradient steps descend only for Δt < 2/c_G, so either estimate c_G (a few Lanczos steps) or backtrack, and enforce the SPD condition of the BLR precision update by step reduction, since the expected Hessian can be indefinite at chart folds and under attention curvature.

Closed form against sampling. Closed form at O(n³): the collective energy form KL(Q ‖ P) + E_Q H_o for Gaussian factors and records (compute this form; do not sum local free energies, which count each record once per incident agent, and do not substitute block-marginal entropies, which overstate the bound by the total correlation); the Gaussian evidence in information form, which Supplement G never displays and L8 derived and checked to 3.6e-15; the transported KL and its gradient; Schur, Kron, and Galerkin coarse maps; Fisher metrics and natural gradients on Gaussian fibers (the covariance block is half the affine-invariant metric, and vech storage must double off-diagonal covector entries); the Fréchet derivative of exp as the upper-right block of exp([[B, T], [0, B]]). Sampling or quadrature: expected loop defects and record costs at latent links (E[e^ω] has no closed form for noncommuting generators), Z_S and every tilted-prior expectation (thermodynamic integration over the loop coefficients or annealed importance sampling from the Gaussian base; Z_S cancels in inference at a fixed prior and is needed only to learn anchors or loop coefficients or to report the absolute value of the bound), Bayes reversal kernels outside finite and linear-Gaussian models, and the law-level formation statistics, whose rows are McKean–Vlasov and need an ensemble. Link learning needs a prior, because the covariance-optimized link term approaches its supremum without attaining it.

Numerical hazards, measured against high-precision references by L8. Evaluate the transported KL in pullback form with a Cholesky factor of Σ_j and an analytic Tr ω, never by forming (ΩΣΩ^⊤)⁻¹ or det(expm(ω)); at cond(ΩΣΩ^⊤) = 2.1e10 the error is 5.7e-13 against 9.6e-7. Near-zero KL loses accuracy in the trace form (relative error 5e-3 at KL ≈ 2.4e-14); use the eigenvalue form where attention depends on small KL differences. Factor hidden blocks by Cholesky in Schur and Kron elimination, and give every closure or no-new-anchor test a tolerance proportional to scale × cond × machine epsilon, since the theory supplies none. Work in information form with Cholesky log-determinants. Rank thresholds for quotient Gaussians change the law and should be declared parameters. Keep generator laws inside the principal-logarithm domain (prior widths below about π/√K per entry) and monitor the imaginary spectral gaps of each ω_e; do not re-chart silently to the principal logarithm, since that changes the prior. Normalize rows in log space (at T = ε⁸ the Gibbs weights underflow), carry infinite record energies as +∞ rather than NaN, and accumulate Fisher clocks from the quadratic form or in float64. With GL⁺(K) links that have a density, the holonomy-fixed parent family is empty almost surely, so that score is +∞ unless links are restricted to a group conjugate into O(K). The four-cycle formation regime (T = ε⁸, ε ≤ T^{7/2}) and the finite-depth formation regime cannot be simulated directly; numerical formation studies need the exact sheet dynamics rather than Euler steps, and common random numbers for learned-versus-frozen comparisons.

Build order (from L11, consistent with the other lenses, adjusted to the author conventions). Stage 0: a sparse, GPU-capable graph data model with edge copies in reversal pairs, independent copies for the two directions of a mutual pair, identity-linked agent self edges, loop copies for coarse depth, and rows as subsets of incoming copies; tests for the reversal involution, holonomy ordering (including the 2-cycle holonomy Θ_ij Θ_ji), and passive-gauge invariance. In stage 3 the 2-cycles join the loop family, with a test that a large loop coefficient drives Θ_ji toward Θ_ij⁻¹. VFE_3.0's frame-aligned transports exp(φ_i) exp(−φ_j) are the special case with trivial holonomy. Stage 1: the held-link collective free energy with product Gaussian matter and per-receiver rows, with the loop-copy branch; tests against quadrature at N = 3, the local–global potential identity, and the sender terms of one-way rows. Stage 2: dynamics (Fisher BLR for matter, the multiplicative replicator for rows, a chromatic schedule, line search for simultaneous steps), with the Jacobi counterexample as a regression test. Stage 3: latent generators, reusing `latent_links`, `latent_natural`, and `latent_prior`, adding a Z_S estimator, the anchor M-step, and the chart-domain monitor; test the held-link limit as generator precision grows. Stage 4: the coarse step with hard partitions (rooted transports, dressed marks, event pushforwards with the loop term, Schur parents), with F_tower = F_0 under the adaptive reading as a test. Stage 5: the declared hierarchy from the g10 star, then the law-level composite potential as a declared (not in-bound) objective. Formation statistics and multi-head label models come after.
