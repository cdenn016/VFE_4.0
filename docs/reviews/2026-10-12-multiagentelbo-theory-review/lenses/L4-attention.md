## 2. Findings

### L4-1: The transformer correspondence table derives its rows in two different generative models
**Location:** `c06_attention.tex:857-874` (`tab:obs-transformer-correspondence`), rows "Query and key projections", "Softmax row", "Temperature", and "Value and output maps, residual connection"; `cor:obs-selective-softmax` (`c06:718-754`); `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` (`c06:781-849`).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The query/key, softmax, and temperature rows cite `cor:obs-selective-softmax`, a law-point-level model whose energies are role divergences between law points and whose row is the posterior row `eq:obs-attention-posterior` with the key covariance as metric. The value/residual row cites `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update`, a sample-level comparison-record model in which, by part (b) of that proposition, the weights are not that posterior row. So no single model realizes all rows marked "derived". The comparison-record model on its own already supplies the dot product: with shared query map and precision, its exact weights are `w_ij = softmax_j(log pi_ij + (S^Q mu_i)^T (Sigma^pred)^{-1} S^K k_j - (1/2)|S^K k_j|^2_{(Sigma^pred)^{-1}})`, a dot-product logit plus a key-norm bias with metric `(Sigma^pred)^{-1} = (tau W^{-1} + S^Q Sigma_i S^{Q T})^{-1}`. That metric is query-side and folds the receiver uncertainty into the temperature (a02 [2]: residual 1.1e-16).
The value row also holds only for one receiver conditioned on exactly known source states (`y_{-i}` fixes `k_j`). A layer that updates every receiver in parallel from source means is a different object: the mean-field (CAVI) update over `q(k_i) q(J_i) prod q(k_j)` has exactly the transformer-shaped mean `Sigma^+ Sigma^{-1} mu_i + sum_j b_ij L^val_j mu_j` (a02 [3]: 2.6e-15), but its row `b_ij` is the averaged-energy softmax with metric `W/tau` and source-variance penalties, which differs from both `w_ij` and the plug-in `Sigma^pred` row (a02 [3], one random instance: exact row with sources integrated (0.623, 0.026, 0.090, 0.261), plug-in `Sigma^pred` row (0.672, 0.034, 0.022, 0.273), CAVI fixed point (0, 0, 0, 1)).
**Fix:** Either cite one model for the whole layer (the comparison-record model, adding its dot-product form with metric `(Sigma^pred)^{-1}` and effective temperature matrix `tau W^{-1} + S^Q Sigma_i S^{Q T}`), or mark each row with the model it is derived in; in the value row, state that it is the single-receiver Gibbs conditional with known sources, distinct from the mean-field parallel update.
**Falsifies:** A single declared generative model in the manuscript whose posterior yields both the row `eq:obs-attention-posterior` with key-covariance metric and the value map `L^val` of `eq:obs-selective-receiver-update`.
**Status:** table rows tagged "derived"; each row is derived in its own model, so the table as a whole is mildly inflated.

### L4-2: The multi-head row understates what is derived: the exact multi-head receiver update has the operator form
**Location:** `c06:870` (table row "Multiple heads": "no label-level construction ... absent"); `c06:23-27` (NOT-CLAIMED: "that declared per-head rows follow from a label model"); `def:obs-attention-label-model` (`c06:41-50`, rows `(i,h)`); `prop:obs-selective-head-coupling` (`c06:876-891`); `open:obs-multihead-attention` (`c06:925-964`).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** `def:obs-attention-label-model` already admits agent-head rows `(i,h)`, and `prop:obs-selective-head-coupling` builds two heads with exclusive labels and comparison records, so a label-level multi-head construction exists at one scale. Extending the computation of `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` to H heads with records `N(S^Q_h k_i - S^K_{h,j} k_j, tau_h W_h^{-1})`, each joint-label component of `k_i` has mean `Sigma^+ (Sigma^{-1} mu_i + sum_h S_h^T Wt_h S^K_{h,j_h} k_{j_h})` with `Sigma^+ = (Sigma^{-1} + sum_h S_h^T Wt_h S_h)^{-1}`. This mean is additively separable in the head labels, so the exact posterior mean depends on the coupled joint label table only through its per-head marginals: `E[k_i | y_{-i}, o] = Sigma^+ Sigma^{-1} mu_i + sum_h sum_j beta^{(h)}_{ij} Sigma^+ S_h^T Wt_h S^K_{h,j} k_j`, with `beta^{(h)}` the exact marginals of the non-factorizing joint posterior. That is the declared operator `eq:obs-selective-attention-residual`, with value maps fixed as in the one-head case and a shared precision-weighted residual.
a05: exact mean (0.9422, 1.594, -1.0032) against Monte Carlo (0.9417, 1.591, -1.002); the joint table does not factor (max deviation from the product of marginals 0.013), yet the operator form with exact marginals matches the exact mean to 4.4e-16. The per-head rows are not independent per-head softmaxes: head 1 single-head row (0.110, 0.458, 0.432) against coupled marginal (0.001, 0.537, 0.462).
**Fix:** Change the table row to "per-head rows derived at one scale as marginals of the coupled head-label posterior; the exact multi-head receiver update has the operator form; recursion with bounded head count open (`open:obs-multihead-attention`)", state and prove the H-head update as a part of `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update`, and narrow the NOT-CLAIMED sentence to the coarse-graining recursion.
**Falsifies:** A comparison-record model with two heads sharing one Gaussian receiver in which the exact posterior mean differs from the operator form with exact head marginals. The separability argument excludes this whenever component means are affine and additive in the head labels and the receiver precision is label independent.
**Status:** table "absent" and NOT-CLAIMED are understated (a deflation, not an inflation), but "no label-level construction" is false as written given `def:obs-attention-label-model` and `prop:obs-selective-head-coupling`.

### L4-3: The exact receiver update is silent on the self edge, where its formula fails and the self component acts as an attention sink
**Location:** `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` (`c06:781-849`); random-source clause of `prop:obs-attention-constant-row-gap` (`c06:201-207`); the label model admits self edges (`c06:49-50`, `cor:obs-attention-self-edge`), and supports are "complete unless masked" (`c01:170`).
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The proposition conditions on `y_{-i}`, which "fixes the source states `k_j`". For an included self copy `e: i -> i` the source state is `k_i` itself, which is not fixed, so the shared formulas for `w_ij`, `Sigma^+`, and `L^val` do not apply. With the identity twist (`S^K_ii = S^Q_i`) the self record is uninformative about `k_i`: its weight is `pi_ii N(0; 0, Wt^{-1})`, a factor `det(Sigma^pred Wt)^{1/2} = det(I + Wt S^Q Sigma_i S^{Q T})^{1/2}` above what the naive formula gives, and its component leaves the mean at `mu_i` with no `Sigma^+` shrinkage. a04 (K = d = 2, uniform prior over self, j1, j2, 600000 Monte Carlo draws): Monte Carlo weights (0.893, 0.106, 0.001), exact closed form with the self component (0.8928, 0.1058, 0.0014), naive application of the proposition with `k_i` replaced by `mu_i` (0.561, 0.434, 0.006); means (-0.706, -0.236), (-0.704, -0.235), (-1.484, -0.296); sink factor 6.53.
The random-source clause of `prop:obs-attention-constant-row-gap` likewise needs `Y_i` uncorrelated with `Y_j`, which fails for `j = i`; there the self logit is relatively raised by `Tr(W Cov Y_i)/(2 tau)` because the common trace term is absent from the self row. This is an exact mechanism for self-concentrated attention mass, the phenomenon studied by Xiao et al. (ICLR 2024).
**Fix:** Add "no self copy, `j` different from `i`" to the hypotheses of `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` and of the random-source clause, or add the self component: weight `pi_ii N(0; (S^Q - S^K_ii) mu_i, Wt^{-1} + (S^Q - S^K_ii) Sigma_i (S^Q - S^K_ii)^T)`, component precision `Sigma^{-1} + (S^Q - S^K_ii)^T Wt (S^Q - S^K_ii)`, and, in the identity-twist case, residual weight `w_ii I + (1 - w_ii) Sigma^+ Sigma^{-1}`.
**Falsifies:** A reading of the hypotheses under which `y_{-i}` fixes the self source, or a Monte Carlo check in which the naive formula matches the exact posterior with a self edge included.
**Status:** ESTABLISHED; correct for source sets without a self copy, incomplete for the admitted self-edge case.

### L4-4: The attention label model is typed at the sample level but used at the law-point and configuration levels
**Location:** `def:obs-attention-label-model` (`c06:58-61`: energies are "gauge-invariant functions of sample-level latent states"); `c03_probability.tex:460-468` ("The source energies of `eq:obs-attention-likelihood` and every record kernel are sample-level", followed by "the formation model ... is configuration-level, with records that read the configuration"); `cor:obs-selective-softmax`(a) (`c06:719-737`); `c13_formation.tex:1374` ("Each receiver carries one source label of `def:obs-attention-label-model`") with configuration-level costs `D_e(x) = (1/2)|a_e x|^2` (`c13:1363-1369`), and `c13:276-293`.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** `cor:obs-selective-softmax`(a) applies `eq:obs-attention-posterior` with `D_ij(y) = d_ij`, a KL divergence between law points, and c13 applies the label model with energies that read the configuration `x = (k_bar, m_bar, omega)`. Both lie outside the definition as written, and c03 states the restriction to the sample level explicitly. Bayes formula is level-agnostic, so every identity survives (the c13 full row `eq:rg-formation-full-source-row` is the product of `eq:obs-attention-posterior` rows with `D_ij/tau_i = delta chi(...)`, and the gating potential `V_pi` of `prop:rg-gated-depth-one`(b) is `T sum_i R_i^P` of `prop:obs-attention-reduced-hessian`), but the typing apparatus (`req:gen-typing-prohibition`, `prop:prob-law-point-energy`) exists to keep exactly this distinction, and the definition that the downstream chapters cite does not cover their use.
**Fix:** Restate `def:obs-attention-label-model` with energies that are measurable functions of the latent state at a named description level, and change the c03 sentence to "are functions of the latent state at the level of the declared model".
**Falsifies:** A reading of `def:prob-description-levels` under which law-point and configuration coordinates count as "sample-level latent states"; `c03:460-468` excludes this reading as written.
**Status:** definition (untagged); the dependent results are ESTABLISHED and unaffected in substance.

### L4-5: Copy-dependent comparison spaces in part (d) of the receiver update are ill-typed
**Location:** `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update`(d), `c06:844-848`.
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** Part (d) allows "copy-dependent ... comparison spaces" and gives `w_ij` proportional to `pi_ij N(0; r_ij, Sigma^pred_ij)`. The record `o_i` must take values in one record space with one reference measure for Bayes formula to compare labels. If the copies have distinct comparison spaces, either the record space is their disjoint union, in which case `o_i` reveals the label and the posterior is a point mass, or a common identification and reference measure must be declared. With unequal dimensions the densities `N(0; r_ij, Sigma^pred_ij)` are taken against Lebesgue measures of different dimension, and their ratio depends on the units chosen in each space.
**Fix:** Restrict (d) to a common comparison space `H_i^cmp` with copy-dependent query maps and precisions, or require a declared common record space and reference measure.
**Falsifies:** A declared record space for copy-dependent `H_e` in which the stated weights are a posterior.
**Status:** ESTABLISHED; clause (d) is fine for maps and precisions, ill-posed for spaces.

### L4-6: The correspondence table has no positional-encoding row, and its twist sentence holds only for absolute positional encodings
**Location:** `c06:865` (row "One key map shared by all edges": "a nonidentity twist, including nontrivial link holonomy, has no counterpart in that architecture"); the table has no position row.
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** Rotary position embedding gives `q_m^T k_n = x_m^T W_q R_{Theta,n-m} W_k x_n` with `R_{Theta,n-m} = R_{Theta,m}^T R_{Theta,n}` (Su et al. 2021, arXiv 2104.09864, Sec. 3.2.2, Eq. 16; checked against the arXiv HTML), an edge-dependent nonidentity twist `R_i^{-1} R_j` in reference coordinates with trivial holonomy (a coboundary). ALiBi (Press, Smith and Lewis, ICLR 2022) is an additive log-prior bias, which in this framework is `log pi_ij`. The sentence is correct for the 2017 architecture with absolute sinusoidal encodings added to the embedding, which is the architecture the table names, but a reader will take "transformer" to include the RoPE and ALiBi variants.
**Fix:** Add a row "Positional encoding: ALiBi is a structural log-prior `log pi_ij` (derived, `prop:obs-attention-elbo`); RoPE is a flat edge twist, equivalently position-dependent frames (declared); nontrivial holonomy has no counterpart", and qualify "that architecture" as the absolute-encoding transformer.
**Falsifies:** A RoPE logit that cannot be written as `(S^Q_ref z_i)^T (twist) (S^K_ref z_j)` with a coboundary twist.
**Status:** table row tagged "derived"; correct for its named reference, incomplete as a correspondence.

## 4. Implementation notes for VFE_4.0

Every attention row the theory defines is a softmax of `log pi_ij - E_ij / tau_i` over the support `N_i`, so the primitive an implementation needs is a support-restricted log-sum-exp. Masks are zero prior weight, not a negative infinity added after a softmax; since `pi_i` is a probability, `N_i` is nonempty by construction and no row can be fully masked, but an agent with no incoming copies carries no row and must be dropped from the set of receiving rows rather than given an empty one. The theory supplies three distinct rows, and an implementation must name which one it computes: the generative posterior row `beta^P(y)` at a latent configuration (closed form given energies); the constant recognition row of `prop:obs-attention-elbo`(b), which needs expected energies `E_Q D_ij` (closed form for quadratic and Gaussian-KL energies, where the source covariance enters as the trace penalty of `prop:obs-attention-constant-row-gap`, and sampling otherwise); and, in the comparison-record model, the predictive-covariance row `w_ij` of `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update`, which is a dot product with metric `(tau W^{-1} + S^Q Sigma_i S^{Q T})^{-1}` (L4-1). The temperature enters only as `D / tau`, so `tau_i` and the energy scale are not separately identifiable from one row, and the inverse-square-root key-width scaling is a declared choice.

The exact receiver update of `prop:obs-selective-receiver-update` is closed form for one receiver with known sources and must not be applied to a self copy in the source set (L4-3); with a self copy the self component has its own weight and leaves the mean unshrunk, an exact attention sink of size `det(I + Wt S^Q Sigma_i S^{Q T})^{1/2}`. A parallel layer update of all agents is under-specified by the text: the Gibbs-conditional update conditions on sampled source states, while the mean-field (CAVI) update over `q(k_i) q(J_i) prod q(k_j)` gives the transformer-shaped mean with plug-in source means and an averaged-energy row. These give different rows on the same data (L4-1), and the schedule (parallel, sequential, damped) is not fixed by any result in scope. With several heads per receiver the exact update is still closed form and has the multi-head operator form, but the per-head rows are marginals of a joint label table of size `prod_h |N_i|`, exponential in the head count; a factorized (mean-field) head-label approximation is the tractable choice and costs the total-correlation term of `eq:obs-attention-correlated-ledger` (L4-2). Exact label integration for a whole network is a mixture over `prod_i |N_i|` assignments (as in the c13 full row `eq:rg-formation-full-source-row`), so any exact algorithm beyond tiny graphs needs label sampling, as the c13 kinetics do, or a factorized recognition law.

For row dynamics, the replicator flow of `cor:obs-attention-replicator` at fixed averaged energies has the closed form `beta(t)` proportional to `beta_0^{exp(-r t)} beta*^{1 - exp(-r t)}` (a06, 5.4e-14), so the row relaxation rate `r_i` is the exponential rate of approach along the exponential-family geodesic, and the exact discretization is the multiplicative update `beta <- beta^{1-h} (beta*)^{h}`, renormalized, with `h = 1 - exp(-r dt)`; explicit Euler on the replicator can leave the simplex and should not be used. When the energies move, the joint rate `eq:obs-attention-joint-rate` has an unsigned term, so descent of the row free energy under simultaneous belief and row updates needs a schedule argument that the text does not give. The label-integrated potential `R_i` has the indefinite Hessian of `prop:obs-attention-reduced-hessian`, which the witness makes negative at a symmetric point, so Newton or Gauss-Newton steps on `R_i` need a positive-definite surrogate (the frozen-weight curvature plus the prior) or damping. Rows should be computed in log space with the support-restricted log-sum-exp, energies of different description levels must not be mixed in one row (L4-4), and the receiver update should be formed through the Woodbury form `Sigma_i S^{Q T} (Sigma^pred)^{-1}`, which stays stable as `tau -> 0`, where `Wt` diverges. The communication layer (`def:obs-selective-interface`, `def:geo-selective-matrix-link`) is declared data: no result in scope selects encoder, transmission, decoder, or value-extraction maps, apart from the value map that the comparison-record model fixes, so an implementation that learns them must declare the generative parameterization and the metric, as `def:obs-selective-interface-learning` requires.
