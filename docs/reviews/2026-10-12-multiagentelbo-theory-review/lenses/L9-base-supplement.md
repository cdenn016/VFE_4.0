## 2. Findings

### L9-1: The native free model is a 2D Ising lattice gas in a field; the B = 1 C^4 and Fisher statements are special cases of classical analyticity, and the 12t < 1 domain is not needed for them
**Location:** b09_lattice_thermo_fisher.tex:49-68 (hyp:rg-thermo-native-source-box), :109-133 (thm:rg-thermo-native-cfour-pressure), :135-162 (prop:rg-thermo-native-ctwo-metric), :186 (eq:rg-thermo-native-identity-density); b08_lattice_pressure.tex:208-219 (def:rg-pressure-native); antecedent paragraph b07_lattice_rg.tex:43-75.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Expanding both products of the native raw factor and averaging over normalized counting, a monomial survives exactly when its edge set is the mod-2 boundary of its plaquette set, so
Z_L(b,t) = sum over plaquette sets S of t^{|S|} b^{|boundary_2 S|}.
In plaquette spins sigma_p = 2 n_p - 1 this is a nearest-neighbour Ising model on the (L-1)x(L-1) plaquette grid with coupling K = (1/2) log(1/b) > 0 (ferromagnetic for 0 < b < 1), bulk field H = (1/2) log t < 0 (nonzero for every 0 < t < 1), and a boundary field of the same sign. Script s2_native_dual_ising.py: brute-force enumeration against the dual sum at L = 2 (relative difference 1.3e-15, 2.9e-15) and L = 3 (3.1e-12, 1.4e-12, float summation of 2^18 terms); the row transfer matrix of width L-1 reproduces the dual sum (L = 3: 7.229423646222864e-3 against 7.229423646222859e-3); the finite identity N^{-1} I = Hess A_L holds at L = 3 against exhaustive enumeration to 5.7e-9 (central-difference level) on entries of size 1.46. By Lee and Yang (Phys. Rev. 87, 1952) the zeros in the field variable lie on Re H = 0, so a real nonzero H is not a limit point of zeros, and by Lebowitz and Penrose (Commun. Math. Phys. 11 (1968) 99-124, DOI 10.1007/BF01645899) the infinite-volume free energy is then analytic jointly in the temperature and field variables. Composing with the analytic map (b,t) -> (K,H), the native raw pressure p, hence A = p + 2 log cosh h + log cosh j and H = Hess A, is real-analytic on all of 0 < b < 1, 0 < t < 1, not only C^4 on 0 < j < atanh(1/12). I checked the Lebowitz-Penrose statement only through the publisher abstract and a secondary summary, not the full text. The supplement cites Osterwalder-Seiler for small-t analyticity in general dimension and Fradkin-Shenker for the phase diagram, but not this two-dimensional reduction; van Enter, Fernandez and Sokal (1993, Sec. 6.1.1, checked against the text of arXiv:hep-lat/9210032) already place the high-temperature RG results where analyticity of the free energy can be proven.
**Fix:** State the duality identity after def:rg-pressure-native (one line of proof), cite Lee-Yang and Lebowitz-Penrose for analyticity of p on the open unit square, present thm:rg-thermo-native-cfour-pressure and the B = 1 case of prop:rg-thermo-native-ctwo-metric as reproved special cases on the admitted box, and say explicitly that the 12t < 1 restriction is needed only for the B >= 2 constructions (thm:rg-spatial-constrained-specification and the push densities), not for the native pressure or its Hessian.
**Falsifies:** A volume L at which the native partition function differs from the dual sum (the identity is algebraic, so one such L refutes it), or a full-text reading of Lebowitz-Penrose showing that their analyticity theorem requires a hypothesis the dual model fails.
**Status:** ESTABLISHED; correct as stated and not inflated; the gap is a missing reduction and citation that also misstates the parameter domain an implementer needs.

### L9-3: The raw pressure is not convex in the natural coordinates; only A is, and Hess p is not a metric
**Location:** b09_lattice_thermo_fisher.tex:128-131 and 143-145; bc_thermo_proofs.tex:604-613 (prop:rg-thermo-native-boundary-score-counterexample).
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** The manuscript states correctly that Hess p differs from H by diag(2 sech^2 h, sech^2 j) and that the second h-derivative of p vanishes at j = 0. It does not say that p fails to be convex. At j = 0 the bulk plaquette variance gives H_jj = 1 + 4 b^6 - 5 b^8 per cell (four edge-sharing neighbours, covariance b^6 - b^8 each), so the second j-derivative of p is 4 b^6 - 5 b^8 < 0 for b^2 > 4/5. Script s4_raw_pressure_nonconvex.py (exact dual transfer, one-sided differences at j = 0): b = 0.95 gives -0.321 (L = 8) and -0.336 (L = 11) against the bulk value -0.377; b = 0.9 gives -0.066 and -0.057 against -0.027; b = 0.5 gives +0.028 and +0.032 against +0.043 (finite-L and one-sided truncation account for the gaps; signs agree). No statement in Supplement B claims convexity of p (grep for "convex" in b07-b09, bb, bc returns only the erasure citation), so this is a missing caution, not an error.
**Fix:** Add one sentence after eq:rg-thermo-native-fisher-pressure: the raw pressure is not convex (the j = 0 example), and only the natural density A has a positive-semidefinite Hessian equal to the Fisher density.
**Falsifies:** An error in the bulk variance count, or a definition of p in the text that already includes the log-cosh terms.
**Status:** ESTABLISHED statements correct; clarity only.

## 4. Implementation notes for VFE_4.0

Supplement B is deferred material: nothing in the main manuscript at c_* depends on it, so a first VFE_4.0 implementation of the single-point theory needs none of it. If base transport is implemented later, the computable objects are local connection forms A (Lie-algebra-valued one-forms on base charts, supplied data, never inferred from frames), parallel transports along declared curves, curvature dA + A^A, and holonomy around declared loops. Transport is closed form only when the connection components commute along the curve (the abelian SO(2) examples of b04 give exp(-kappa times signed area)); otherwise it is a path-ordered exponential that must be integrated with a group-valued update (Magnus or a Lie-group Runge-Kutta scheme), and the conventions must be fixed once and tested: nabla = d + A, A_new = Ad_g A - dg g^{-1} under u_new = u g^{-1}, transport dv/dt = -A(dgamma/dt) v. For Gaussian sections the covariant first jet is closed form (the location part is d mu plus the represented action of A on mu with the sign convention of def:pb-connection-difference, the covariance part is the corresponding congruence derivative), and the Fisher pullback h_s is the Gaussian Fisher form of that jet; it is a semimetric whose rank equals the rank of the jet, so any inverse needs the constant-rank hypothesis of thm:pb-pullback-rank-quotient.

The projected (quotient) connection A^quot = (F A - dF) F^dagger with F^dagger = F^T (F F^T)^{-1} is closed form but conditioned like (F F^T)^{-1}, which blows up at a rank drop; the code must assert constant rank. Staged coarse-graining must propagate the induced quotient metric (F_1 F_1^T)^{-1} into the second minimum-norm lift; resetting the intermediate metric to the identity is a different construction and gives a connection that is not gauge equivalent to the direct one (verified determinants 15625/61731 against 3645/103823). The data the theory leaves to the implementer, with no canonical choice, are the connections themselves, the base measure and channel weights w_b, w_m (with the cross-scale conditions X1 and X2 that make weighted comparisons monotone), design weights, comparison isomorphisms J_l, identification kernels I_b, the reference trajectory of an action beta, and the interpolation behind any continuous beta (prop:rg-continuous-beta-underdetermined). Each must be an explicit configuration input, and any reported exponent or beta is meaningful only relative to them.

For the lattice cluster, the native pressure and its Hessian need no Monte Carlo and no high-temperature restriction: by L9-1 the free partition function is a two-dimensional Ising lattice-gas partition function on the plaquette grid, computable exactly by a row transfer matrix of size 2^{L-1} (script s2 runs L = 13 in seconds; widths near 20 are feasible), with derivatives by exact differentiation of the transfer recursion or by complex-step. The quantity that is a metric is H = Hess A in natural coordinates (h, j); the raw pressure p is not convex (L9-3), and the conversion p = A - 2 log cosh h - log cosh j must use the finite plaquette count (L-1)^2/N at finite volume. For B >= 2 the Fisher density per coarse cell I_B is not the Hessian of any pressure; it is the expected Fisher matrix of the four-state predictive row with the complete lexicographic past fixed and the future integrated, so a computation needs a hidden-state forward recursion over strips with the fine boundary integrated. The 1D analog (script s1) shows the free-law and infinite-law versions agree per cell with an O(1/L) offset in the cumulative matrix, so increments in L, not cumulative averages, should be reported. The constructive constants (c1, c3, V_B, the basin gate B_0) are certificates, not estimators: they are loose by large factors (the ledger records a factor of about 16 for the Dobrushin boundary bound) and should not drive numerics. Fixed blocking words must be applied before any volume limit; the theory controls no simultaneous depth and volume limit.
