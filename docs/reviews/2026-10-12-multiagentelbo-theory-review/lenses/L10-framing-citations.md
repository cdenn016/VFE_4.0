## 1. Scope and verdict

Read in full: `main.tex` (153 lines, title, PDF subject, structure), `c01_program_scope.tex` (247), `c14_interpretation.tex` (433), `c15_related_work.tex` (286), `ca_open_problems.tex` (57), `ca_numerical_index.tex` (27). Read the opening (first 50 to 80 lines) and the closing summary (last 25 to 60 lines) of `c02` through `c13` and `c08b`. Targeted reads: `c02:220-250` (def:geo-agent), `c05:492-504` (open:restrict-intrinsic-criterion), `c06:820-900` (the transformer correspondence table), `c08:470-500`, `c09:510-524` (open:cg-natural-selector), `c12:150-170` and `c12:930-965` (downward coupling), `c13:540-580` (own-law parameter dynamics), `ca_parent_early.tex:1128-1142`. The verified ledger was grepped for every label and citation in scope, and its last section (rows 700-735) was read in full. Seventeen cited sources (26 locators) were checked against primary text. Overall verdict: the scope paragraphs and Chapter 14 are careful. They decline realist, physical-law, agency, and physical-time readings, and they give the program's distinctive readings explicit mathematical and empirical falsifiers, each with a negative branch. I found no unfalsifiable claim that the manuscript asserts. The defects this lens finds sit in three places. First, the program-aim paragraph of Chapter 1 asserts, as the program's identity, three theses (participatory, formation through coupled inference, parents acting back on children) that no family in the manuscript jointly realizes; Chapters 12 to 14 concede as much. Second, Chapter 14 presents as its main statement a theorem that belongs to the locus (learning of the aggregate prior) that the same chapter classifies as non-discriminating. Third, the related-work positioning omits the nearest canonical counterparts for attention-as-inference, endogenous cluster formation, Markov-blanket renormalization, and Kadanoff exactness. Several of these counterparts are already in `references.bib` but cited nowhere. The citation spot-checks found one locator wrong and one verb overstated; every other locator I checked supports its use.

## 2. Findings

### L10-01: The program aim asserts three theses that no numbered statement realizes together
**Location:** `c01_program_scope.tex:25-27` (sec:intro-program-aim); also `c01:17-18` and the physicist's summary at `c01:238-247`
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** "The program is a participatory multi-agent theory. Agents are local sections of associated bundles, collectives form through the agents' coupled inference, and parents act back on their children through normalized laws rather than through quantities that read the children's beliefs." Status from the evidence present: S (structural framing), stated in the indicative as the program's identity. The manuscript's own statements go against each clause.
(i) Participation: `c14:172-173` says "None of these separates the program from a hierarchical model with learned priors; formation could." `c14:374` says "Assignment selection alone does not establish that recursive claim."
(ii) Formation: `c01:134-136` says formation in thm:finite-depth-adaptive-formation comes from "linear relaxation of a prepared mean through a supplied hierarchy of interaction strengths; source jumps and learning are perturbations the proof controls, not the cause of formation". `c13:1566-1567` says the reduction succeeds in a regime "in which nothing adaptive decides the outcome". In the endogenous gated family the story "stops there" at depth one (`c13:1573`, prop:rg-gated-depth-one), and in prop:phil-seeded-contrast-formation the pair is seeded by the random initial assignment while learning is idle.
(iii) Parents acting back: the only proved parent-to-child coupling is prop:full-graph-downward-coupling, in a declared top-down hierarchy where the parent is an argument of the child's prior, that is, an ordinary hierarchical prior. Even there, a family-level claim needs "a proved nonzero cross-derivative in that family" (`c12:955-962`). In adaptive towers, which the formation witnesses are (`c12:16-17`), "the downward kernel is a reversal kernel, and the coupling it carries is not read as causal downward influence" (`c12:37-39`). In the formation witnesses each level learns its own parameter from its own law (`c13:551-571`, eq:rg-formation-own-law-dynamics), and no downward kernel reads the parent.
So no family has both endogenously formed parents and parents acting on their children. In Lakatos's (1978) terms, by Chapter 14's own accounting, the hard-core label "participatory" currently has no excess content over hierarchical Bayesian modeling. Stating it as the program's identity in the opening, and not as an aim, overstates what the record supports.
**Fix:** Replace `c01:25-27` with: "The program aims at a participatory multi-agent theory, in which collectives would form through the agents' coupled inference and parents would act back on their children through normalized laws. At present formation is proved under a supplied hierarchy or a seeded assignment, downward coupling only in a declared hierarchy, and no result separates the construction from a hierarchical model with learned priors (Cref ch:philosophy)."
**Falsifies:** An ESTABLISHED statement for one family in which (a) a parent forms under the kinetics without a supplied hierarchy or an initial seed, and (b) that parent's coordinates enter the children's generative kernel with a proved nonzero cross-derivative.
**Status:** Untagged "aim" prose that makes a claim; the NOT-CLAIMED scope paragraph does not cover it. Inflated relative to c12, c13, and c14.

### L10-03: "Formation could" is a low-severity discriminator, and the formation chapter cites no prior art
**Location:** `c14:172-173`; the summary at `c14:432-433`; `c13_formation.tex` (whole chapter); the settling conditions of open:rg-formation-general and open:full-graph-hierarchy
**Severity:** high

Endogenous cluster formation under attention-gated or confidence-gated relaxation is established for models that are not participatory in the manuscript's sense:

- Geshkovski, Letrouit, Polyanskiy and Rigollet, "A mathematical perspective on Transformers" (Bull. AMS 62 (2025) 427-479; arXiv:2312.10794): "clusters emerge in long time".
- Geshkovski, Koubbi, Polyanskiy and Rigollet, "Dynamic metastability in the self-attention model" (arXiv:2410.06833): exponentially long multi-cluster metastable states with a staircase energy profile, which suggests coalescence in successive stages; the abstract checked does not state the merging sequence.

The gated family of c13 (attended edges contract, unattended edges stretch) is the bounded-confidence mechanism with attention as the gate. **Falsifies:** A proof that self-attention particle dynamics and bounded-confidence dynamics fail the handoff-margin or restoring clause of open:rg-formation-general. That would make the discriminator severe as written.
**Status:** Open problem plus untagged summary. The discriminating claim is S and is asserted without a severity analysis.

### L10-05: c15's distinction from multi-agent active inference is contradicted by the Markov-blanket renormalization work it omits
**Location:** `c15:280-282` (summary); `c15:28-45`
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** `c15:280-282` says parent laws "are computed from the fine joint rather than shared, omitted, or fitted as in multi-agent active inference". Friston, Fagerholm, Zarghami, Parr, Hipolito, Magrou and Razi, "Parcels and particles: Markov blankets in the brain" (Network Neuroscience 5(1):211-251, 2021; arXiv:2007.09704), build "particles of particles" by a renormalization-group construction: the states at the next scale are computed from the fine dynamics (principal eigenstates of each particle's blanket states), and the partition is applied recursively. Palacios et al. 2020 (L10-03) simulate the formation of hierarchical blankets under variational free energy minimization. Neither is cited. KirchhoffParrPalaciosFristonKiverstein2018 and biehl2021technical are in the bib, uncited. The real distinction from Friston et al. 2021 is that their coarse states come from an approximate eigenmode reduction of the fine dynamics, with no normalized coarse law and no evidence identity, whereas thm:rg-exact-coarse-vfe pushes normalized laws forward exactly. The manuscript does not state this distinction. The other three descriptions checked out: Waade et al. 2025 (PMC11853804, Section 2.3: "The observations of the sensory agents and the actions of the active agents comprise the blanket states of the group agent", with a fitted group model of the same structure and a "non-trivial relationship"), Friston et al. federated inference (Neurosci. Biobehav. Rev. 156, 2024; PMC11139662), and Heins et al. (PNAS 121(17), 2024) at abstract level. For Heins et al., "generative models of their neighbors" and "without a group-level model" are not in the abstract, and the body was not read.
**Fix:** Replace the clause at `c15:280-282` with: "pushed forward exactly as normalized laws, whereas the renormalization of Markov blankets in Friston et al. (2021) computes coarse states from an eigenmode reduction of the fine dynamics without a coarse evidence identity, and other multi-agent active-inference work shares, omits, or fits the group-level model". Cite Friston et al. 2021 and Palacios et al. 2020 in the first section of c15.
**Falsifies:** A reading of Friston et al. 2021 in which the coarse states are not functions of fine-scale quantities. The PMC full-text summary I read says they are.
**Status:** The NOT-CLAIMED sentence of c15 covers identification, not characterization. The stated distinction is inflated.

### L10-06: "Exact coarse-graining" is Kadanoff's exact-RG condition, and the selector problems claim no criterion "is known" without engaging information-theoretic RG

(8)-(9)); the coarse free-energy decomposition adds the chain rule of relative entropy." **Falsifies:** A demonstration that latent-only evidence preservation differs in content from the Kadanoff normalization condition, for example because Kadanoff's kernel acts on the observed variables in a way that changes the identity.
**Status:** thm:rg-exact-coarse-vfe is correctly tagged ESTABLISHED. The positioning is inflated.

### L10-09: The testability settling conditions require dependence, not severity
**Location:** `c14:400-427` (open:phil-testability); `c14:378-393` (claim:physical-law-identification); `ca_open_problems.tex:54-55`
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** open:phil-testability is settled positively by a pre-registered test "together with a proof that under the declared kernel the law of the tested statistic depends on that hypothesis". Mayo's (1996) severity requirement: a pass counts as evidence only if the test had a high probability of failing were the hypothesis false. "Depends on" admits a statistic whose law moves by an arbitrarily small amount, so a pass could carry almost no weight, and the "acceptance tolerance" is not tied to an error probability under the baseline. The condition has real strengths: pre-registration ("fixed before the data", which meets Worrall's 2014 use-novelty), baselines without holonomy or formation, and an unidentifiability negative branch. Separately, the "What holds" list contains thm:cg-loop-operational-holonomy and the pairing theorem. Both are model theorems that no observation could count against, although the chapter's opening question (`c14:4-5`) asks what an observation could count against.
**Fix:** Replace "a proof that under the declared kernel the law of the tested statistic depends on that hypothesis" with "a proof that, under the declared kernel and sample size, the test rejects the baseline without link holonomy (respectively without formation) with probability at least 1 - alpha and accepts under the hypothesis with probability at least 1 - beta, with alpha and beta stated in advance". Make the same change in claim:physical-law-identification and in the table rows.
**Falsifies:** A definition, somewhere in c10 or c14, of "acceptance tolerance" as an error-probability bound. I did not find one.
**Status:** Open problems. The settling conditions are nontrivial but under-specified on severity.

## 4. Implementation notes for VFE_4.0

This lens adds no new computational objects, but it constrains what an implementation may claim and which arms it must run. An implementation that wants to test the participatory reading must run three comparisons, not one. The first is the frozen-prior arm of conj:phil-contrast-formation. The second is a hierarchical model with the same learned source-prior row but no agent-level participation structure: by L10-02, the pairing-selection theorem does not separate these two, so any gap between them is new information. The third is a non-participatory particle baseline with the same comparison energies, either self-attention particle dynamics in the sense of Geshkovski et al. or bounded-confidence gating. By L10-03, formation alone is expected in all three. A result is evidence for the program only on a statistic where the arms are predicted to differ, with error probabilities fixed before the runs (L10-09). Every such run would be the first NUMERICAL entry of the main manuscript, so it needs a named control and seed in the sense of tab:status-taxonomy.

Several choices are under-specified, and an implementer would have to invent them. Blocks and coarse channels are inputs everywhere: open:cg-natural-selector and open:restrict-intrinsic-criterion are unsettled. Any automatic partition selector is therefore an addition outside the theory. Mutual-information (RSMI) and information-bottleneck selectors are the nearest literature options, and they need a budget or coefficient that the theory does not supply. Parent dynamics after activation are open (open:rg-pointwise-parent-dynamics). An implemented parent update is an approximation and must be labeled as one, not as the "exact coarse-graining" of the title. The exact part is the pushforward of the one-time law plus the KL chain rule, which is closed form for finite and Gaussian laws. A parent that "acts back" on its children exists only in a declared top-down hierarchy (prop:full-graph-downward-coupling). An implementation that adds downward feedback in an adaptive tower is declaring a new generative model, not realizing one the theory has analyzed. For the attention rows, the variational-attention estimators of Deng et al. (2018) are the standard implementation of a recognition law over a latent source label. They can be reused as long as the gauge-typed comparison energies of c06 replace the dot-product logits.
