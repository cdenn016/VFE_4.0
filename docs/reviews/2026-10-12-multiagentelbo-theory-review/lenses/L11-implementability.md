Verdict for this lens: what MultiAgentELBO implements of the single-point theory is faithful. An independent quadrature evaluation of the collective free energy of the latent-link model with per-receiver comparison records matches `latent_links.relative_free_energy` plus log Z_S to 8.2e-13 (s1), and every link-sector formula I recomputed holds (s2). The defects are on the implementability side. The theory leaves the identity of edge copies under mutual attention undeclared, which makes the link space ill-posed for symmetric supports (L11-1). It types self edges only through the hypothesis D_ii = 0, which the coarse levels violate (L11-2). Two routing decisions an implementer must make before writing code, the energy route and the multiscale reading, are present in the text only implicitly (L11-6, L11-7). The large objects of Part II, namely the structural channel with a random mark, membership kernels, event pushforwards, the tower VFE, and the composite potential, have no implementation in MultiAgentELBO.

## 2. Findings

### L11-1: Edge-copy identity under mutual attention is circular and undeclared, so the link space is not determined by the stated data
**Location:** `c06_attention.tex:41-50` (`def:obs-attention-label-model`: "Row i carries a source label J_i valued in the incoming edge copies of i ... These supports determine the interaction multigraph"); `c01_program_scope.tex:168-171` (the multigraph "is the reversal closure of the union of these supports"); `c08b_lie_links.tex:139-165` (`def:link-exponential-chart`, link space of dimension |E_+| dim G). Code: `MultiAgentELBO/src/multiagent_elbo/realizations/gaussian/latent_calibration.py:74-75` and `coupled_learning.py:15-58` (one reversal pair per unordered pair; the second row reads it with `reverse=True`) versus `locality.py:82-98` (`independent_links`, one independent exp(sigma G_ij) per ordered pair).
**Severity:** high
**Evidence:** The label alphabet of row i is a set of copies of a multigraph that the same definition says is determined by the supports. When j is in the support of row i and i is in the support of row j, nothing fixes whether row j's copy i -> j is the reversal ē of row i's copy e (one link Θ_e, with Θ_ē = Θ_e^{-1}) or a new copy e' (a second reversal pair with an independent link). The two readings give different latent spaces: for complete support on N agents, |E_+| is N(N-1)/2 under the first and N(N-1) under the second. Under the second, every mutual pair is a closed walk (e, e') with holonomy Θ_e Θ_e', generically nontrivial, which changes the loop family available to the generator prior (`def:link-generator-prior`), the fixed sectors of c10, and the coarse loop copies of `def:full-graph-coarse-links`. The two MultiAgentELBO lanes already disagree: the latent-link lane uses one pair, the locality lane draws independent ordered maps. The 2026-10-01 review (`docs/reviews/2026-10-01-code-theory-next-phase/REPORT.md`, "Model and navigation details") recorded the code side as a typing detail; the theory side is still open.
**Fix:** Declare the copy set before the rows: the structural data list the reversal pairs of the multigraph with their multiplicities, each row selects its support among the incoming copies of that declared set, and the default for a mutually supported pair is one reversal pair read by both rows (Θ_ij = Θ_ji^{-1}); independent copies are an explicit model addition. Replace "is the reversal closure of the union of these supports" by "contains the reversal closure of the union of these supports", with the copy set as a declared datum.
**Falsifies:** A sentence in c01, c02 (`def:geo-graph-links`) or c06 that fixes which copy a mutually supported row reads, or a result showing that every statement downstream of `def:link-exponential-chart` is invariant under the choice.
**Status:** `def:obs-attention-label-model` is a definition; not inflated, but under-specified at a load-bearing point.

### L11-2: Self edges are typed only through the hypothesis D_ii = 0, which coarse loop copies violate, and a naive product-matter cost for them is wrong by a factor of almost six in the test case
**Location:** `c06_attention.tex:49-50` ("whether N_i contains the self edge is part of the model"), `c06_attention.tex:103-105` (`prop:obs-attention-elbo`(a): "an included self edge with D_ii = 0"), `c06_attention.tex:276-302` (`cor:obs-attention-self-edge`); `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:496` (loop term of `eq:full-graph-edge-pushforward`) and `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:591-617` (internal copies become loops whose divergence is KL(q_I || Hol_# q_I)). Code: `coupled_learning.py:49-51` rejects self sources; `coupled_learning.py:120-123` uses the residual covariance Sigma_i + M Sigma_j M^T.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** A self edge is an element of "the incoming edge copies of i", so it is a loop copy and carries a link Θ_e like every copy (`def:geo-graph-links`). For the comparison record its energy is ½ |(I - ρ(Θ_e)) k_i|²_W, which vanishes only when ρ(Θ_e) = I. The text never says whether depth-0 self edges carry identity links, latent generators, or no link, and `prop:obs-attention-elbo`(a) takes D_ii = 0 as a hypothesis. At depth ℓ+1 loop copies arise by construction with holonomy links and nonzero divergence, so the depth-0 convention is not preserved by the coarse step. On the code side, `CoupledModel` excludes self sources, so nothing is wrong today, but the expected-cost kernel `costs_from_maps` assumes independent receiver and source blocks. Script s3(a), K = 2: the product formula gives 4.00866, the exact loop-copy cost ½|(I-M)μ|² + ½ Tr((I-M)Σ(I-M)^T) is 0.70337, and Monte Carlo gives 0.70267. Removing the guard to admit coarse loop copies would therefore corrupt the objective silently.
**Fix:** Theory: state in `def:obs-attention-label-model` that a self edge is a loop copy with link Θ_e, that D_ii = 0 holds exactly when ρ(Θ_e) leaves the compared object unchanged (identity link at depth 0 by declaration, holonomy at coarse depth), and cite `prop:full-graph-coarse-links` for the coarse case. Code (VFE_4.0): a separate loop-copy branch with residual covariance (I-M)Σ_i(I-M)^T.
**Falsifies:** A passage typing the depth-0 self edge's link, or a proof that the depth-0 convention is preserved by `def:full-graph-coarse-links`.
**Status:** The ESTABLISHED statements are correct under their hypotheses; the gap is typing, not inflation.

### L11-3: No discrete schedule or step rule beyond sequential block updates; simultaneous exact updates can increase the collective free energy
**Location:** `c05_evidence_bound.tex:792-804` (`prop:elbo-exact-e-coordinate`(iv)) and `c05_evidence_bound.tex:834-837` ("replacing the conditionals of several correlated blocks at once is not covered"); `c08_two_timescales.tex:46-82` (`prop:obs-local-block-descent`, continuous time only); `c08b_lie_links.tex:1425-1455` (`prop:link-recognition-flow`(d)-(e): descent needs a curvature bound with an unspecified constant c_G); `g10_reciprocal_pairs.tex:347-352` (`open:obs-star-update-robustness`). Code: `coupled_learning.py:196-214`, `latent_links.py:363-380`, `latent_joint_flow.py:148-165`.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The theory guarantees monotone descent for sequential exact or damped block replacement, for the continuous natural-gradient flow, and for one BLR step whose size is bounded by a curvature constant that is not computable in the cases of interest. A 512-agent GPU implementation needs simultaneous updates. Script s3(b): product recognition of N(m*, Λ^{-1}) with Λ the 3x3 matrix of unit diagonal and off-diagonal 0.6 (eigenvalues 0.4, 0.4, 2.2); simultaneous exact mean updates (Jacobi, spectral radius 1.2) give F = 0.4312, 0.3780, 0.4568, 0.6264, 0.8906, 1.2784, 1.8394, while the sequential sweep gives 0.4312, 0.0170, 0.0045, 0.0012, and so on. The BLR precision update Λ' = (1-Δt)Λ + Δt pr E[∇² i] also leaves the SPD cone when the projected expected Hessian is indefinite, which happens at the saddles of `prop:glink-sample-record`(iv) and under the negative attention curvature of `prop:obs-attention-reduced-hessian`. MultiAgentELBO resolves both by global backtracking against a fixed-sample objective, which its own docstrings state is not a population descent guarantee.
**Fix:** Add to c08 a remark that (a) updating an independent set of the record hypergraph in parallel is a sequential sweep, so chromatic schedules inherit `eq:obs-damped-block-update`; (b) any other simultaneous step is an explicit Euler step of `eq:obs-local-natural-gradient` and decreases F only under a step bound or an accepted line search in the sense of `def:elbo-generalized-m-acceptance`; and (c) the SPD condition of `eq:link-blr` is enforced by step reduction.
**Falsifies:** A theorem in c05 or c08 covering simultaneous block updates for product families without a step condition, which s3(b) excludes.
**Status:** Correct as scoped; the guarantee an implementation needs is absent rather than misstated.

### L11-4: The generator-prior normalizer and tilted-prior expectations are needed by the bound and by the stated M-coordinates, and no estimator is specified
**Location:** `c08b_lie_links.tex:465-477` (`prop:link-generator-prior`, only the bounds exp(-E S) <= Z_S <= 1); `c08b_lie_links.tex:1470-1523` (`prop:link-anchor-m-coordinate`: the maximizer is the anchor at which the tilted prior mean equals the recognition mean; with a loop term the joint anchor-covariance maximizer need not exist); `c08b_lie_links.tex:1376-1379` (`eq:link-gaussian-free-energy` contains log Z_S). Code: `latent_links.py:3-4` and `:199-210` ("All energies omit only the fixed log Z_S"); `latent_prior.py:184-286` (independence Metropolis for the tilted prior); a repository-wide search finds no anchor or loop-coefficient M-step and no Z_S estimator.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** With loop coefficients c_p > 0 the following are not closed form: the value of the bound, which needs log Z_S; the anchor M-coordinate, which needs E_{P_α}[ω] as a function of α, a root-finding problem with an expectation under a non-Gaussian law inside it; a learned loop coefficient, which needs ∂ log Z_S/∂c_p = -E_P[Q_p]; and a learned prior covariance. The only closed forms are `eq:link-anchor-quadratic` for quadratic actions (verified, s2(b), residual 2.5e-16) and the abelian translation instance. MultiAgentELBO evaluates only F - log Z_S and so cannot compare generative parameters, as its docstring states.
**Fix:** Add an implementation remark to c08b: log Z_S by thermodynamic integration along the loop coefficients, log Z_S(c) = -∫_0^1 Σ_p c_p E_{P_{tc}}[Q_p] dt, or by annealed importance sampling from the Gaussian base; tilted-prior expectations by the Metropolis sampler whose proposal is the base law; the anchor update as a stochastic-approximation root finder on `eq:link-anchor-gradient`.
**Falsifies:** A closed form for Z_S or E_{P_α}[ω] for nonabelian loop actions, or a statement that these quantities are never needed.
**Status:** The ESTABLISHED propositions are correct; the gap is algorithmic.

### L11-5: No domain condition for the exponential chart; the prior width must scale like 1/sqrt(K) to keep the generator law on the injective principal domain
**Location:** `c08b_lie_links.tex:139-165` and `:215-238` (chart image only), `c08b_lie_links.tex:438-463` (`def:link-generator-prior`, arbitrary positive covariance), `g11_latent_links.tex:231-252` (`prop:glink-sample-record`(iii)-(iv): the Fréchet derivative is singular where eigenvalue differences lie in 2πiZ minus zero, and a stationary point there can be a saddle). A search of c08b for injectivity of exp, branches, or multimodality finds only injectivity of dρ.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** The chart discussion treats surjectivity (which twists are reached) but not injectivity. Distinct generators give the same link once eigenvalue imaginary parts leave (-π, π), the record current cannot move a generator across points where L(ω, ·) is singular, and a Gaussian recognition law R on ω (`prop:link-gaussian-recognition-vfe`, `prop:link-recognition-flow`) then represents one branch of a posterior that is multimodal by construction. Script s4 measures the prior mass outside the principal domain under N(0, s² I) on gl(K) in Frobenius coordinates: for K = 2 it is 5.0e-6, 1.6e-3, 1.2e-2 at s = 1, 1.5, 2; for K = 8 it is 2.8e-2, 4.5e-1, 8.0e-1. The exit scales with s sqrt(K), as the circular law predicts. At the fiber dimensions VFE_4.0 targets, unconstrained default widths would put a large fraction of prior mass where Gaussian generator recognition is not an adequate family.
**Fix:** Add a standing hypothesis or remark to c08b: the base covariance is chosen so that the prior mass outside the principal-logarithm domain is below a declared tolerance (operationally, entry scale below a constant times π/sqrt(K)), recognition is monitored for the same condition, and the statements of the chapter about Gaussian generator recognition are read on that domain.
**Falsifies:** A proof that the generator posterior is unimodal for every base covariance, or that the free energy restricted to Gaussian R is insensitive to branch structure.
**Status:** No stated result is false; the definition admits parameter regimes in which the stated recognition family is inadequate, and the text does not say so.

### L11-6: The scale-local row free energies of c12 are law-level and have no in-bound realization; the only in-bound energy route is the sample-level comparison record
**Location:** `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:262-296` (`def:full-graph-transported-divergences`, D_e = KL(q_i || Θ_# q_j) as law-point energies), `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:330-354` (`def:full-graph-composite-potential`, "carries no evidence interpretation"), `c06_attention.tex:718-754` (`cor:obs-selective-softmax`(a): softmax over law-point divergences is the posterior row at point-mass law-point recognition, whose VFE is infinite; `open:prob-law-point-prior`). Code: the sample-level route in `latent_links.py:100-110` and `coupled_learning.py:109-124`; the law-level mean part appears only as a frozen Laplacian in `graded_retention.py:7-36`.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** Two energy routes coexist. The sample-level comparison record (`prop:obs-selective-receiver-update`, `cor:link-delta-rule`, `prop:glink-sample-record`) is a normalized record kernel, so its free energy is an evidence bound; s1 confirms that the code computes exactly that bound. The law-level transported divergence, which is the energy of VFE_3.0 (KL between transported Gaussian beliefs, softmax attention over it), enters a bound only through a normalized Gibbs law over law points, whose existence is `open:prob-law-point-prior`. c12 says so for the composite potential, but the chapter titled "The Multiscale Graph VFE and Closing the Tower" types its row free energies on the law-level D_e, and an implementer following c12 builds the composite potential. The two routes also weight residuals by different precisions: the record uses the receiver's W_i, the law-level KL the transported source precision Θ^{-T} Σ_j^{-1} Θ^{-1} (`graded_retention.connection_laplacian` uses the latter correctly; derivation from both directed mean KLs).
**Fix:** One sentence at the start of `sec:full-graph-vfe-scale-local` naming the in-bound alternative: replace D^{x,ℓ}_e by the expected comparison-record energy, for which `prop:obs-attention-elbo`(b) makes the row free energy a sector of the tower VFE.
**Falsifies:** A normalized law-point prior under which the law-level row free energy is a sector of one bound.
**Status:** Correctly scoped in the text (composite potential, NOT-CLAIMED); the issue is a routing decision left implicit.

### L11-7: Under the adaptive-tower reading parents carry no free recognition coordinates; a declared hierarchy with links exists only as the one-level Gaussian star
**Location:** `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:166-189` (`lem:full-graph-adaptive-reversal`: K_↓ is the reversal kernel, supported on child configurations compatible with the realized parent), `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:121-132` (the same structural channel pushes the recognition law), `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:982-993` (a deterministic and an independently sampled parent "are therefore not two free descriptions of one joint"), `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:933-966` (`prop:full-graph-downward-coupling`, binary example only); `g10_reciprocal_pairs.tex:233-294` (`thm:obs-star-fixed-point-contraction`). Code: `network.py:166-187` (`GaussianParent.lift_moments`, equilibrium reversal kernel), `latent_parent_feedback.py:52-95` (`downward`), `latent_coarse.py:142-212`; no star or declared parent-generates-children model exists.
**Severity:** medium
**Evidence:** In an adaptive tower the tower generative law, posterior, and recognition law are the pushforwards of their depth-0 laws by one channel C, so by the chain rule KL(Q_0 ⊗ C || Π_0 ⊗ C) = KL(Q_0 || Π_0), and the tower VFE equals the depth-0 VFE: parent beliefs are functions of the children and add no inference. A recognition law that gives the parent its own law, independent of the children, has infinite divergence from a reversal kernel concentrated on the compatible fiber (`prop:elbo-subspace-support-singular`). Parents that carry their own inference therefore need a declared hierarchy, whose only Gaussian instance with link transports is the one-level anchored star y_i | y_0 ~ N(Θ_i y_0, Σ_i) of g10. Neither a multilevel declared Gaussian hierarchy with dressed links nor a recognition family for it is written, and MultiAgentELBO implements only the adaptive reading.
**Fix:** A remark in `sec:full-graph-vfe-joint` stating F_tower = F_0 for the adaptive tower, and a pointer to the g10 star as the Gaussian declared hierarchy, with the multilevel version (stars of stars, with the dressed links of `def:full-graph-coarse-links` as transports) named as the construction an implementation should use.
**Falsifies:** A tower recognition family in the text that gives parents free coordinates and finite tower VFE under the adaptive reading.
**Status:** The statements are correct; the computational consequence is left to the reader.

### L11-8: Exact edge-block Hessian extraction in the code depends on every record reading one primitive edge, which composite coarse twists violate
**Location:** `MultiAgentELBO/src/multiagent_elbo/realizations/gaussian/latent_links.py:316-323` (`expected_generator_derivatives`: Hessian columns from the gradient of `g[..., d].sum()`); theory `c12_multiscale_vfe.tex:648-660` (`eq:link-coarse-twist`, the coarse twist e^{ω_e'} e^{ω_e} reads two fine generators).
**Severity:** low
**Evidence:** Summing a gradient component over all edges before differentiating returns the diagonal edge block exactly only because each record term depends on a single edge, as the docstring says ("Record terms have one primitive edge per factor"). A record that reads a composite twist, which a latent-link model needs at depth ℓ+1 if the fine generators stay latent, would fold the cross-edge blocks into the diagonal without raising any error. This is a hazard for reuse, not a present defect.
**Fix:** In a VFE_4.0 port, compute per-edge Hessian blocks with an explicit edge mask or a forward-over-reverse product per edge, and assert the one-edge-per-record structure wherever the shortcut is kept.
**Falsifies:** A record path in the current code that already reads two primitives (none found).
**Status:** Code note; no theory status involved.

### L11-9: `LatentLinkModel.offset` admits per-slot observations, which breaks the one-record-per-row form of `def:obs-attention-label-model`
**Location:** `latent_links.py:31` and `:42` (`offset` of shape (N, A, K)); `latent_prediction.py:70-97` (records generated with one observation per row, shape (N, K)); theory `c06_attention.tex:62-68` and `:781-799` (one record o_i per row, c_i independent of j).

If the slots of a row carry different offsets, the stacked object is not one record of that form. All call sites I read broadcast one o_i across the slots, so the model is used correctly; the validator does not enforce it.
**Fix:** Store offsets as (N, K), or assert equality across the slot axis in `__post_init__`.
**Falsifies:** A call site that intends slot-specific templates (the record would then need its own likelihood statement).
**Status:** Code note.

### 4.2 What must be computed, and in which form

At one base point, the objects that are closed form for Gaussian product matter are the collective free energy at held links (Gaussian KLs, the record normalizer -log c_i, and expected record energies that depend on the source covariance through Σ_i + MΣ_jM^T for distinct endpoints and through (I-M)Σ_i(I-M)^T for a loop copy), the constant-row optimum β^{Q*} with its row potential R^Q, the generator KL from a Gaussian base law, the BLR step, Schur marginals and reversal kernels of a Gaussian parent, and the dressed links of a hard partition. Everything that involves the exponential chart under a law is not closed form: E_R[e^{±ω}], the second moments E[e^ω ⊗ e^ω], expected loop defects, and expected record costs at latent links need quadrature or Monte Carlo, and g11 reduces them to per-edge first and second exponential moments only for walks without a repeated reversal pair. The normalizer Z_S and every tilted-prior expectation need MCMC or annealing (L11-4). The law-level transported divergence is closed form for Gaussians but enters a bound only through an unresolved law-point prior (L11-6).

### 4.3 Choices the theory leaves to the implementer

The implementer must declare the copy set and the convention for mutually supported pairs (L11-1) and the link type of self edges (L11-2). The fast-slow split and the rate ratio are declarations by `def:obs-fast-slow-blocks`, and `prop:hist-scalar-mobility-orbit` shows that an anisotropic choice can change the orbit, so they are model choices rather than tuning. The discrete schedule and step rule (L11-3) and the estimator of expectations under R (fixed common samples with backtracking, as in MultiAgentELBO, or deterministic quadrature) are not fixed by the theory. Prior widths must be set relative to the principal-logarithm domain (L11-5). At the multiscale level the implementer chooses between the adaptive tower, whose parents are diagnostic pushforwards that add no free energy, and a declared hierarchy, whose parents are latent causes (L11-7). The temperature τ is not a free softmax scale in the record model: it multiplies the record noise covariance, so learning τ must carry the -(dim/2) log τ of the normalizer, which MultiAgentELBO includes (`latent_links.py:189`).

### 4.4 Numerical hazards

The Fréchet derivative of the exponential is singular at eigenvalue differences in 2πiZ minus zero, so BLR Hessians can be indefinite and gradients can vanish at saddles (`prop:glink-sample-record`(iv)); generator laws should stay on the principal domain, and the exit fraction should be monitored. Rooted transports lose accuracy at depth in proportion to their condition number; `latent_coarse._rooted_transport_pair` composes step inverses and the retained hierarchy stores tree copies as exact identities for that reason, and VFE_4.0 should do the same. Simultaneous updates need a step rule (s3(b)). Under Anaconda, the MultiAgentELBO pytest process aborts in `numpy.linalg.eigh` once torch is loaded (prune plan, section d), so CPU unit tests there run under bare `python`.

### 4.5 Recommended build order

Stage 0 is the graph data model: agents, edge copies in reversal pairs with Θ_ē = Θ_e^{-1}, an orientation E_+, loop copies, parallel copies, and rows as sparse subsets of incoming copies with the mutual-pair convention declared (L11-1). Its tests are the reversal involution, holonomy ordering against `eq:link-reference-holonomy`, and passive-gauge invariance of twists. MultiAgentELBO's `CoupledModel` should not be reused for this stage, because it forbids self sources and stores dense (N, A) source tables; a sparse edge-list model that runs on the GPU is a rewrite. VFE_3.0's gauge-frame transports Ω_ij = exp(φ_i) exp(-φ_j) are the frame-aligned links Θ = U_i U_j^{-1} of this theory, which have trivial holonomy and are strictly stronger than graph flatness; in VFE_4.0 they are the special case of independent link generators with every cycle product equal to the identity, and the frames stay passive.

Stage 1 is the held-link collective free energy with product Gaussian matter and per-receiver rows, porting the formulas of `coupled_learning.free_energy` and `latent_links.matter_free_energy` and adding the loop-copy cost branch (L11-2). Its tests are an s1-style quadrature check at N = 3, the local-global potential identity, the presence of the sender terms of one-way rows in the gradient, and the numbers of `cor:obs-attention-self-edge` for blocked uniform rows.

Stage 2 is the dynamics: Fisher BLR for matter, the replicator for rows, a chromatic sequential schedule over the pairwise interaction graph with rows frozen, and a line search for any simultaneous step. Its tests are monotone descent, the s3(b) counterexample as a regression test, and agreement between the chromatic schedule and the sequential sweep.

Stage 3 adds latent generators, reusing `latent_links` (chart, defects, `_loop_derivatives`, generator KL), `latent_natural.gaussian_blr_step`, and the `latent_prior` sampler nearly verbatim, and adding a Z_S estimator, the anchor M-coordinate, and the domain monitor. Its tests are finite differences of the loop derivatives, `eq:link-anchor-quadratic`, BLR exactness on a quadratic integrand, and the held-link limit as the generator precision grows (`thm:link-held-limit`).

Stage 4 is the coarse step with hard partitions: rooted transports and dressed marks (port `latent_coarse` with its step inverses), event pushforwards with the loop term of `eq:full-graph-edge-pushforward` (new), and Schur parents (`network.marginalize`). Its tests are the holonomy identity of `prop:full-graph-coarse-links`, invariance under a root change and dependence on a tree change, unit mass of event laws, and the equality of the tower VFE and the depth-0 VFE under the adaptive reading.

Stage 5 is the declared hierarchy, starting from the g10 star with `eq:obs-star-rate` as its test, and only then the law-level composite potential as a declared descent objective (L11-6). Formation statistics (c13) and the multi-head label model, which is open, come after these stages. The roughly sixty research modules for Fisher balls, Gaussian-mixture panels, parent bins, and codecs are study artifacts of the latent-link RG plan and need not be carried over.
