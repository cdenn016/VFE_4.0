## 2. Overall verdict

The panel's combined judgment is that no main theorem of MAgent Theory 2.0 is shown false, and that its formation standard is not yet a well-posed test, so that no construction in chapters 06 to 14 or in Item 7 has a selection result that a lens records as proved to depend on the interaction under a fixed reading of that standard, apart from the plain (F5) of chapter 13, which D17-PA records as proved under the declared selector and which, for `pa:persistent-grouping`, XC2-FRAME reports holds only for selector thresholds in (1/8, 1/3) (H08). The cluster files merge 571 rows of results that lenses reconstructed, recomputed or compared without finding a defect (Section 9), and the coverage critic records a proof reconstruction for 262 of the 279 theorem-grade results before the gap lenses added more. Two statements are reported false at high severity. Clause (iii) of `form:kappa-independence` (`11_conditional_formation`), that the null flow reproduces the membership history, is reported false by D15-WISH, GAP03-S2 and XC3-SCOPE under the label-preserving null convention that chapter 13, companion c13 and Item 7 declare, and is recorded as correct as stated by XC2-FRAME and relied on by XC8-REMED; the positions differ on which convention the text declares, not on the arithmetic of either convention (H01). The convex no-go after `it7:attractor` in the unintegrated Item 7 is reported false by D27-ITEM7 and GAP07-S3 (H02). The other 34 error findings (15 medium, 19 low) concern remarks, single proof steps, two hypothesis sentences, one clause in (iii) of the theorem `it1:learning` (false at lambda = 0 and against the prior covariance, D23-LEARN-03), companion and working-document sentences and reported numbers beside correct results, and no lens reports that any of them falsifies a main theorem. The weight of the review lies in five recurring lines. First, the abstract and its satellites state results more broadly than the proofs: the no-global-free-energy obstruction (seven lenses, H03), "grouping requires the interaction" for fusion (H11), the chapter 12 null reproduction (S2-M08, S4-M02), and a "physically noninjective" parent in chapter 13 (H05). Second, the six-clause standard leaves its null flow, probe class, counterfactual class, topology, quantifier, horizon and selector to the constructor (H04 to H08), so that non-interacting agents pass (F5) (H09), an anchored linear consensus meets every clause under the readings chapters 11 and 12 use (H10), and verdicts flip on identical dynamics with the selector threshold (H08) or the row reading (H04). Third, the compressions discard coordinates that no admitted probe reads and that fade, and the contextual geometry is prepared and transient (2026-10-22 H7, T4 and T5 recur: S3-M02, S3-M03, S4-M13), while the gauge structure does no dynamical work in any formation result (S4-M15). Fourth, the program-level lenses report that every multi-stage construction sits on the reciprocal potential, that nothing is proved under the global free energy that VFE_4.0 implements, and that agents never act and parents never act back (H16 to H18, C01, S4-M14). Fifth, the working documents drift from the integrated chapters, and Item 7 claims an instance meeting every clause while the abstract says none exists (H12 to H14, S3-M17 to S3-M20, S4-M04). Against this, the gap lenses closed most reconstruction gaps the coverage critic ranked (`form:parent`, the uniqueness clause of `form:full-flow`, the chapter 12 late-phase constant, explicit persistence constants for chapter 13, well-posedness of the per-agent chart flow, Borel measurability of chart flows inside history laws), and the lenses supply concrete repairs: a replacement standard (I02), a mass-carrying parent for Item 7 (I03), Gaussian closure of the global and per-agent flows (I04), and a formation construction under the collective free energy at critical priority (C01). Much of the numerical evidence behind the findings themselves is floating point on single families and is listed as uncertified in Section 9.
