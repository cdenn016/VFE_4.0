### XC8-REMED-01: The abstract and the summary statements drop the identity-channel hypothesis of `prop:dyn-no-global-vfe`, and constant-output channels violate the unqualified claim exactly
**Category:** inflation
**Severity:** high
**Location:** frontmatter.tex:5; chapters/03_dynamics.tex:4 and :52; chapters/01_foundations.tex:197 and :226; chapters/06_outlook.tex:6; README.md:34; AUTHORING.md:39; result prop:dyn-no-global-vfe at chapters/03_dynamics.tex:375-385
**Evidence:** The proposition holds the rows fixed and positive and assumes that some agent attends to a source through an identity channel; part (b) also fixes the channels. The abstract says "we prove that it is not the free energy of any fixed normalized model whose recognition laws are the agent laws", and the other locations repeat the claim without hypotheses. Constant-output channels K^q_ij(v,.) = C^q and K^s_ij(w,.) = C^s are admitted by def:int-channel and eq:dyn-peer-costs (chapter 11 uses channels constant in their input). For them, with fixed rows, E = sum_i [KL(q_i x s_i || R_i) + KL(q_i||C^q) + KL(s_i||C^s)] + const, and E = 2F(Q; o) + b for the normalized joint P(y, o) = U(y) l(o|y) with U uniform on the contents, a binary registered record l(1|y) = prod_i sqrt(R_i(k_i,m_i) C^q(k_i) C^s(m_i)) / c, and the product recognition Q of the agent laws q_i x s_i. Script `s07_constant_channel_vfe.py` (three agents, binary sectors, random references and rows) finds E - 2F constant over 200 random interior configurations, spread 1.2e-14. The proposition is not contradicted, but the abstract-level statement is. The excluded class is degenerate (it has no source force), and part (b) extends to every fixed input-dependent channel (see Fix); noisy fixed channels and law-dependent channels are nevertheless outside the proof as written. The severity follows the brief, which places inflations in the abstract at high; the repair is one qualifier on the claim that separates the reciprocal potential from the two free-energy objectives, plus a two-line extension.
**Fix:** Qualify every occurrence ("when some attended edge compares through a channel whose output depends on its input, for example an identity channel"). Extend (b): for a fixed channel K on the edge from j to i, the mixed derivative of beta_ij KL(q_i||q_j K), once along a mass-preserving direction w of q_i and twice along a mass-preserving direction v of q_j, is beta_ij sum_u w(u) (vK)(u)^2 / (q_j K)(u)^2. It is nonzero on an open set unless vK = 0 for every mass-preserving v, that is, unless K is constant-output, while every fixed-model free energy with product recognition has vanishing mixed derivatives of this order. The constant-output class is then the exact exception, where E = 2F + b.
**Falsifies:** An error in the construction of s07 (P is a normalized joint with a binary record; recognition is the product of the agent laws; a = 2 > 0).

### XC8-REMED-02: The corollary "interaction-sensitive selection requires receiver-dependent targets" is stronger than `inh:common-clock` and `form:mean-field` prove
**Category:** inflation
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/05_inheritance.tex:1365-1373 (remark after inh:common-clock); chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:208 (after form:mean-field); chapters/06_outlook.tex:16; companion/c05_inheritance.tex:251-252; echoed in future_TODO.md:92
**Evidence:** `inh:common-clock` (05:1329-1363) assumes a shared model-independent evaluator G_0, self excluded from the belief sources, and every peer channel equal to one constant-output law C[M] of the additive moment M = sum_i E_{q_i} T. It proves d/dt log(q_i/q_j) = -2 log(q_i/q_j) + c_ij(t), with -1 along the null flow, and the remark correctly concludes that "any relabeling-equivariant selector that reads only these pairwise log-ratios modulo constants" reproduces the null partition sequence with halved event times. It then concludes "Interaction-sensitive selection under the reciprocal potential therefore requires receiver-dependent targets, such as selective channels with nonconstant Gibbs rows" (05:1372-1373). Chapter 11 says such selection "needs targets that depend on the receiver" (11:208); the outlook says it "requires receiver-dependent targets, since shared references with additive moment channels make every pairwise law comparison relax on a common clock"; the companion says it "needs receivers that weigh different sources differently". The proof itself gives log q_i(t) = b(t) log q_i(0) + W_t + c_i(t), with b = e^{-2t} and a common base W_t under the full flow, and b = e^{-t}, W = (1 - e^{-t}) log G_0 under the null flow. Centered log-ratios see only b; a divergence between two laws also sees the common base W_t, and the interaction changes W_t. Script `s02_common_clock_selector.py` implements the exact architecture (N = 4, three letters, C[M] = softmax(kappa M) with M = sum_i q_i, self excluded, Gibbs rows equal to the priors because all costs in a row coincide); the common-clock identity holds with residual 1.3e-15 for the full flow and 3.3e-16 for the null flow. Script `s03_common_clock_margin.py` uses G_0 = (0.7, 0.2, 0.1), kappa = 4 and a Jeffreys-divergence threshold selector (join a pair when KL(q_i||q_j) + KL(q_j||q_i) first reaches delta = 0.05 from above; relabeling-equivariant and measurable in the state). Full flow: pair {0,1} reaches delta at t = 0.442 and {2,3} at 0.483. Null flow: {2,3} at 0.950 and {0,1} at 0.983. The first selected pair differs with a crossing-time margin of 0.033, and it still differs for 21 of 25 random perturbations of size 0.02 of all twelve log coordinates. So P differs from P^0 on an open set of initial laws in an architecture whose targets are receiver-independent and whose Gibbs rows are constant. This is numerical evidence on one open set, not a theorem, but it is enough to falsify the universal statements, and the outlook's justification ("every pairwise law comparison relax[es] on a common clock") is false for the divergences themselves.
**Fix:** Replace the four sentences by what the propositions support: within this class, every relabeling-equivariant selector that reads only centered pairwise log-likelihood ratios fails (F5); escaping requires a selector that reads more than these ratios, receiver- or source-dependent targets (including channels that read the source sample), heterogeneous evaluators, or self sources. The exact tilt conjugacy sketched in XC8-REMED-13 can replace the remark; it gives a sharp sufficient condition for failing (F5).
**Falsifies:** A proof that every relabeling-equivariant measurable selector in the common-clock class gives P = P^0 on the admitted class; or an error in the field of s02, which the clock-identity residual checks against the proposition itself.

### XC8-REMED-13: In the common-clock class the full flow is the null flow at doubled time followed by one common tilt, which gives a sharp sufficient condition for failing (F5) and shows how tilt-sensitive selectors pass it
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/05_inheritance.tex:1329-1373 (inh:common-clock) and :1389 ((F5)); chapters/11_conditional_formation.tex:196-208
**Evidence:** From the proof of inh:common-clock, log q_i(t) = e^{-2t} log q_i(0) + W_t + c_i(t) along the full flow and log q_i^0(tau) = e^{-tau} log q_i(0) + (1 - e^{-tau}) log G_0 + c_i'(tau) along the null flow. At tau = 2t the difference W_t - (1 - e^{-2t}) log G_0 does not depend on i. Hence q_i(t) = Tilt_{omega_t}(q_i^0(2t)) for every i, where Tilt_omega(q) = omega q / E_q omega and omega_t is one positive function common to all agents. This is exact (it uses only the identity the proposition proves) and gives the sharp form of the remark: a selector produces P = P^0 for every admitted datum if it is invariant under common tilts and monotone time changes, and selectors that are not tilt-invariant (divergence thresholds, s03) can pass (F5) although the interaction acts only through one common tilt. The second half bears on the standard: (F5) is selector-relative, and in this class it can be met by choosing a tilt-sensitive selector while every relative coordinate evolves as in the null flow.
**Fix:** State the conjugacy as a corollary of inh:common-clock. It does not transfer to form:mean-field: there the null flow keeps the self cost with the channel evaluated at frozen peer moments, so its forcing V^0_i depends on the agent, and only the Wishart shape coordinates keep a common clock (form:kappa-independence (iii)). Consider stating (F5), or its strong form, modulo time reparametrization and modulo symmetries that intertwine Phi and Phi^0, so that a common tilt cannot by itself certify interaction necessity. Difficulty: low for the corollary; the revision of (F5) is a definitional choice for the author.

## Extensions and improvements

The most useful next step in this scope is the exact form of the common-clock no-go (XC8-REMED-13). From the identity of inh:common-clock, the full configuration at time t is the null configuration at time 2t multiplied, agent by agent, by one common positive tilt omega_t and renormalized. (This uses the self-excluded hypothesis; in the form:mean-field class the null forcing depends on the agent, and the conjugacy has to be checked separately.) A corollary of three lines therefore says that a relabeling-equivariant selector reproduces the null partition sequence for every admitted datum if it is invariant under common tilts and monotone time changes, while a divergence-threshold selector can separate the two flows (s03 gives an open set). This replaces the overstated corollary of XC8-REMED-02 with a sharp statement and exposes a weakness of (F5): in this class, interaction necessity can be bought by a tilt-sensitive selector although every relative coordinate evolves as in the null flow. A strong form of (F5) stated modulo time reparametrization and the symmetries intertwining Phi and Phi^0 would exclude that loophole.

Part (b) of prop:dyn-no-global-vfe becomes an iff with a two-line computation (XC8-REMED-01): on an attended edge with a fixed channel K, the mixed third derivative of the peer cost is beta sum_u w(u)(vK)(u)^2/(q_jK)(u)^2, which vanishes identically exactly when K is constant-output, and in that case the potential with fixed rows equals 2F + b for an explicit normalized model with a binary registered record (s07). Stating both directions turns the obstruction into a classification of when the reciprocal potential is, and is not, a fixed-model free energy with product recognition, and the law-dependent case then remains the precise open part.

Global existence for the chapter 7 public flow is within reach (XC8-REMED-12). The joint maximum and minimum principle of prop:dyn-global-flow and lem:boundary-global extends to bounded log-density charts on general spaces for identity and bounded-kernel public channels: at the essential maximum over agents and public points the incoming terms are bounded below by the negative incoming weight, the forcing H lies in [0, M] by smooth:wellposed (i), and energy decrease bounds the normalizers. This would make the autonomy statements of smooth:spectrum-flow, smooth:equal-spectra and smooth:cgf-completion global in time and would settle author decision 2 for the one chapter that still states only local existence.

The duplicated results (XC8-REMED-05) should be merged into chapter 5 general statements with the union of the hypotheses: one common-clock proposition covering self excluded (null rate -1, common forcing) and self compared through the common constant channel (coefficient -2, agent-dependent null forcing); one scalar-closure proposition; one equivariant-descent lemma with both the free proper finite-dimensional form and the fixed-point form in a Banach chart; and a citation of prop:dyn-chart-flow wherever a chapter re-proves local well-posedness. geo:minimal-contraction can then be used in chapter 5 as the geometric monotonicity statement that 2026-10-22 I3 proposed.
