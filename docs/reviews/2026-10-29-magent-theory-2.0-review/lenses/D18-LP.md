### D18-LP-01: A bump argument settles the germ-local case of the unrestricted question for native-family parents; analytic preparations give its positive counterpart
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** high
**Location:** chapters/14_collective_local_parent.tex:378 (closing paragraph: "Genuinely unbounded contextual-order memory ... are not classified"), lp:path-memory-obstruction (346-370), lp:causal-jet-obstruction (372-376), lp:relative-identification (86-92); companion/c14_collective_local_parent.tex:225; frontmatter.tex:13; chapters/06_outlook.tex:14
**Evidence:** Statement. Let $\mathcal X$ be a preparation class satisfying the chapter's cone condition with its rotational union, closed under $x_0\mapsto\mathsf R_{\kappa(c)}x_0$ for smooth $\kappa$ of small $C^2$ norm, and containing one section $x_0$ whose initial $B_i(c)$ are positive multiples of a single context-constant unit vector $e$ (admitted by the cone condition; the other sectors, including the nonflat marked sector, are arbitrary). Consider parents whose actual laws are native laws (`lp:law` with the presentation and Gibbs-label factors) at a configuration $\bar x(c,t)$, which reproduce every admitted fixed-context response law, and which satisfy (L) germ locality: the parent at $c$ depends on the preparation only through its germ at $c$ (any contextual order, arbitrary causal temporal processing, explicit dependence on $c$ and on the record word allowed); (E) erasure: the parent of $\mathsf R_gx_0$ equals the parent of $x_0$ for every constant $g$; (H) the parent phase defined below does not depend on random record outcomes, or geometric equality is required on each record branch. Then no such parent preserves the minimized contextual Fisher tensor, at any single time, for every member of $\mathcal X$.

Proof. (i) By `lp:relative-identification` and `lp:cone`, reproducing the public, marked, presentation and relative-angle response laws at $c$ forces $\bar x(c,t)=\mathsf R_{-\Theta(c,t)}x(c,t)$ for one angle field $\Theta$; (L) and (E) give germ dependence of $\Theta$ and $\Theta[\mathsf R_gx_0]=\Theta[x_0]+g$. (ii) The physical tangent $\mathcal Jx$ is Fisher-orthogonal to the marked orbit and to the history and label scores (rows are invariant under both actions), so the minimizing class is unchanged and $\bar h-h=-2\,d\Theta\odot b_t+I_t\,d\Theta\otimes d\Theta$ with $b_t(X)=\E_H\sum_i(X\arg B_i)\mathcal I(|B_i|)$, $I_t=\E_H\sum_i\mathcal I(|B_i|)$, $\mathcal I(r)=rI_1(r)/I_0(r)$ (D18-LP-09; script s05, residual 2.2e-16). (iii) Positive rows and the absence of any $\psi$-reading likelihood keep every $B_i(c,t)$ on the ray of $e$ for every record history, so $b_t\equiv0$ for $x_0$ and therefore $d\Theta[x_0]=0$. For $x_0^\kappa=\mathsf R_\kappa x_0$, equivariance of the pointwise flow gives $\arg B_i^\kappa=\arg e+\kappa$ and $b_t=I_t\,d\kappa$, so $d\Theta^\kappa\odot(d\Theta^\kappa-2\,d\kappa)=0$ pointwise; a symmetric product of two covectors vanishes only if a factor does, so $d\Theta^\kappa\in\{0,2\,d\kappa\}$ at every point. (iv) Take $\kappa=\kappa_2\rho(c^1)$ with $\rho$ a smooth monotone step and $0<|\kappa_2|<\pi$. On the connected slab $\{d\kappa\ne0\}$ the two closed disjoint branch sets cannot both be nonempty, so along a path crossing the slab $\Theta^\kappa(c_2)-\Theta^\kappa(c_1)\in\{0,2\kappa_2\}$. But $\kappa$ is locally constant near $c_1$ and $c_2$, so (L) and (E) give $\Theta^\kappa(c_1)=\Theta[x_0](c_1)$ and $\Theta^\kappa(c_2)=\Theta[\mathsf R_{\kappa_2}x_0](c_2)=\Theta[x_0](c_2)+\kappa_2$, with $\Theta[x_0]$ constant; the difference is $\kappa_2$. Contradiction.

Scope. This covers unbounded contextual order and arbitrary temporal processing for native-family parents with history-independent phase, which is the class of the chapter's own accumulator (its phase is $\alpha_0(c)$) and of every frame fixed at preparation; for that class it contains the native-family case of `lp:path-memory-obstruction` and of the order-zero part of `lp:jet-obstruction` and extends both to any order. Sharpness: on real-analytic preparations on a connected patch the germ at $c$ determines $x(c_0,t_*)$ by analytic continuation, so $\Theta(c):=\arg S(x(c_0,t_*))$ is germ-computable, equivariant and constant; the resulting parent is the frozen-frame construction of the handoff section (14:182) with $T=t_*$, and it reproduces histories, erases the common angle and preserves the geometry. Any obstruction for germ memories must therefore use non-analytic variations, as the bump does. Residual: with history-dependent germ-local phases and joint (not branchwise) equality, (iii) becomes $\E_H[I^H((a_H-k)^2-k^2)]=0$ with $a_H=d\Theta^H$, $k=d\kappa$, which no longer forces a pointwise dichotomy; I found solutions of this integrated condition only for rules that read $\kappa(c)$ itself (for example $a_{H_{1,2}}=k(1+\sqrt2\cos\varphi)$ and $k(1+\sqrt2\sin\varphi)$ with $\varphi=2\pi\kappa/\kappa_2$ on two equally weighted branches), which violate (E). Parents outside the native family (for example twirled $\psi$ laws) and mechanisms that read neighboring or reference contexts are outside the argument.
**Fix:** Add the statement as a proposition after `lp:path-memory-obstruction`, add the analytic realization as a remark, and restate the closing paragraph (14:378), the companion (c14:225), the abstract and the outlook with the residual cases only.
**Falsifies:** A germ-local, erasing, history-independent phase rule preserving the tensor on a class closed under such bumps; or a native-family parent that reproduces every fixed-context response law without being a common rotation of the fine configuration (that would contradict `lp:relative-identification`).

### D18-LP-02: "Physical" and "nongauge" are undefined; the erased orientation is a global frame redundancy that only an excluded diagnostic sees
**Category:** weakness
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/14_collective_local_parent.tex:5, 41 ("The common physical rotation of $\psi$ is a separate nongauge action"), 84, 92, 159 ("It still changes the excluded child absolute-angle diagnostic"), 285; companion/c14_collective_local_parent.tex:5, 71, 181; chapters/06_outlook.tex:14
**Evidence:** No constitutive datum, record kernel, channel or admitted probe refers to an absolute $\psi$ frame: the self evaluator is uniform in $\psi$ (14:23), `lp:likelihood` does not read $\psi$, the peer channels copy $\psi$ (a relation between the agents' frames, preserved by a common rotation), the rows read only $B_j\cdot M(B_i)$ and $|B_j|$ (`lp:weights`), and "No absolute $\psi$ response is admitted at any stage" (14:84). A passive change of the $\psi$ lab frame therefore acts exactly as the "common physical rotation": rotate every $B_i$, and there are no fixed $\psi$ data whose embeddings would also rotate. The only object that separates the fibers is the child diagnostic $\E e^{i\Psi}$, which the chapter itself calls excluded (14:159), whereas chapter 13 calls its analogue "a permitted child-level distinction for assessing physical loss" (13:316). No active transformation or observable algebra is defined against which "nongauge" is judged. In gauge terms, the identity $\psi$ links reduce the per-agent $\psi$ frame freedom to the diagonal subgroup, and the quotient is by that residual global symmetry; `lp:relative-identification` is the statement that individual rotations are not symmetries because the links fix relative frames. Clause (F4) of `inh:recursive-formation` (05:1388) asks that "some admitted child-level probe distinguishes two configurations with the same retained value"; in this chapter no admitted probe does, so (F4) can be met only by reading the excluded diagnostic as admitted. The chapter does not claim (F4) explicitly, but its title ("Collective Compression") and the outlook ("permits one common physical orientation to be lost only collectively") invite that reading. This is the pattern of 2026-10-22 H5 and X1-GAUGE-02 (chapter 12: an orientation resolved only by an external protocol, "nongauge" without definition), recurring in the new construction; D17-PA-01 records the chapter 13 analogue.
**Fix:** Define the active $\psi$ action and the observable algebra, state that the quotient is by a global symmetry of every constitutive datum and admitted probe, describe the loss as relative to the declared child diagnostic, and say whether that diagnostic counts as admitted for (F4); alternatively add an admitted $\psi$-reading reference inside the population, which (as X1 noted for chapter 12) breaks exact descent.
**Falsifies:** A constitutive datum, record kernel or admitted probe in the chapter that reads the absolute $\psi$ frame.

## Extensions and improvements

The germ-local theorem of D18-LP-01 is the most valuable next step, because it converts the chapter's open list into a dichotomy for native-family parents. Its proof is half a page: by `lp:relative-identification` any native-family parent that reproduces the fixed-context responses is a phase field $\mathsf R_{-\Theta}x$; the exact identity of D18-LP-09 makes tensor equality the pointwise condition $d\Theta\odot(I_t\,d\Theta-2b_t)=0$; a collinear test section forces $d\Theta=0$; and a smooth step $\kappa$ forces $d\Theta^\kappa\in\{0,2d\kappa\}$ on a connected slab, contradicting the endpoint jump $\kappa_2$ that germ locality and erasure impose. The remaining case is a history-dependent phase under joint equality, where the condition is $\E_H[I^H((a_H-k)^2-k^2)]=0$. A natural route is to exploit that a germ-local equivariant rule can be written $\Theta^H=\arg S+\Phi^H$ with $\Phi^H$ a rotation-invariant germ functional; on the linear part of a step, with all other sectors context-constant, the invariant germ data are translation-invariant, so without explicit $c$-dependence $\Phi^H$ is constant there and the condition fails. Explicit $c$-dependence is the obstacle; I expect a two-step argument (two steps $\kappa,\kappa'$ that share germs on a subinterval but have different jumps) to close it. Difficulty: moderate for the history-dependent case, low for what D18-LP-01 states.

The positive counterpart deserves a remark in the chapter. On real-analytic preparations on a connected patch the germ at $c$ determines the reference value by continuation, so the handoff construction frozen at $t_*$ is germ-computable, erasing and geometrically exact. This shows that every obstruction for germ memories must use non-analytic variations, and it identifies what the "reference" really supplies: one global integration constant for $\alpha_0$, which a $C^\infty$ germ cannot carry and an analytic germ can. The open problem of D18-LP-08 should then be posed over a declared smooth class closed under compactly supported rotations, with the analytic and coherent classes listed as solved restricted cases.

For the coherence clause, the exact defect identity of D18-LP-09 reduces necessity on a window to showing that $t\mapsto b_t(X)/I_t$ is not identically $X\alpha_0/2$ on an interval when $X\alpha_0\ne0$. In the small-amplitude linear limit this ratio is a Moebius function of $e^{-12t/5}$ whose constant value is $X\alpha_0$ (s08), which proves necessity there. In the exact model every quantity is real-analytic in $t$ on a record branch, so equality on an interval propagates to the whole branch; as $t\to\infty$ in the attracting ball the $B_i$ align along the Perron direction of the limiting $B$-matrix, so $b_t/I_t\to\lim_tX\alpha(c,t)$, and necessity reduces to excluding $X\alpha_0=-2X\int_{t_*}^\infty\omega_{\rm loc}\,dt$ together with the finite-time identity. Difficulty: moderate; a perturbation argument from the linear limit would give necessity for sufficiently small $\epsilon,\delta$.

The decay of D18-LP-03 is structural for per-agent models with this symmetry, so nontransient collective compression needs a new interaction. A minimal candidate that keeps the common rotation as an exact symmetry is a relative-angle likelihood comparing $\psi_i$ with a fresh copy $\psi'_j$ of a peer's transmitted angle, $\ell\propto e^{\lambda\cos(\psi_i-\psi'_j-\omega)}$. Its expected log likelihood is $\lambda\,\Re(\zeta_i\overline{\zeta_j}e^{-i\omega})$, which adds $\lambda R_\omega M(B_j)$ to the $B_i$ target, giving $\dot B_i=-B_i+\sum_{j\ne i}\beta_{ij}B_j+\lambda R_\omega M(B_j)$. For small $B$ this is a linear coupling of size $\lambda/2$ that can overcome the decay rate $\min_i\beta_{ii}$; saturation of $M$ ($|M|<1$) then bounds $B$, so the erased angle keeps positive Fisher inertia, and for $\omega\ne0$ the common phase rotates at a constant rate, so the erased variable has nontrivial dynamics. The record kernel depends on a peer's transmitted sample, which extends `def:dyn-per-agent`; the field stays pointwise and equivariant, so `lp:local-realization`, `lp:causal-flow` and the obstructions carry over unchanged in structure, while the grouping and null arguments must be redone. Difficulty: moderate.

Finally, the clarity of the chapter would improve if `lp:causal-coherence` and `lp:path-memory-obstruction` were stated as consequences of two short identities, the cost isometry for constant rotations and the defect identity of D18-LP-09, with the freezing-method attribution of D18-LP-10. That would leave `lp:local-realization`, `lp:jet-obstruction` and the new germ-local proposition as the theorems of the chapter.
