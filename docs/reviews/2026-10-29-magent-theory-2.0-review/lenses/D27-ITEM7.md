### D27-ITEM7-02: Condition (B) is asserted for an undeclared protocol class, with no response or joint kernel

**Severity:** high
**Location:** `Item_7_formation_feedback.tex:165` (it7:exact), `:254` ("The protocol class is fixed by it7:handoff before the retention"); `chapters/05_inheritance.tex:553-566` and `:699-701` (inh:inherited-parent (B)); persists from 2026-10-23 formation-audit finding 2 and the requirement "Operational inheritance".

## Extensions and improvements

The repair the program needs first is a mass-carrying parent with a read-only protocol, and it is short. Let the parent of a unit carry $p_U=(n_U,\bar y_U,\bar o_U,\pi_U)$, with Gibbs exponents $n_U\mathrm{KL}(P_U\Vert\nu)/T$, temperature $T/n_U$ and Fisher metric $\Lambda$. Define the merge of units $U_1,\dots,U_m$ by $n=\sum n_k$, $\bar y=\sum n_k\bar y_k/n$, $\bar o=\sum n_k\bar o_k/n$, and $\pi=\sum n_k\pi_k^{\rm re}/n$, where $\pi_k^{\rm re}$ moves the mass that $U_k$ gave to the other merged units onto the new parent. Associativity is the associativity of weighted means. Agreement with the primitive rule follows from the blockwise compensation identity, and `d27_identities_output.txt` (D2, D3) confirms it to machine precision for sizes 2 and 4 with heterogeneous priors. Add the running clocks to the retained state; both selectors read only presented laws, so the selection event, the clocks and the merge are functions of the retained state, and the hybrid process descends. For the protocol class of read-only fresh draws from $P_U$ at declared times with fixed records, the joint kernel of response and next retained state is a product of a kernel of $R$ and a point mass at the parent flow of $R$, and finite adaptive histories factor by induction on the query sequence, because responses do not feed back. This closes (F3) for the instance and settles type closure for the class of mass-carrying agents.

The second-stage compression should then be decided against D27-ITEM7-05. If the parent type keeps no event timestamps, (F4) holds at both stages, and the witnesses of `d27_f4_witness_output.txt` can be cited as numerical evidence until validated. If the age of a parent is part of its type, as a hybrid automaton with clocks suggests, the instance loses its second-stage compression, and recursion needs a second-stage forgotten coordinate that is not a function of the parent's age; for example, the members' record means could carry a persistent coordinate that the union's rows do not read, which connects to it7:open-persistent.

The counterfactual part is now complete for the reduced equation (D27-ITEM7-11); the remaining numerical obligation is the first-stage event history, which a validated integration on a small box would discharge (D27-ITEM7-20). With these three steps the instance would be a recursive formation in the declared Gaussian hybrid model with a declared selector and handoff, with the qualifications the text already lists (transient compression, Gaussian scope in sufficient-statistic coordinates, narrow window, strong (F5) failing).

For the derived selector, the obstruction to interaction necessity at the first stage is structural: the double-use term $\max(D_{ij},D_{ji})$ and the saving of labels reward mutual attention at any divergence (D27-ITEM7-08). Two concrete variants deserve a calculation. The first evaluates the merged model with the own parent at prior zero, so that the comparison rewards only sources outside the block; a pair whose members attend mainly to each other is then not merged, at any divergence, and whether the instance's record groups are still selected is untested. The second keeps the pool parent but subtracts the members' current mutual-attention free energy, so that the comparison measures the change in evidence for the outside sources only. Neither has been checked here beyond these remarks. The exact Gaussian closure survives both, because the parent still presents the pool.

For the program's long-term goals (PIFB2's meta-agents with top-down influence), Item 7 supplies the first construction in which a parent acts back on its members and the second stage depends on the first through a bistable reduced equation under per-agent free energies. Its reusable general-law ingredient is the pooled shared row at temperature $T/n$. Its limits are those that any general-law extension must address: exactness only in sufficient-statistic Gaussian families, compression of transient coordinates only, and a declared label-sharing rule.
