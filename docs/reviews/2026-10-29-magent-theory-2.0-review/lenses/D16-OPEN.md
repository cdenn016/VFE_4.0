### D16-OPEN-05: "On the product reference family the phases are constant" is false off the von Mises subfamily
**Category:** error
**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/12_open_formation.tex:421 (open:loss-information)
**Evidence:** open:product-reference admits arbitrary positive angular profiles, which evolve as $f_0^{e^{-2t}}$ (open:angular-flow). The phase of the first moment is fixed only for von Mises and other axially symmetric profiles. For $\log f_0=\cos\theta+2\cos(2\theta-1)$ the phase is 0.3929 at $t=0$, 0.2913 at $t=0.347$, 0.0793 at $t=1.151$ and 0.0084 at $t=2.303$; a von Mises phase stays at 0.9 to twelve digits (s4). **Fix:** Replace "On the product reference family the phases are constant, so" by "By equivariance the discarded offsets are constant group elements along the flow, so", replace "$n_i$ times its inertia" by the sum of the inertias at the record times, and replace "their circular mean estimates" by "the maximum-likelihood location estimate from them estimates", or restrict the sentence to the von Mises family.
**Falsifies:** A proof that anchor phases are constant for every positive angular profile under open:angular-flow; s4 is a counterexample.

## Extensions and improvements

The cheapest change with the largest effect on the chapter's standing is a proved null control. The null flow is explicit on the whole admitted class: on bits $\operatorname{atanh}x_i(t)=e^{-t}\operatorname{atanh}x_i(0)$, and on correlated laws the conditionals relax at rate two with the same exogenous bit forcing as the full flow (in the "remove peer sources" reading). In the scaled limit its rows at time $t$ are a Gaussian kernel of bandwidth $e^t$ on $u(0)$, so at the reference of open:routing the pair events are the solutions of $e^{-2b^2}/(1+e^{-2b^2}+\epsilon)=1/10$ with $b=2e^{-t}$ and the core-parent event solves $B(Ae^{-t},2e^{-t})=1/10$; s6 gives the margins at fixed $h$ (pair weight 0.45, first-pair contrast 0.35, $dB/dt=0.40$ at the parent event). open:persistence with $\Psi=\Phi=\Phi^0$ then gives an open set on which the null forms the same block hierarchy, and the open-law transfer carries over unchanged. Stated against (F5) read on block hierarchies, this turns "does not establish (F5)" into "(F5) fails on the admitted class", which is what the classification needs (D16-OPEN-01). If the authors prefer to keep (F5) on ordered partition sequences, the reference should be moved off the tie hypersurfaces (unequal initial half-gaps such as 2, 2.05, 2.1), so that both flows keep a fixed event order on a neighborhood; otherwise the literal clause is met on wedges (D16-OPEN-02). The same comparison settles the amplification grade of inh:recursive-formation, with an honest caveat. After $\tau$ the pair weights exceed $1/4$, so $c_p\ge1/2$ and $-\dot b/b\ge2-68e^{-8}$; hence at $a=4$ the full ratio $b/a$ is at most $\tfrac14e^{1.977\tau}(A/4)^{-0.974}$ times the frozen null ratio $2/A$ (below $5\times10^{-3}$ at $A=4096$), and at the core-parent event, with the ratio taken in the partition just before the event, the factor is of order $10^{-7}$ at $A=4096$ (a numerical estimate from the half-gaps of s2). At the first-stage events, and at every event if the ratio is taken in the partition just after it, the factor comes only from the earlier event time: at the first stage both flows cross at the same half-gap $\sqrt{\log9/2}$, so the factor is $e^{-(t_N-t_F)}\approx e^{-0.0575}=0.944$, and the same time shift governs the post-event ratio at the core-parent join, whose smallest between-block distance is to the far pair. Once the null comparison is proved, the construction is amplifying for a declared factor 0.95, which shows that the amplifying grade as defined can be met by a time shift alone; the definition should say in which partition the ratio is taken.

The open problem open:necessity-open is closer than the chapter suggests. s8 finds, in the limit field of the same architecture, a moderate-separation region (core half-gap ratio $r=b/a\approx0.48$ to $0.49$ with $b=2$, far pair at $R=60$) where the full flow forms two pairs and then joins them with an exterior present, while the null never forms the core pairs; the difference survives every one of six random perturbations of all six coordinates by up to 0.1. The mechanism is the one X2-FRAME-09 described: under the null all distances shrink at one rate, so a configuration whose within-pair and inner gaps are comparable never reaches the contrast $\delta$, and the rows flatten; peer contraction separates the scales. A theorem needs a validated enclosure of the twelve-dimensional exact field at a moderate temperature (h near $10^{-2}$ to $10^{-3}$), first on the reflection-invariant two-dimensional reduction, then on a box, plus the $e_0$ transfer to correlated laws. This would give a construction meeting (F1) to (F5) (weak form) and (F6a) on an open class with exact inheritance, with (F6b) and the strong (F5) still to be settled; the inheritance, interface and geometry parts of open:formation apply verbatim because they hold for every partition.

Constructive constants remain the third gap. Two pieces are already available from 2026-10-22 G1: the late comparison closes with $\varepsilon_b=1$ by a four-inequality invariant-region certificate, which makes $A=34$ admissible, and the early phase works from $A\ge34$. The temperature is controlled by the h-step. Comparing each cluster relative to its exact center removes the common-mode error $\tfrac23h^2u^3$, so only $hR$ small is needed, and replacing $e^{CL}$ by the exponential of the integrated logarithmic norm $\mu_\infty(DF)$ along the contracting reference tube should bring the Gronwall factor to order one. At $A=34$, $L\le3.57$ and $R\approx1.8\times10^3$, G1's estimate is a stated temperature of order $10^{-8}$ to $10^{-10}$, instead of the present unquantified value, which the written route places below $3\times10^{-26}$ (D16-OPEN-07).
