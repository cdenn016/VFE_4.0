### D06-PRED-01: The Fisher flow of the local free energy on the joint law is the tempered-posterior path, and exact conditioning is its limit
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** appendices/conditional_memory.tex:40-65 (cmem:free-energy; cmem:product-label: "Exact conditioning, this structured approximation, and the product-family Fisher flow are separately specified operations"); chapters/03_dynamics.tex:23-24 (rem:dyn-product), :421-433 (def:dyn-record-interrupted, eq:dyn-evidence-potential); chapters/04_prediction.tex:51-56
**Evidence:** The appendix separates exact conditioning from the product-family Fisher flow but never says what the Fisher flow of the local free energy does on the correlated joint family, which rem:dyn-product already defines without records. With the record, on a finite positive table, the flow of thm:dyn-finite-flow is d/dt zeta = -zeta (h - E_zeta h) with h = dF/dzeta = log(zeta/zeta^o) + const. For phi = log(zeta/zeta^o) this gives d/dt phi = -(phi - E_zeta phi), so phi_t = e^{-t} phi_0 + c(t) and

    zeta_t  is proportional to  zeta_0^{e^{-t}} (zeta^o)^{1 - e^{-t}}.

From the prior reference zeta_0 = rG this is zeta_t proportional to rG l(o|u)^{beta_t} with beta_t = 1 - e^{-t}, a tempered posterior. For the Gaussian record, N(xi; u, C)^beta is proportional to N(xi; u, C/beta) as a function of u, so zeta_t is exactly the cue/result posterior of cmem:gaussian-posterior at record covariance C/beta_t: weights proportional to pi_a N(xi; nu_a, L_a + C/beta_t), gains L_a(L_a + C/beta_t)^{-1}, response beta_t Cov_t(Y,X|xi) C^{-1}. On the one-parameter family zeta_beta proportional to zeta_0^{1-beta}(zeta^o)^beta, d/dbeta KL(zeta_beta||zeta^o) = (beta - 1) Var_beta(log(zeta^o/zeta_0)) and the Fisher metric is Var_beta, so the natural-gradient flow is the scalar ODE d beta/dt = 1 - beta. Script s7 (c): `solve_ivp` of the table flow against the closed form and against the tempered posterior, maximal error 6.0e-13 for t up to 3; N(xi;u,C)^beta / N(xi;u,C/beta) is constant in u to 1.3e-15. A flow restricted to the structured family of cmem:product-label can only reach a stationary point of that family; in s1, coordinate descent on it (monotone) converges to a point 0.142 above the exact minimum -log Z. That the Fisher-Rao flow of relative entropy is solvable in this way is known (for example Domingo-Enrich and Pooladian 2023; from memory, not rechecked).
**Fix:** Add after cmem:free-energy a proposition: on finite positive tables the joint-law Fisher flow of F(.;o) is zeta_t proportional to zeta_0^{e^{-t}}(zeta^o)^{1-e^{-t}}; KL(zeta_t||zeta^o) decreases to zero; the path from rG is the tempered posterior; for the Gaussian bank it is the posterior at record covariance C/(1-e^{-t}). Present it as the continuous-time counterpart of the assimilation map of def:dyn-record-interrupted for the evidence-augmented local term of eq:dyn-evidence-potential, with the product-family flow as its restriction. For continuous (A,X,Y) the closed form is an explicit curve that satisfies the equation pointwise; uniqueness needs a declared chart admitting quadratic log-density perturbations (AUTHORING regularity rule), or the claim can be restricted to the beta-family, where it is the scalar ODE above. Difficulty: low on tables, moderate for well-posedness on densities.
**Falsifies:** A finite positive table on which the Fisher flow of KL(.||zeta^o) leaves the geometric bridge (excluded by the explicit solution; s7 residual 6e-13).

### D06-PRED-02: A declared depth composition (the next cue is the previous result) has an exact finite filter for a fixed label and would discharge the closing requirement of the appendix
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** appendices/conditional_memory.tex:208-229 (cmem:sequential; closing paragraph: "A repeated conditional-prediction agent must therefore specify its accessible history, communication-generated record, retained joint law, and any projection")
**Evidence:** The only sequential model in scope draws a fresh cue at every step (cmem:history), which makes the label filter exact by construction; the manuscript says so. The composition that motivates the appendix, in which the result of one step is the cue of the next, is an equally explicit normalized model: A_l Markov with T, U_0 ~ mu_{A_0}, V_l ~ K_{A_l}(U_l, .), U_{l+1} = V_l, O_l | U_l ~ N(U_l, C_l). For the Gaussian bank and T = I it is a family of M linear-Gaussian state-space models, and the exact posterior of (A, U_l) given O_{0:l} is M Kalman filters plus label weights proportional to pi_a times the product of innovation likelihoods. Script s7 (e), scalar, M = 2, four records: the per-label Kalman evidence equals the stacked joint-Gaussian evidence to 1.8e-15; the exact label posterior is (0.125, 0.875), while the fresh-emission filter cmem:filter gives (0.275, 0.725). Script s5 (iii): with identical cue laws the label filter is w_0 = (1/2, 1/2) after o_0 = -1 and after o_0 = +1, but the exact next-record means are -0.375 and +0.375 while the fresh-emission model predicts 0 in both cases, so label-only carry is not sufficient once the next cue reads the result. For T different from I the exact posterior is a mixture over up to M^{l+1} label histories (switching linear dynamical systems; Ackerson and Fu 1970, Ghahramani and Hinton 2000, from memory), and the classical collapse steps (GPB, IMM) are moment projections of the kind analyzed in cmem:projection, applied conditionally on the current label.
**Fix:** Add this model after cmem:sequential with (i) normalization and the exact per-label Kalman filter for T = I; (ii) the label-history mixture for general T; (iii) per-label moment projection as assumed-density filtering, with cmem:projection-loss applied conditionally on the current label (a retained state of w_l and label-conditional Gaussians keeps the label/content dependence and loses only the within-label non-Gaussian part, whereas w_l times q_G also loses I(A;U)); (iv) the two failure examples above as the exact reason a fresh-emission filter cannot be reused. Difficulty: low; the ingredients are classical.
**Falsifies:** A fresh-emission reading of the composed model that reproduces its exact label posterior (excluded by s7 (e)).

### D06-PRED-03: A learning rule for the bank follows from the exact identity only once results are recorded; cue records cannot identify the result mechanism

No record-driven rule can learn the result mechanism in the declared model. For the parameters that the records do reach, Fisher's identity gives grad_theta(-log Z_theta(o)) = -E_{zeta^o_theta}[grad_theta log P_theta(m,k,o)] under differentiation through the integral, which is the envelope of cmem:free-energy-split at the posterior; for the Gaussian bank and independent episodes this is EM for a Gaussian mixture with noisy inputs, with a closed-form M-step for pi, nu and L. **Fix:** State the non-identifiability as a one-line corollary of cmem:posterior. If a learning criterion is wanted, add the result-record construction and its EM or Fisher-identity rule as a declared extension, keeping the statement of AUTHORING.md that the present construction supplies no learning rule. Difficulty: low.

### D06-PRED-04: The hypotheses of cmem:free-energy are unnecessary for its identity, and in the separated form used by cmem:gibbs they can exclude the minimizer
**Category:** gap
**Severity:** low
**Location:** appendices/conditional_memory.tex:40-58 (cmem:free-energy, cmem:free-energy-split, cmem:gibbs); chapters/04_prediction.tex:51
**Evidence:** The proposition assumes "the absolute continuity, finite relative entropies, and integrable log likelihood needed below". Under the manuscript's definition eq:agent-free-energy, F(Q;o) = KL(Q||zeta^o) - log Z(o), and the chain rule for relative entropy on standard Borel spaces gives cmem:free-energy-split for every candidate Q = s(dm) q_m(dk), as an identity in (-infinity, +infinity], with no hypothesis beyond 0 < Z(o) < infinity (then Z_m(o) is finite for r-almost every m, and the set where Z_m(o) = 0 has w^o-measure zero). The hypotheses matter only for the energy-entropy form KL(s||r) + int [KL(q_m||G_m) - E_{q_m} log l] s(dm), which cmem:gibbs uses through f_a, and that form can fail at the posterior. Take one presentation, U ~ Uniform(0,1), and O | U = u ~ N(0, sigma(u)^2) with sigma(u) = u log(u)^2 / sqrt(2 pi) on (0, 1/e] and constant on (1/e, 1). At o = 0, l(0|u) = 1/(u log(u)^2) near zero and Z(0) = e, but int l log l du = +infinity: with s = -log u the truncated tail integral is log S - 2 int_1^S log(s)/s^2 ds (s8: 0.96, 4.92, 11.82, 25.63 at S = 10, 1e3, 1e6, 1e12, while the tail mass converges to 1). Hence KL(Pi^0||mu) = +infinity, the separated expression of F at the posterior and the cost f_a(Pi_a) are infinity minus infinity, the sentence "At the exact conditionals, f_a = -log Z_a(o)" is undefined, and the stated hypothesis "integrable log likelihood" removes the posterior from the class over which the minimum is asserted, whereas eq:agent-free-energy gives F(Pi^0; 0) = -1. On finite tables everything holds: s1 gives residual 1.8e-15 for cmem:free-energy-split over 200 random correlated Q, 2.7e-15 for the separated form, smallest gap F + log Z = 1.15 over random Q and 0 at the posterior, Gibbs weights minimal at fixed q_a (smallest excess over 500 random s: 7.5e-4) and equal to w at the exact conditionals (difference 0.0).
**Fix:** State cmem:free-energy for every candidate law under eq:agent-free-energy, in (-infinity, +infinity]. In cmem:gibbs define f_a := KL(q_a||Pi_a^o) - log Z_a(o), always in (-infinity, +infinity], and note that it equals the separated expression whenever KL(q_a||G_a) and E_{q_a}|log l| are finite.
**Falsifies:** A reading in which the free energy is defined only by the separated expression; then the proposition needs the hypothesis KL(zeta^o||rG) < infinity, which the example violates.

### D06-PRED-05: The sufficiency equivalence after thm:predictive-chain needs KL(s||t) finite, but it follows the remark that removes finiteness

**Severity:** low
**Location:** chapters/04_prediction.tex:35-37 (remarks after thm:predictive-chain)
**Evidence:** Line 35 says the identity holds in [0, infinity] without finiteness; line 37 then says "KL(sK||tK) = KL(s||t) exactly when the Bayesian inverse of K computed at t returns s from sK". With KL(s||t) finite, as in the theorem, this is correct: the conditional term vanishes exactly when s^o = t^o for sK-almost every o, which gives recovery; conversely, recovery means ds/dt = K(d(sK)/d(tK)) under P_t, and Jensen's inequality gives KL(s||t) <= KL(sK||tK) (s2: sufficient channel, conditional term 1.7e-17 and recovery error 1.4e-17; non-sufficient channel, 1.25 and 0.59). ## Extensions and improvements

The most valuable step is D06-PRED-01, because it gives the appendix a precise relation to the dynamics chapter at no conceptual cost. On the correlated joint family the Fisher flow of the local free energy, which is the local part of the evidence-augmented potential eq:dyn-evidence-potential, has the closed-form solution zeta_t proportional to zeta_0^{e^{-t}} (zeta^o)^{1-e^{-t}}; started at the reference joint rG it passes through the tempered posteriors rG l^{1-e^{-t}} and reaches the exact posterior, and for the Gaussian bank every intermediate law is the cue/result posterior at record covariance C/(1 - e^{-t}). Exact conditioning is then the infinite-time limit of the joint-law flow, while a flow restricted to the structured family of cmem:product-label can reach only a stationary point of that family; the difference between the two limits is the structured gap that s1 measures (0.142 nats at the coordinate-descent fixed point of that instance). This also supplies a continuous-time replacement for the assimilation maps of def:dyn-record-interrupted with an explicit rate, and within one agent it shows which flow targets the posterior (the joint-law flow of the local free energy) and which targets a restricted optimum (the structured or product flow). The next theorem is the per-agent version: with peers frozen, the joint-law Fisher flow of the per-agent free energy of prop:dyn-per-agent follows the geometric bridge to the per-agent posterior; with moving peers the flow is nonautonomous, and its limit behavior is open. On finite tables both statements are elementary; on densities they need a declared chart (the regularity rule of AUTHORING), and the one-parameter bridge family, on which the flow is d beta/dt = 1 - beta, gives a rigorous version immediately.

The second step, D06-PRED-02, replaces the fresh-emission filter by the composition that the appendix itself says must be declared. With U_{l+1} = V_l, a fixed label and the Gaussian bank, the model is M linear-Gaussian state-space models and its exact filter is M Kalman filters with label weights; with label switching it is a switching linear dynamical system whose exact posterior grows as M^{l+1} and whose standard approximations (GPB, IMM) are the per-label moment projections of cmem:projection. The cost of the projection through depth can then be computed exactly with the chapter-5 identity inh:causal-memory: take M_t to be the projected filter state, and the path divergence between the true record process and the process predicted by the projected filter splits into the information about the next record that the projected state discards plus the prediction error with the information it keeps. That is a theorem about the declared composition, with the identity already in the manuscript, and it states exactly which interface a retained carrier preserves.

The third step, D06-PRED-03, concerns learning. The result kernel is not identifiable from cue records at all, because the evidence does not contain it, so any local learning rule needs records that read results: a supervised result record, or in the interacting protocol the messages, which are samples of a speaker's result prediction. Once such records exist, the exact identity cmem:free-energy-split gives the EM or Fisher-identity rule, and for the Gaussian bank the M-step is in closed form. This would supply, for one agent, the learning criterion that chapter 3 lists among the things the reciprocal potential gives up, without asserting anything about the reciprocal potential itself.

The improvements D06-PRED-10 to D06-PRED-15 are one-line or one-paragraph changes. The readout counterexample becomes an equivalence by Fourier inversion and identifiability of finite Gaussian mixtures; the inclusive/exclusive contrast becomes exact on the declared product carrier, where the free-energy carrier is the precision-weighted Gaussian and is never wider than the moment carrier, and the scalar example is non-stationary for every a > 1.3236 by a two-line argument; the positive Fisher tensor bounds the magnitude of the signed response (D I^{-1} D^T <= Cov(Y|xi)), which makes the distinction between the two tensors quantitative; heterogeneous record covariances become a literal species once the covariance is part of the record with an ancillary law; the fresh-emission label filter is sufficient for the whole future, not only the next label; and the Gaussian predictive criterion has the canonical-correlation optimum, which is the one derived (not declared) selective channel available in chapter 4.
