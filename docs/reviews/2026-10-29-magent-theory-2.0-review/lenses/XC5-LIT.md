**Severity:** medium

Surveys 14:155-204, 2017). Sending a fresh sample from the speaker's posterior predictive is the iterated-learning protocol of Griffiths and Kalish (Cogn. Sci. 31:441-480, 2007), whose transmission chain converges to the prior. **Fix:** At :6 add: "With posterior announcements in place of samples this is the dialogue of Geanakoplos and Polemarchakis (1982), whose limit is Aumann's agreement (1976); Gamma_t = W lambda_t is the public-belief decomposition of social learning (Smith and Sorensen 2000; Mossel and Tamuz 2017); transmission of a posterior-predictive sample is the iterated-learning protocol (Griffiths and Kalish 2007)." ### XC5-LIT-05: The limit in it1:learning is the classical posterior of a partially identified model

**Fix:** After it1:learning (ii) add: "This is the limiting posterior of a partially identified model: the identified coordinates concentrate and the nonidentified ones keep their conditional prior (Poirier 1998; Gustafson 2010). The loop prior acts only through that conditional prior, which (iii)-(v) compute."
**Falsifies:** Dependence of the limit in (ii) on the data beyond the identified coordinates. Part (i) excludes it.

### XC5-LIT-06: The induced loop action of a link-dependent content precision has a closed form and a definite sign
**Category:** extension
**Severity:** medium
**Location:** chapters/19_curvature_hierarchy.tex:259-262 (it3:open-fluctuation), :5, :62-124 (it3:induced)
**Evidence:** it3:open-fluctuation asks for "the signs of the resulting loop terms" in k(w) - 1/2 b'J^{-1}b + 1/2 log det J(w). In a declared group-valued record model whose records read theta_e^{-1} y_i - y_j through unit codes, the content precision is P plus a connection Laplacian on the multigraph of directed links, and each directed two-cycle is a cycle of that multigraph with holonomy theta_ij theta_ji. For U(1) or SO(2) links Forman's theorem expands det J over cycle-rooted spanning forests, with on-site precisions rooting the trees and cycle weights 2 - 2cos(hol) >= 0 (squared for the real realification). On one loop of length n with self precision p, det(pI + Delta_theta) = 2cosh(na) - 2cos(theta) with 2cosh(a) = 2 + p (Block C of `induced_loop_action_check.py`, relative residual 2.6e-15). So 1/2 log det J is minimized at flat holonomy and its loop dependence decays like exp(-na) with loop length: the content fluctuations induce a Wilson-type loop penalty of definite sign, while the record term -1/2 b'J^{-1}b can still favor nonzero classes, as 19:122-124 observes for the quadratic part.
**Fix:** Sketch: for T_e = I and orthogonal links compute S(theta) = 1/2 log det(P + Delta_theta) - 1/2 b'J^{-1}b exactly by Forman's expansion; expand to second order in the cycle holonomies and compare the coefficient with the anchored loop prior of eq. it1:loop-prior; for rank-two bundles use Kenyon 2011. Codes of rank below K turn Delta_theta into a sheaf Laplacian, for which a forest expansion must be derived. Difficulty: low for U(1) or SO(2) on small graphs, medium for rank two, high for GL(K) with low-rank codes.
**Falsifies:** A declared nonlinear record model whose content precision is link independent; then the determinant is constant, as in the tangent model at 19:99.

## Extensions and improvements

The most useful single addition for chapter 19 is the determinant calculation of XC5-LIT-06. In a declared group-valued record model with unit codes, the content precision is a connection Laplacian on the multigraph of directed links plus the self precision, so the dictionary action obtained by integrating out contents is 1/2 log det(P + Delta_theta) - 1/2 b'J^{-1}b. Forman's expansion writes the first term as a sum over cycle-rooted spanning forests in which each cycle, including each directed two-cycle theta_ij theta_ji, carries the factor 2 - 2cos(hol) for U(1) or SO(2) links; on one loop the closed form 2cosh(na) - 2cos(theta) (checked to 2.6e-15) shows that the induced loop penalty is minimized at flat holonomy and decays exponentially in loop length at rate a = arccosh(1 + p/2). That gives it3:open-fluctuation a definite sign for the fluctuation part and a quantitative competitor to the anchored loop prior of chapter 17, whose second-order expansion it can be matched against. Codes of rank below K replace the connection Laplacian by a sheaf Laplacian, and a forest expansion for that case would be new.

The tangent loop prior of eq. it1:loop-prior is a Gaussian lattice gauge action in linearized holonomies, made normalizable by an anchor that is not gauge invariant. A gauge-invariant alternative is available: keep the loop term, replace the anchor by a gauge-fixing condition on the pure-gauge space ker B (for example a lattice Lorenz condition, sum over incident edges of omega_e = 0 at each vertex), and compute the normalizer on the gauge-fixed slice. Comparing that prior with the anchored one would separate what in it1:learning (iii)-(v) comes from the loop term and what comes from the anchor, which matters because the anchor, not the loop term, fixes the invisible flat directions N intersect ker B.

For the interacting-memory appendix the natural next theorem is an agreement statement. Under posterior-sample messages, does the common posterior lambda_t converge almost surely, and do the agents' posteriors of a common target converge to equal values, as in Geanakoplos and Polemarchakis? The martingale convergence of lambda_t is immediate on finite spaces; agreement of the limits requires that each speaker's sampling law become common knowledge, which holds when the posterior-predictive sampling kernel separates private posteriors. The binary example of eq. imem:binary-exchange can test this in a few lines of exact arithmetic. A positive answer would connect the appendix to Aumann's theorem; a counterexample with persistent disagreement would show what randomized messages cost relative to posterior announcements.

Chapter 15's finite and continuum constructions can be joined through the orbit distance (XC5-LIT-15). Once C_* is written as a graph Dirichlet energy into the orbit space, graph refinement becomes discrete-to-continuum convergence of Dirichlet energies into a Riemannian quotient, and the parabolic system of ctx:c:thm:local becomes the candidate limit of the finite flows of ctx:f:thm:ode. Learned codes (XC5-LIT-16) turn the species-language question into information design, where the receiver-side identifiability results of chapter 17 are already the receiver's half of the game.
