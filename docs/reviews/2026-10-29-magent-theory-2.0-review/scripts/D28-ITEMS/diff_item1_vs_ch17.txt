- \section*{Related work} The ingredients appear separately in the literature; their combination was not found in a standard search, which is not exhaustive.
- Opinion dynamics on discourse sheaves \citep{HansenGhrist2021} gives agents private opinion spaces and linear expression maps into shared discourse spaces, with dynamics that can also evolve the expression maps; opinions are vectors, and there is no receiver reconstruction, gauge group or holonomy.
- A cognitive-geometric model of interpretation \citep{Amornbunchornvej2025} uses linear interpretation maps between agents' value spaces, with beliefs lost in their null spaces and composite maps along chains.
- Communication without a shared reference frame treats unknown frame changes as gauge data \citep{BartlettRudolphSpekkens2007,vanEnk2006}.
- The meeting of minds in conceptual spaces models meaning as maps between individual spaces with agreement as a fixed point \citep{WarglienGardenfors2013}.
- The relativistic theory of consciousness \citep{LahavNeemeh2022} holds that an observer reading another system's representation measures only its physical coordinates and constructs a new representation of its own; the code-then-reconstruction chain \cref{it1:chain} is a formal version of that measurement, and the identity dictionary between equivalent systems is the transformation it uses for that case.
- Communication as active inference with a shared generative model is developed by \citet{FristonFrith2015} and \citet{FristonEtAl2020Language}, and language as efficient compression by \citet{ZaslavskyEtAl2018}, in the tradition of \citet{Shannon1948}, the information bottleneck \citep{Tishby1999} and coding with side information \citep{WynerZiv1976}.
