### S4-C01: Formation under the collective free energy with a latent structure variable (extension, critical priority)
**Sources:** XC6-PROG-01 (extension, critical).
**Location:** `chapters/03_dynamics.tex:236-270` (sec:dyn-likelihood, prop:dyn-global-local); `chapters/01_foundations.tex:199-226` (def:content-law, prop:content-law); `chapters/05_inheritance.tex:1396-1399` (inh:formation-open); PIFB2 `05_section.tex:6-13`, `10_section.tex:18-36`; earlier theory `SPEC.md:72-75`, `CONTENT.md:824-902`.
**Finding and evidence:** No selection, formation or parent-dynamics result in 2.0 is proved under the global free energy; the only parent object under a global joint is the supplied rigid-cluster restriction of chapter 19, which AUTHORING calls "supplied-cluster reductions, not selection" (`AUTHORING.md:91`). The lens notes that the global picture is the only one of the three objectives with an evidence, one objective fixed across records and an exact meaning for top-down influence (the PIFB2 cavity prior uses each child's record once). Sketch: extend the model of prop:dyn-global-local by a structural latent Pi (a partition of the agents, or a nested pair) with a proper prior on a finite catalog, block latents z_B ~ r_B, children y_i | z_B for i in B, and the comparison records of eq:sample-comparison. The posterior of Pi is a derived selector; under recognition Q(Pi) x Q(z) x Q(y) the structure factor is Q(Pi) proportional to pi(Pi) exp(-F_Pi) with F_Pi the block free energy, of which the "structure free energy" of `Item_7_formation_feedback.tex:52-54` is a special case. For linear-Gaussian children with conjugate block latents the block posterior depends on the children only through summed natural statistics, which is a Gaussian-family feature and must be stated as one. Deleting the comparison records leaves Q(Pi) at its prior, so selection is caused by the records (weak (F5)); the second-level partition posterior reads first-level block statistics, so a counterfactual first-level handoff changes it (counterfactual dependence in the sense of the earlier R14). Obstacles named by the lens: (F2) becomes posterior concentration and needs an evidence horizon clause (earlier R15); exact inheritance fails beyond conjugate linear-Gaussian families (Item 7 reports that closure of the pooled parent for every configuration forces a Gaussian location family), so general laws need an augmented parent state or a graded defect (S4-M13); a factorized label recognition discards label-state dependence (PIFB2 `05_section.tex:122`). Prior art to cite and separate from: Heller and Ghahramani 2005, the nested Chinese restaurant process (Blei, Griffiths and Jordan 2010), partitioned variational inference (Ashman et al. 2022). Difficulty: medium for a static Gaussian theorem (exact posterior on a finite catalog, cavity feedback, exact block inheritance, counterfactual dependence of the second level); high for a dynamical version meeting (F1) to (F6) on a non-Gaussian family.
**Proposed fix:** pose the construction as the first case of inh:formation-open under the global free energy, with the derived selector, the cavity feedback and a graded persistence clause stated in the theorem.
**Falsified by:** a proof that in this model the second-level partition posterior is the same for every first-level handoff state on an open set of record configurations, or that the parent recognition factor fails to close even for conjugate linear-Gaussian children.
**Related:** S4-H09 (choice of primary objective), S4-H10 (VFE_4.0 implements only the global free energy), S4-H12 (type closure).
