A result is credited to a lens when its label, or an equation label inside its statement or proof block, occurs in that lens's material; 570 of the 917 non-heading labels were mapped to their enclosing result this way (an equation label is mapped only when no section break or later heading intervenes). A label shared by a chapter and a working document is credited to the working-document copy only for lenses that read that working document at that line.

Two limits matter for reading the table. First, the method code of a checked row (R for reconstruction, X for recomputation, D for reading or comparison) is assigned from the wording of the row, so a lens that reconstructed a proof without saying so is undercounted, and a row whose text says "not a proof" was overcounted once (`pa:nonflat-window`, corrected by hand in the table). For every theorem-grade result below R I read the lens rows themselves; section (b) records what was actually done. Second, "examined" means only that a lens recorded something; listing a label in a dispatch return is the weakest form, and several cross-cutting lenses (XC3-SCOPE, XC4-CONS, XC5-LIT) examined statements for their own aspect without checking proofs. ### GAP01: Small-parameter persistence proofs of the per-agent grouping theorems and the nonflat windows

Scope: `chapters/13_per_agent_inheritance.tex:60-398` (`pa:four-transfer` 60, `pa:four-grouping` 96, `pa:persistent-grouping` 138, `pa:angular-flow` 214, `pa:angular-grouping` 241 with `pa:null-box`, `pa:null-bound`, `pa:null-sign-bound` at 261-274, `pa:nonflat-window` 352); the imports in `chapters/14_collective_local_parent.tex:66-90` and the window `lp:nonflat` at :176; `companion/c13_per_agent_inheritance.tex:122-258`. Cluster S3. Suggested agent: research-dynamics.

Reason: these are the program's per-agent selection theorems with persistent grouping, and `pa:angular-grouping`, `pa:phase-parent` and `pa:nonflat-window` are cited by chapter 14 and by the outlook. Every statement is asymptotic in a small parameter, and no lens reconstructed the small-parameter argument: D17-PA ran floating-point histories and says it did not estimate epsilon_0 or the entrance time; XC3-SCOPE, XC6-PROG and XC7-IMPL reconstructed only the limit field (M = -(7/5)I + (2/5)11^T, u_* = (15/7, 15/7, 5/7)) and the null-box bounds; D17-PA's row for `pa:nonflat-window` calls itself numerical support, not a proof; D18-LP, XC1-GAUGE, XC2-FRAME and XC7-IMPL list the positive-epsilon windows as not checked. The flow these theorems are about also lacks a uniqueness proof on {-1,1} x S^1 x S^1 (D17-PA-03).

Focus questions:
