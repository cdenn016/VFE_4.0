## 2. Cluster verdict

No lens in the cluster found a critical defect, and no main theorem of chapters 5 to 12 was shown false: the inheritance, closure, fusion, erasure, phase, boundary, Wishart and routing results hold wherever the eleven lenses reconstructed or recomputed them, and the three gap lenses supplied proofs for results and review claims that had been checked only numerically or in outline (`form:parent` through a Bregman identity, the uniqueness clause of `form:full-flow` for an explicitly defined solution class, the decay rates of `form:geometry-decay`, the late-phase constant eps_b of `open:routing` by a validated interval certificate, an explicit radius for `fus:open-two-stage`, the asymptotic stability of the chapter 9 reference at abs(J) = 1, and the d = 1 minimality Jacobian of chapter 10). The high-severity problems of the cluster sit in the formation standard of chapter 5 and in the interaction-necessity verdicts built on it. Six lenses report that the null flow of `inh:null-flow` either is underdefined (no declared row convention, no global-free-energy form, no definition beyond finite tables, no post-handoff dynamics, no horizon) or measures something other than interaction (it is passed by agents that do not interact once a constant-output channel is deleted) (S2-H01 to S2-H03 and S2-M03, from D09-INH, D10-FORM, D15-WISH, GAP03-S2, D16-OPEN and D11-FUS), and D15-WISH and GAP03-S2 report that under the row convention declared in chapter 13, companion c13 and Item 7 the sentence of `form:kappa-independence` (iii) that the null reproduces the membership history fails at zero coupling; this is the only stated result in the cluster that a lens reports false, the other reported errors being in remarks, headings, proof steps, a hypothesis sentence and the companion. D10-FORM reports that the child-level probe of (F4), the counterfactual class of (F6b) and the topology of (F1) are left to the construction, that chapters 9, 11 and 12 apply (F4) under incompatible readings, and that an anchored linear consensus meets every clause under the readings chapters 11 and 12 use. Chapter by chapter the (F5) verdicts are reported as formal or undecided: in chapter 6 the pass rests on an exact-equality selector while the membership sequence does not depend on the coupling (D11-FUS); in chapter 8 the status sentence contradicts the current (F5) and the selector is not a partition (D13-ERAPUB, D10-FORM); in chapters 9 and 10 the exact retentions exist without the interaction (D13-ERAPUB, D14-BND); in chapter 11 the verdict depends on the undeclared row convention (D15-WISH, GAP03-S2); in chapter 12 no failing clause is proved, the literal clause is met on thin wedges by event reordering, and the abstract states an unproved null reproduction as fact (D16-OPEN). The no-go remarks that interaction-sensitive selection requires receiver-dependent targets and that scalar additive closure excludes interaction necessity are reported stated beyond their hypotheses by three lenses (D09-INH, D10-FORM, D15-WISH). The remaining findings are local: false descriptive sentences in remarks and the companion, cross-chapter inconsistencies, missing hypotheses in auxiliary claims, citation locators and notation. Read together, the reports leave no construction in chapters 6 to 12 for which a lens records a proved selection that depends on the interaction under a well-posed standard; the concrete leads they record are numerical (D16-OPEN-10 in the chapter 12 architecture at moderate separation, D11-FUS-07 off the common-offset fusion family).
